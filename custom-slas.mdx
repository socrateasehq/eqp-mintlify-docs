<Tip>
Equip signs custom paperwork (MSAs, SLAs, DPAs, vendor forms) only for engagements with an **annual contract value (ACV) of $25,000 or more**. Below that threshold, all customers use Equip under the standard public terms.
</Tip>

Equip is a product-led, fully **self-serve** platform. Every customer is covered by the standard [Terms of Service](https://equip.co/terms-service/), [Privacy Policy](https://equip.co/privacy-policy/), and public [Pricing](https://equip.co/pricing/).

Billing is **credit-based and pay-as-you-go**. There is no subscription, and you purchase and manage credits directly in the product. Please evaluate Equip using these publicly available documents.

## What Can Be Customized

Whether Equip can accommodate custom paperwork depends entirely on your projected **annual contract value**:

| Request | ACV under $25,000 | ACV $25,000 or more |
|---|---|---|
| Custom MSA, SLA, or DPA | Not signed | Considered, subject to review |
| Redlines to legal terms (governing law, indemnity, confidentiality) | Not modified | Limited redlines considered |
| Vendor onboarding forms | Not completed | Considered, subject to review |
| Bespoke security questionnaires | Not completed | Considered, subject to review |

<Note>
Custom paperwork for larger engagements is **considered, not guaranteed**. Each request is reviewed individually, so share your requirements early.
</Note>

## How to Request One

If your projected ACV meets the $25,000 threshold, share your requirements with the Equip team:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Estimate your annual contract value">
    Work out your expected yearly spend on Equip credits. Custom paperwork is only reviewed when the projected ACV is $25,000 or more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gather your requirements">
    Collect the documents you need reviewed, such as your MSA, SLA, DPA, or vendor forms, along with any specific redlines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact the Equip team">
    Reach out via **live chat** in the product or email **hello@equip.co**. Include your projected ACV, your requirements, and your timeline so the team can review and respond.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If your ACV is below the threshold, you can still start immediately. Equip is self-serve, so you can create an account, run a pilot with the **10 free trial credits** every new team receives, and scale up under the standard terms without any paperwork.
</Tip>

## FAQs

**Do you sign our MSA, SLA, or DPA for a pilot or small purchase?**

No, not for ACV under $25,000. Please proceed under the standard [Terms of Service](https://equip.co/terms-service/) and [Privacy Policy](https://equip.co/privacy-policy/).

**Can you complete our security questionnaire?**

For ACV under $25,000, Equip does not complete bespoke questionnaires. Use the public documents for your evaluation.

**Can you change governing law, indemnity, or confidentiality clauses?**

No changes are made for ACV under $25,000. These clauses are standardized in the [Terms of Service](https://equip.co/terms-service/).

**Where can I see what is included and how pricing works?**

See the public [Pricing](https://equip.co/pricing/) page. Equip is credit-based and pay-as-you-go, with no subscriptions or seat fees.

<Note>
Support is available to every customer, regardless of contract size, via live chat and email at hello@equip.co.
</Note>
