<Tip>
Equip signs custom paperwork (MSAs, SLAs, DPAs, vendor forms) only for engagements with an **annual contract value (ACV) of \$25,000 or more**. Below that threshold, all customers use Equip under the standard public terms.
</Tip>

Equip is **self-serve**. Every customer is covered by the standard [Terms of Service](https://equip.co/terms-service/), [Privacy Policy](https://equip.co/privacy-policy/), and [Pricing](https://equip.co/pricing/) page. Please evaluate Equip using these public documents.

## What Can Be Customized

Whether Equip can accommodate custom paperwork depends entirely on your projected **annual contract value**:

| Request | ACV under \$25,000 | ACV \$25,000 or more |
|---|---|---|
| Custom MSA, SLA, or DPA, vendor onboarding forms, bespoke security questionnaires | Not supported | Considered, subject to review |
| Redlines to legal terms (governing law, indemnity, confidentiality) | Not supported | Limited redlines considered |

<Note>
Custom paperwork for larger engagements is **considered, not guaranteed**. Each request is reviewed individually.
</Note>

## How to Request One

If your projected ACV meets the \$25,000 threshold, share your requirements with the Equip team:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Estimate your annual contract value">
    Work out your expected yearly spend on Equip credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact the Equip team">
    Reach out via live chat in the product or email **hello@equip.co**. Include your projected ACV, the documents you need reviewed (MSA, SLA, DPA, or vendor forms), any specific redlines, and your timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Below the threshold, you can still start immediately: create an account, run a pilot with the **10 free trial credits** every new team receives, and scale up under the standard terms.
</Tip>

## FAQs

**Do you sign our MSA, SLA, or DPA for a pilot or small purchase?**

No, not for ACV under \$25,000. Please proceed under the standard [Terms of Service](https://equip.co/terms-service/) and [Privacy Policy](https://equip.co/privacy-policy/).

**Can you complete our security questionnaire?**

For ACV under \$25,000, Equip does not complete bespoke questionnaires. Use the public documents for your evaluation.

**Can you change governing law, indemnity, or confidentiality clauses?**

No changes are made for ACV under \$25,000. These clauses are standardized in the [Terms of Service](https://equip.co/terms-service/).

**Where can I see what is included and how pricing works?**

See the [Pricing](https://equip.co/pricing/) page.

<Note>
Support is available to every customer, regardless of contract size, via live chat and email at hello@equip.co.
</Note>
