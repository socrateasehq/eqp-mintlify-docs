Every Equip team is on a subscription plan (Standard, Premium, or Elite) with its own credit allowance and recruiter-seat limit, and shares a single **credit balance**. New teams start with **10 free credits at signup**, no card required.

## Subscribing to a Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Billing page">
    Go to [**Account > Billing**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/billing/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Click **Choose a Plan** to open the [Pricing page](https://equip.co/pricing/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a plan and subscribe">
    Pick a plan and billing cycle, then click **Subscribe**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: Billing page showing the Subscription card with Choose a Plan button */}

## Credit Pricing

One credit costs **\$1**, and different actions use different amounts:

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| **Assessment** | 1 credit per candidate per test |
| **One-way AI Interview** (up to 20 questions) | 1 credit per candidate |
| **One-way AI Interview** (21 to 40 questions) | 2 credits per candidate |
| **Conversational AI Interview (Quick)** | 2 credits per candidate |
| **Conversational AI Interview (Comprehensive)** | 3 credits per candidate |
| **Job Fit Score re-ranking** | 0.5 credits per candidate |
| **Bulk CV import** | 0.01 credits per CV |

The **ATS**, including job posts, the candidate pipeline, and your careers page, is free. Credits never expire while your subscription is active.

<Note>
See [Credit Deduction Policy](/credit-deduction-policy) for exactly when each credit is deducted.
</Note>

## Volume Discounts

Larger top-ups get an automatic discount when you pay, with no coupon code to enter.

| Credits in one top-up | Discount |
|---|---|
| 500 or more | 5% |
| 1,000 or more | 10% |
| 2,000 or more | 15% |

## Topping Up Credits

Any team member can top up credits. Once subscribed, a **Top-up Credits** button appears next to **Choose a Plan** on the [**Billing** page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/billing/).

<Warning>
Topping up requires an **active subscription**. On the free trial, **Buy More** asks you to choose a plan first.
</Warning>

{/* screenshot: Billing page showing the Buy More button, credit input, discount, and net cost */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Buy More">
    Click **Buy More** next to your credit balance in the sidebar.
    {/* screenshot: Buy More button in the recruiter dashboard sidebar */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the number of credits">
    The minimum purchase is **10 credits** and the self-serve maximum is **5,000 credits** per purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the cost and pay">
    Teams in India pay through Razorpay, can add a **GSTIN** to the invoice, and have 18% GST added. All other teams pay through Stripe Checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The invoice is emailed to the **Team Admin**. See [Invoices](/invoices) for billing history.

<Note>
Every deduction and purchase is recorded in the [**Credit Usage**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/credit-usage/) ledger, which you can export as a CSV.
</Note>

## Low and Negative Balances

Equip lets your balance go slightly negative so a hiring round is not interrupted, but there are limits:

| Balance | What happens |
|---|---|
| Below 0 | Your team cannot see any candidate results |
| Below -25 | Candidates can no longer start your assessments or AI interviews |

This means the most candidates you can invite at any point is your current credit balance plus 25. Candidates who already started an assessment or AI interview can still finish it.

<Warning>
Below negative 25, invited candidates see a **Not Available** message instead of the assessment or AI interview. Top up before large invitation batches.
</Warning>

Equip emails the Team Admin a low-balance alert when the balance drops past **20 credits** and again past **5 credits**.
