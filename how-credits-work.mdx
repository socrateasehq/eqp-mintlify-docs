<Tip>
Equip is pay-per-use: 1 credit = $1 (or ₹90), the ATS is free, and credits never expire. Buy credits from the **Purchase Credits** page; volume and first-purchase discounts are applied automatically at checkout.
</Tip>

Every Equip team has a single shared **credit balance**. All team members see and spend from the same balance, and new teams start with **10 free trial credits** so you can assess real candidates before paying anything.

There are no subscriptions and no per-seat fees. You only spend credits when candidates actually use the product.

## Credit Pricing

One credit costs **$1** (USD) or **₹90** (INR). Your team's country determines the currency: teams in India are billed in Rupees, everyone else in US Dollars.

Different actions consume different amounts of credits:

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| **Assessment** | 1 credit per candidate per test |
| **One-way AI Interview** | 1 credit per candidate |
| **Conversational AI Interview (Quick)** | 2 credits per candidate |
| **Conversational AI Interview (Comprehensive)** | 3 credits per candidate |
| **Job Fit Score re-ranking** | 0.5 credits per candidate |
| **Bulk CV import** | 0.01 credits per CV |

The **ATS** itself, including job posts, the candidate pipeline, and your careers page, is completely free. Credits never expire, so anything you buy now stays available until you use it.

<Note>
Exactly when a credit is deducted matters: for assessments, the first credit is deducted at invitation and later credits when the candidate starts each test. See [Credit Deduction Policy](/credit-deduction-policy) for the full rules and a worked example.
</Note>

## Volume Discounts

Larger purchases earn automatic discounts. There are no coupon codes to enter; the discount is applied on the Purchase Credits page before you pay.

| Credits in one purchase | Discount |
|---|---|
| 500 or more | 5% |
| 1,000 or more | 10% |
| 2,000 or more | 15% |

Only one discount applies per purchase. If your team is also eligible for the [first-purchase discount](/first-purchase-discount), Equip applies whichever discount is worth more money, and the page shows you which one was used.

## Purchasing Credits

Any team member can buy credits from the **Purchase Credits** page in the dashboard.

<!-- screenshot: Purchase Credits page showing the credit input, discount, and net cost -->

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Purchase Credits page">
    Click the **Buy** button in your dashboard to open the Purchase Credits page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the number of credits">
    Type how many credits you want. The minimum purchase is **10 credits** and the self-serve maximum is **5,000 credits** per purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the cost">
    The page shows the cost before discount, the applicable discount, and the net cost. Teams in India can optionally enter a 15-character **GSTIN** so it appears on the invoice, and 18% GST is added to the discounted amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Teams in India pay through a Razorpay payment link and can use UPI, NEFT, or credit and debit cards. All other teams pay through Stripe Checkout with Visa, Mastercard, or American Express; bank transfers are available for some countries.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your credit balance updates automatically as soon as the payment succeeds. The invoice is emailed to the **team admin**, and anyone on the team can view billing history; see [Invoices](/invoices) for details.

<Note>
Every deduction and purchase is recorded in the **Credit Usage** ledger under Account > Credit Usage, where you can also export the history as a CSV.
</Note>

## Low and Negative Balances

Equip lets your balance go slightly negative so an active hiring round is not interrupted mid-way, but there are limits:

| Balance | What happens |
|---|---|
| Below 0 | Your team cannot see any candidate results |
| Below -25 | Candidates can no longer attempt your assessments |

This also means the maximum number of candidates you can invite at any point is your current credit balance plus 25.

<Warning>
A negative balance directly affects candidates: once it drops below -25, invited candidates are blocked from attempting. Top up before large invitation batches to protect the candidate experience.
</Warning>

To help you stay ahead, Equip emails the team admin a low-balance alert when the balance drops past **20 credits** and again past **5 credits**.
