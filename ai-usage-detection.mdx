<Tip>
tl;dr: Standard proctoring catches AI tools opened in another tab. For stealth tools like InterviewCoder, or AI on a second phone, enable **Auxiliary Device** proctoring: the candidate pairs their phone with their computer, and the phone's camera watches their keyboard, hands, and screen throughout the test or interview.
</Tip>

## How Detection Works

The same layers are available for **assessments** and **AI interviews** (one-way and conversational).

| How a candidate might use AI | What catches it |
|---|---|
| Copying the question into ChatGPT | **Disable Copy & Paste** makes it hard to move question text out of the test |
| Opening ChatGPT in another tab or app | **Enforce Full Screen** and **Detect Switched Tab** flag the switch, and **Switched Tab Screenshot** captures what they switched to |
| Overlay tools on the same computer (Cluely, InterviewCoder) | **Auxiliary Device** proctoring, which films the screen from outside the computer |
| Asking AI on a second phone | **Auxiliary Device** proctoring, which shows their hands and surroundings |

The first two layers come from Equip's standard [proctoring settings](/proctoring-settings) and [anti-cheating settings](/anti-cheating-settings).

## Auxiliary Device Proctoring

**Auxiliary Device** works alongside the default proctoring settings of an assessment or AI interview.

### How to enable it for assessments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create or open the assessment">
    Proctoring settings cannot be edited during creation, so create the assessment first, then open it from your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Auxiliary Device in the Test Order tab">
    Turn on **Auxiliary Device** and click **Update**.
    {/* screenshot: Test Order tab with the Auxiliary Device option enabled */}

    <Frame caption="Auxiliary Device is the last option in a test's proctoring settings, which open from Edit Settings on the Test Order tab">
      <img src="/images/ai-usage-detection/auxiliary-device-enabled.png" width="662" alt="Lower part of the Proctoring Settings dialog for a programming test, listing Record User Session checked, Show Webcam Preview unchecked and Auxiliary Device checked with the note Candidate will have to pair their phone to their computer to take the test, above the Cancel and Save Settings buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Auxiliary Device proctoring is **free**, with no extra credit cost. It is disabled by default.
</Note>

### How to enable it for AI interviews

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the interview's settings">
    Open the interview from [**AI Interviews**](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/) and go to its settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Auxiliary Device under Proctoring Settings">
    Expand **Proctoring Settings** and turn on **Auxiliary Device**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When creating an interview in the setup flow, the **Enhanced** proctoring level turns on Auxiliary Device along with the other options.

### What candidates do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    The candidate opens the test on a computer, then scans the QR code on screen with their phone to pair the two devices.
    {/* screenshot: QR code pairing screen shown to the candidate */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record a scan of the surroundings">
    This video is uploaded for you to review later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Position the phone">
    The phone's camera feed shows their keyboard and hands, and Equip captures a screenshot of it every few seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Candidates cannot skip pairing. If Auxiliary Device proctoring is enabled on any test, the questions will not load until a phone is paired.
</Warning>

If the phone locks, the candidate sees a warning on their computer that the camera feed is inactive and must fix it before continuing.

## Reviewing Flags

With Auxiliary Device enabled, the candidate's report adds the phone's evidence: the **surroundings video** and periodic **keyboard screenshots**. For AI interviews, look in the report's **Proctoring** tab.

{/* screenshot: candidate report showing the surroundings video and keyboard screenshots from the auxiliary device */}

If the **Trust Score** is low, watch both for signs of a second device or another person.

<Tip>
If something looks suspicious but inconclusive, play back the [Session Recording](/session-recording) of the candidate's screen.
</Tip>
