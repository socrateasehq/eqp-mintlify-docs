A **Conversational AI Interview** is a live, spoken interview where Equip's AI interviewer asks questions in real time and adapts its follow-ups to each candidate's answers. Paste a job description, and Equip drafts the interview topics for you.

## Creating from a Job Description

{/* screenshot: Create Conversational Interview page with job description form */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the creation page">
    Go to [**AI Interviews**](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/), click **Add AI Interview**, then choose **Conversational Interview**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the interview type">
    Select **Quick Interview** or **Comprehensive Interview** (see the comparison below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the job description and link a job opening">
    Paste the **Job description**, then pick an existing job opening or enter a title for a new one. Enter the **Years of experience**; if you picked an existing job opening, the range comes from it and is locked. **Additional context** is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the interview">
    Click **Generate interview topics**. You can edit, reorder, or delete the generated topics before inviting candidates; see [Questions, Topics, and Scenarios](/interview-content-types).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quick vs Comprehensive

| | **Quick Interview** | **Comprehensive Interview** |
|---|---|---|
| Approximate duration | 15 minutes | 30 minutes |
| Topics | 3 to 5 | 5 to 10 |
| Total questions | 5 to 10 | 10 to 25 |
| Credits per candidate | 2 | 3 |
| Best for | Fast screening rounds | Deeper evaluation rounds |

You can change the type later on the interview's settings page. Switching from Comprehensive to Quick is blocked if the interview has more than 10 questions; remove some first.

<Tip>
Credits are deducted when a candidate is invited to the interview. If a candidate never starts, you can reclaim the credits; see [How Credits Work](/how-credits-work).
</Tip>

## Instructions for the AI Interviewer

On the interview's settings page, the **Instructions for AI Interviewer** field lets you steer the tone, pacing, and evaluation style of the interview. For example:

- "Start with easier questions, and then make them harder."
- "Score higher if they use simpler words."

{/* screenshot: Instructions for AI Interviewer field on the conversational settings page */}

<Note>
These instructions apply to the whole interview. For per-question control, edit the individual items instead.
</Note>
