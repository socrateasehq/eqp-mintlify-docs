Clicking a candidate on the applications dashboard opens their profile in a detail panel. The panel has two tabs: **Candidate Details**, which holds everything you know about the person, and **Candidate Journey**, a timeline of everything that has happened on their application.

## The Details Tab

The **Candidate Details** tab collects the candidate's full record in one scrollable view, so you never have to hunt across pages during a review.

{/* screenshot: candidate detail panel with the Candidate Details tab open */}

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Scores** | Job Fit Score with its AI explanation, plus any submitted scorecards |
| **Preferences** | Details like salary expectations and notice period |
| **Experience** | Work history parsed from the CV or entered on the application form |
| **Education** | Degrees, institutions, and fields of study |
| **Skills and languages** | Skills with expertise levels, and languages known |
| **Custom fields and screening answers** | Responses to your job-specific form fields and screening questions |
| **Attachments** | Files uploaded by your team or the candidate |

You can correct or complete a candidate's contact information at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Edit Candidate modal">
    From the candidate's profile, open the edit option to update their record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the editable fields">
    You can change the candidate's **first name**, **last name**, **email**, **phone**, **LinkedIn profile**, and **current city**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace the resume if needed">
    Upload a new **PDF, DOC, or DOCX** file up to **10 MB**. The previous resume is replaced, and the re-upload is logged in the Candidate Journey.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Edits made by your team update the candidate's record for this application. Each change is attributed to the team member who made it, so there is a clear audit trail.
</Note>

## The Candidate Journey

The **Candidate Journey** tab is a complete activity timeline for the application. It records more than 30 event types, each attributed to the team member or candidate who triggered it.

{/* screenshot: Candidate Journey tab showing a timeline of stage changes and emails */}

Events you will see in the timeline include:

- **Application events**: submitted application, updated candidate details, re-uploaded resume
- **Pipeline events**: changed stage, updated status, changed assignee, moved to a different job post
- **Team activity**: starred this candidate, updated notes, submitted scorecard
- **Emails**: sent or scheduled assessment invites, AI interview invites, video interview invites, custom stage invites, and rejection emails
- **Offer events**: offer email and offer letter activity, from sent and viewed through signed, completed, voided, or declined

<Tip>
Before a debrief or a follow-up call, skim the Candidate Journey first. It answers "where are we with this person?" in a few seconds: what was sent, when, by whom, and what the candidate has done since.
</Tip>

## Attachments and Notes

Beyond the CV, a candidate's profile can hold supporting files and your team's private commentary.

**Attachments** let you keep portfolios, take-home submissions, or reference documents with the candidate. Every attachment shows whether it was uploaded by a **Recruiter** or the **Candidate**.

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Attachments per candidate | Up to **3** |
| File size | Up to **10 MB** each |
| Attachment name | Up to **50 characters** |

**Notes** are free-form, per-candidate comments visible to your team. Use them for interview impressions, context from referrals, or anything that does not fit a structured field. Note updates appear in the Candidate Journey, and notes are included when you export applications to CSV or Excel.

<Note>
Notes and attachments live on the application, alongside stage, status, and starred state. They are internal to your team and are never shown to the candidate.
</Note>

## Unverified Candidates

Candidates who apply directly through your job post link verify their email as part of applying. Candidates added by your team, for example through [Bulk CV Import](/bulk-cv-import), start out **unverified**.

{/* screenshot: amber unverified icon next to a candidate's email with the verification popover open */}

You can spot an unverified candidate by the **amber icon** next to their email address. Hovering over it shows an **Email not verified** popover, where you can copy a **verification link** to share with the candidate directly.

<Warning>
Unverified candidates receive **no stage-change emails** until their email is verified. Invitation dialogs show a warning when you try to invite them, so verify the email first if you want the candidate to receive assessment or interview invites.
</Warning>
