Every completed AI interview produces a report for the candidate's attempt: an **AI-generated evaluation**, the full transcript, and a recording of the session.

## The AI-Generated Report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the interview's results">
    From the [**AI Interviews** dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/), click the AI interview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a candidate">
    Click the candidate whose attempt you want to review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Depending on the settings you enabled, the report shows up to three tabs:

| Tab | What it contains | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Test Summary** | The AI evaluation of the candidate's answers | Always |
| **Proctoring** | Proctoring evidence collected during the interview | Only if proctoring was enabled |
| **Session Recording** | Playback of the candidate's screen and actions | Only if **Record User Session** was enabled |

{/* screenshot: candidate report showing the Test Summary tab with recommendation, strengths, and topic scores */}

For a **conversational interview**, the default Test Summary gives a Recommendation, Strengths, Areas for Improvement, a Summary, and a Topic Performance score per topic. Topic scores are [weighted by difficulty](/interview-content-types).

For a **one-way interview**, the report shows each question alongside the candidate's answer and its AI grade.

<Note>
Teams with **AI Interview Report Templates** enabled can replace the default summary with a custom evaluation. See [Custom Report Templates](/custom-report-templates).
</Note>

If the report shows **"Processing..."**, the AI is still evaluating the attempt. Evaluation usually finishes shortly after submission.

### Downloading a PDF

Click **Download Report** to export the evaluation as a PDF you can share with hiring managers outside Equip.

## Transcript and Recording

In a **one-way** interview report, the transcript is available with each question. In a **conversational** interview report, the full transcript appears below the rest of the report, with each entry timestamped and labeled Interviewer or Candidate.

<Tip>
For conversational interviews, click any line in the transcript to jump the recording to that exact moment.
</Tip>

If the **Workspace** was enabled for a conversational interview, written and coded answers appear in the report too. Code is captured as timestamped snapshots that are seekable against the recording, plus the final version.

{/* screenshot: report transcript panel with a Workspace code snapshot open next to the recording player */}

<Note>
Interviews are recorded server-side, so the recording and transcript are available even if the candidate's browser closed unexpectedly.
</Note>
