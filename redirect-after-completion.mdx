---
title: "Redirect URL After Completion"
description: "Send candidates to a page of your choice after they submit their assessment."
---

By default, a candidate who finishes an assessment sees an **Assessment Completed** screen and simply closes the tab. If you would rather send them somewhere specific, such as a thank-you page, a scheduling link, or the next step in your application flow, enable a **Redirect URL** on the assessment.

## Setting a Redirect URL

The redirect option lives in the assessment's settings and applies to every candidate who completes that assessment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assessment's settings">
    From your dashboard, open the assessment and go to its settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the redirect">
    Find **On Assessment Completion, redirect candidate** and select **Yes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the destination URL">
    Type the full address in the **Redirect URL** field. It must be a valid web URL starting with `http://` or `https://`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Save the settings. The redirect now applies to all future completions of this assessment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<!-- screenshot: assessment settings panel showing the "On Assessment Completion, redirect candidate" option and Redirect URL field -->

<Tip>
Point the URL at a page you control, like a "thanks for applying" page on your careers site. It reassures candidates that their submission went through and tells them what happens next.
</Tip>

## What Candidates Experience

The redirect changes only the very last screen of the attempt. Everything before submission stays exactly the same.

| Redirect setting | What the candidate sees after submitting |
|---|---|
| **No** (default) | The **Assessment Completed** screen with a note that they may contact their recruiter for results. They close the tab themselves. |
| **Yes** | The same completed screen, plus the message "You will be automatically redirected in **5** seconds. Please wait." with a live countdown. The browser then opens your URL. |

<!-- screenshot: Assessment Completed screen showing the 5-second redirect countdown -->

<Note>
The countdown gives candidates a clear signal that their assessment was submitted successfully before they leave Equip. If a candidate later claims they submitted but you see no result, the completion screen is the proof to ask about.
</Note>
