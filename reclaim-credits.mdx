---
title: "Reclaiming Credits from Unattempted Invitations"
description: "Recover credits when invited candidates never start their test or interview."
---

Equip deducts credits the moment you send an **assessment** or **AI interview** invitation, even if the email bounces or is never opened. If the candidate never starts, those credits are not lost: you can **reclaim** them after 24 hours.

## When You Can Reclaim

An invitation credit becomes reclaimable only when both conditions below are true:

| Condition | Details |
|---|---|
| **Candidate has not started** | For an assessment, the candidate has not begun the first test; a test counts as started once its questions load. For an AI interview, the candidate has not started the interview. |
| **24 hours have passed** | The invitation was sent more than 24 hours ago. |

Each reclaim returns the credits deducted when that invitation was sent:

| Invitation | Credits reclaimed |
|---|---|
| **Assessment** | 1 credit (the first test) |
| **One-way AI interview** | The interview's full cost: 1 credit per 20 questions, rounded up, minimum 1 |
| **Conversational AI interview** | 2 credits (Quick Interview) or 3 credits (Comprehensive Interview) |

<Note>
If a candidate started the first test but abandoned it midway, the credit is not reclaimable. See [Credit Deduction Policy](/credit-deduction-policy) for the full breakdown.
</Note>

## How to Reclaim

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reclaim Credits and select the invitations">
    Go to [**Account > Reclaim Credits**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/reclaim-credits/). Click **Reclaim Credit** on a row, or tick several rows to reclaim them together. Assessment and AI interview invitations share one table; the **Reason** column shows the type: **Asmt Invite**, **1WayAI Invite** or **ConvAI Invite**.
    {/* screenshot: Reclaim Credits page showing the table of eligible invitations */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the reclaim">
    Click **Yes, reclaim** in the confirmation dialog.
    {/* screenshot: Confirm Reclaim dialog with Yes, reclaim and Cancel buttons */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your balance increases immediately, and the reclaim appears in your [**Credit Usage**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/credit-usage/) ledger.

## What Happens to the Link

Reclaiming a credit **revokes the candidate's invitation link**, so the candidate can no longer use it to attempt the test or interview.

<Warning>
This cannot be undone. If you want the candidate to attempt later, you must send a fresh invitation, which deducts credits again; they cannot reuse the same link.
</Warning>

If a candidate simply needs more time, leave the invitation active; the credits stay reclaimable for as long as they have not started.

## Common Scenarios

| Scenario | What to Do |
|---|---|
| **Email bounced or was not delivered** | Wait 24 hours, then reclaim the credit. |
| **Invited the same candidate twice** | Reclaim the unused invitation's credit 24 hours after it was sent. |
| **Balance ran low from unattempted invites** | Reclaim unattempted invitations to restore your balance, or purchase credits. |
