---
title: "Reclaiming Credits from Unattempted Invitations"
description: "Recover credits when invited candidates never start their test or interview."
---

Equip deducts credits the moment you send an invitation: for an assessment, **1 credit** per candidate, even if the email bounces or is never opened. If the candidate never starts, that credit is not lost: you can **reclaim** it after 24 hours. This page explains when reclaiming is possible, how to do it, and what happens to the candidate's link afterwards.

## When You Can Reclaim

An invitation credit becomes reclaimable only when both conditions below are true:

| Condition | Details |
|---|---|
| **Candidate has not started** | The candidate has not begun the first test of the assessment (or the AI interview). A test counts as started once its questions load for the candidate. |
| **24 hours have passed** | The invitation was sent more than 24 hours ago. |

Reclaiming applies to both **assessment** and **AI interview** invitations.

<Note>
If a candidate started the first test but abandoned it midway, the credit is not reclaimable. The net effect of Equip's policy is that you pay only for tests candidates actually start; see [Credit Deduction Policy](/credit-deduction-policy) for the full breakdown, including why Equip deducts at invitation time rather than at completion.
</Note>

For example, say you invite candidates A, B, and C to an assessment, so 3 credits are deducted. A never opens the link, B quits midway through the first test, and C finishes. After 24 hours, you can reclaim only A's credit.

## How to Reclaim

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Reclaim Credits page">
    In your dashboard sidebar, go to **Account > Reclaim Credits**. The page lists every invitation that is currently eligible, with columns for **Email ID**, **Reason**, **Job Opening**, and **Sent**.
    <!-- screenshot: Reclaim Credits page showing the table of eligible invitations -->
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the invitations">
    Use the **Reclaim Credit** button on a single row, or tick the checkboxes on multiple rows and use the bulk reclaim button to process them together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the reclaim">
    A **Confirm Reclaim** dialog warns that the candidate's invitation link will become invalid. Click **Yes, reclaim** to proceed, or **Cancel** to keep the invitation active.
    <!-- screenshot: Confirm Reclaim dialog with Yes, reclaim and Cancel buttons -->
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your balance increases immediately by the reclaimed amount. The transaction appears as a **Credit Reclaim** in your **Credit Usage** ledger (Account > Credit Usage).

<Tip>
If an invitation you expected to see is missing from the page, check whether 24 hours have passed since it was sent and whether the candidate has already started.
</Tip>

## What Happens to the Link

Reclaiming a credit **revokes the candidate's invitation link**. The link becomes invalid, so the candidate can no longer use it to attempt the test or interview.

<Warning>
Reclaiming also **deletes all test data** associated with that candidate's attempt, such as any points recorded. This cannot be undone. If you want the candidate to attempt later, you must send a fresh invitation, which deducts a new credit.
</Warning>

Only reclaim credits for candidates you no longer expect to attempt. If a candidate simply needs more time, leave the invitation active; the credit stays reclaimable for as long as they have not started.

## Common Scenarios

| Scenario | What to Do |
|---|---|
| **Email bounced or was not delivered** | The credit is still deducted at send time. Wait 24 hours, then reclaim it. |
| **Invited the same candidate twice** | Equip warns you before a duplicate invite. If you proceed, 2 credits are deducted; reclaim the unused invitation's credit 24 hours after it was sent. |
| **Balance ran low from unattempted invites** | You can invite up to your current credits plus 25 (the maximum negative balance). Reclaim unattempted invitations to restore your balance, or purchase credits. |

<Note>
With a negative balance you cannot see candidate results, and below the negative limit candidates can no longer attempt. Reclaiming eligible credits is often the fastest way to get back above zero.
</Note>
