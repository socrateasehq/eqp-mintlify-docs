---
title: "Reclaiming Credits from Unattempted Invitations"
description: "Recover credits when invited candidates never start their test or interview."
---

Equip deducts credits the moment you send an invitation: for an assessment, **1 credit** per candidate, even if the email bounces or is never opened. If the candidate never starts, that credit is not lost: you can **reclaim** it after 24 hours.

## When You Can Reclaim

An invitation credit becomes reclaimable only when both conditions below are true:

| Condition | Details |
|---|---|
| **Candidate has not started** | The candidate has not begun the first test of the assessment (or the AI interview). A test counts as started once its questions load for the candidate. |
| **24 hours have passed** | The invitation was sent more than 24 hours ago. |

<Note>
If a candidate started the first test but abandoned it midway, the credit is not reclaimable. See [Credit Deduction Policy](/credit-deduction-policy) for the full breakdown.
</Note>

Your balance increases immediately, and the reclaim appears in your [**Credit Usage**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/credit-usage/) ledger.

## What Happens to the Link

Reclaiming a credit **revokes the candidate's invitation link**, so the candidate can no longer use it to attempt the test or interview.

<Warning>
This cannot be undone. If you want the candidate to attempt later, you must send a fresh invitation, which deducts a new credit; they cannot reuse the same link.
</Warning>

If a candidate simply needs more time, leave the invitation active; the credit stays reclaimable for as long as they have not started.

## Common Scenarios

| Scenario | What to Do |
|---|---|
| **Email bounced or was not delivered** | Wait 24 hours, then reclaim the credit. |
| **Invited the same candidate twice** | Reclaim the unused invitation's credit 24 hours after it was sent. |
| **Balance ran low from unattempted invites** | Reclaim unattempted invitations to restore your balance, or purchase credits. |
