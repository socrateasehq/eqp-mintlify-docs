Before your first real test, Equip shows you a **Demo Tests** page. Each demo is free, takes about a minute, and does not count towards your score.

## Why Take a Demo Test

Demo tests help you in two ways:

| What the demo checks | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Interface familiarity** | You learn how questions, timers, and navigation work before the clock matters. |
| **Device and proctoring setup** | You confirm your camera, microphone, and screen permissions work for each test type. |

The demos cover the **specific test types** in your assessment. If a demo loads and runs on your device, the matching real test will too.

<Tip>
Candidates who attempt demo tests tend to score higher on their assessments.
</Tip>

## Starting a Demo

Equip **automatically redirects** you to the Demo Tests page before your first real test, unless you have already attempted or skipped the demos.

{/* screenshot: candidate Demo Tests page with per-test-type rows and Attempted checkmarks */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your assessment link">
    Sign in as usual.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a demo test and complete it">
    The page lists a demo for each test type in your assessment. Grant any camera, microphone, or screen permissions when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to the assessment">
    Your demo answers are not graded and are never shown to the recruiter as results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a demo fails to load, fix the problem before starting the real test. See [Candidate Issues](/candidate-issues) for the troubleshooting steps.

## Skipping

Demo tests are optional. Click **Skip to Assessment** at the bottom of the Demo Tests page to go straight to your first real test.

Skipping makes sense if you have already attempted the demos, or have taken Equip assessments before on the same device and browser.

<Warning>
Do not confuse finishing a demo with finishing the assessment. A common reason a candidate believes they submitted but the recruiter sees no result is that they completed only the demo test. The real assessment is finished only when you see the **Assessment Completed** screen.
</Warning>
