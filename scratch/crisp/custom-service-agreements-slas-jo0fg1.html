Custom Service Agreements, SLAs

For annual contract values (ACV) under $25,000, we DO NOT sign custom MSAs/SLAs/DPAs, modify legal terms, or complete bespoke security questionnaires.


Custom Agreements, SLAs, and Vendor Paperwork at Equip


Equip is a product-led, fully self-serve B2B SaaS. All customers are covered by our standard Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Pricing. Equip is credit-based, pay-as-you-go—there’s no subscription. You can purchase and manage credits directly in-product. Please evaluate and use Equip under the publicly available documents above.


We’ll consider custom paperwork for ACV ≥ $25,000 (e.g., limited redlines, vendor forms, or tailored SLAs) subject to review. If your projected ACV meets this threshold, share your requirements and timeline with our team.


Support is available via live chat and email.



FAQs


  1. Do you sign our MSA/SLA/DPA for a pilot or small purchase?

Not for ACV < $25,000. Please proceed under our Terms and Privacy Policy.


  1. Can you complete our security questionnaire?

For ACV < $25,000, we don’t complete bespoke questionnaires. Use our public docs for evaluation.


  1. Can you change governing law, indemnity, or confidentiality?

No changes for ACV < $25,000. These are standardized in our Terms of Service.


  1. Where can I see what’s included and pricing?

See Pricing (credit-based, pay-as-you-go).
