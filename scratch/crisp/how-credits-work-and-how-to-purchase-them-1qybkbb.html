How Credits Work, And How To Purchase Them

How Credits Work


On Equip, each team has a certain number of credits. For example, when a team is created, it starts off with 10 credits. As candidates attempt assessments, this number decreases (for more details, see our Pricing Page). The table below explains what happens when your credits become negative.


Number of Credits

Outcome

< 0

You (and your Team) cannot see any results

< -25

No candidate can attempt your assessment


Thus, to ensure a good candidate experience, it is imperative that you keep your credit balance healthy!


When is a credit deducted from my account?


A credit is deducted each time an assessment invitation is sent to a candidate. If a candidate doesn't start the first test of an assessment successfully, the credit can be reclaimed. For every test after the first one, a credit is deducted when the candidate starts the test.


For example, you create an assessment with two tests and invite 10 candidates to take the assessment. 2 candidates do not start Test 1, 3 candidates finish Test 1 and drop out before starting Test 2. 5 candidates finish both tests. You will then be charged 15 credits (10 credits for invitations + 5 credits for candidates who started Test 2), but you can reclaim 2 credits.


How To Buy Credits


Indian teams will automatically see the prices in Rupees. They can pay via UPI, NEFT and credit or debit cards.


  1. If you are logged in as a recruiter, you will see a 'Buy' button from where you can purchase credits. For example, you'll see it on Your Dashboard.

  1. On clicking the 'Buy' button, you'll see a form where you can choose the number of credits. Any applicable discounts will be shown here.

  1. You can pay via Visa, Mastercard and American Express. For some countries, Bank Transfers are also available.
  2. Once you make the purchase, your credit balance will update automatically.
  3. The team admin will receive an email of the invoice. The invoice and billing history is available to everyone on the team if they click on the Go to Billing button on the Step 2 page above.
