First Purchase Discount

What is this discount?


We offer a 50% discount on your first payment, if you make this payment within 7 days of creating your Team on Equip. To reiterate, this discount is applicable only on your first purchase, and it must happen within 7 days of you creating a Team. The maximum discount you can avail is $60 (₹5,000 for Indian customers)


If you don't avail this offer in the first 7 days, we give you another chance and give you a 25% discount on your first payment on the same terms as above. This offer is available for 2 weeks after the first offer ends.


How do I know if I am eligible for this?


The easiest way is to check the navbar section on your dashboard. If you are eligible, you should see a timer counting down to when the offer will expire. If you don't see the countdown timer, it means you are not eligible.



How do I avail this?


You don't need to take any extra steps to avail this discount. If you are eligible, the discount will automatically be applied on your checkout page (via a coupon code called FIRSTPURCHASE50 or FIRSTPURCHASE25 respectively). You can see the exact amount that is being discounted on your checkout page


FAQs


  1. Is there a minimum amount I must purchase to get this?

Yes, $10 is the minimum number of credits on our platform. Of course, once you use up the discount, you cannot use it again! So, don't waste it on a small purchase. We WILL NOT give you another chance to use the discount.


  1. Can I use both discount codes?

Nope. If you use the first, you won't see the second. If you are seeing the second, the first has already expired.


  1. Can I first make a trial purchase of a few credits and then use this discount?

Nope. FIRSTPURCHASE50 gets auto-applied on your first purchase, and you cannot use it for a later purchase


  1. I created another account and wasn't able to avail the FIRSTPURCHASE discount

It is crucial to note that everyone isn't automatically entitled to the discount, even if the website shows it. The point of the discount is encourage users who have never heard of Equip to try us out. We introduced this offer in 2024 and have had users who created another account (with a different email or domain) just to avail the discount, because they didn't make the payment within the specified period. (We send multiple email reminders during the discount availability period.) Or, they have a different entity (could be an entirely different entity from a legal and product perspective) and they want to avail the discount for that entity. In all these cases, we have retroactively cancelled the automatically applied discount. The rule of thumb is: if you have already used Equip on a different account (whether you redeemed the discount or not), you are not entitled to it for the new account. To reiterate, the point of the discount is to encourage people who've never heard of Equip to make the first payment. If your new account doesn't satisfy this, we will revoke the discount post-purchase.


If we determine that the same company (same legal entity in its country) is using different domains to create different teams so that it can avail this offer, we will withdraw the discounted credits post-purchase. Even if the entities are different, but the individuals involved are the same or related, we will follow the same process. In the case of any dispute or disagreement related to the applicability of the First Purchase Discount, the decision of the Equip team will be final and binding.
