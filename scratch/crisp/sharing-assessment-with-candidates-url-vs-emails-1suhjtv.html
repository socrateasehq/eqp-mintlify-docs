Sharing assessment with candidates: URL v/s Emails

Email Invitations v/s Common URL


After you create an assessment, you can invite the candidates by either:


  1. Inviting candidates via Email and a Unique URL: You can upload the email IDs of candidates on our portal. We will email them on your behalf and each candidate will get a unique link; OR
  2. Sharing a Common URL with all candidates: The URL will look like equip.co/assessments/abcdefg. This is useful if you are posting the link as part of a job description, on WhatsApp, etc



Email invitations generate a unique URL, which can be used only once. This can also be used to track how many candidates have opened the email, clicked on the assessment link, etc.


How the two methods work


  1. Email Invitations: Each candidate receives a unique link in the email. When they click on the link, we send a one-time password to their email ID. They use this password to authenticate and you can be sure that the link isn't used by someone who doesn't have access to the email
  2. Common URL: When a candidate clicks the URL, they are asked to enter their email ID. Once they enter their email ID, we send an OTP to the email ID they provide. After they authenticate themselves, the assessment begins


Once the assessment begins, there is no difference in the two methods. They behave identically.
