Equip generates a public **careers page** for your company automatically. It lists every published job opening at a single URL you can share, link from your website, or embed directly on your own site.

## Enable Your Careers Page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings">
    Go to [**Account → Careers Page**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/careers-page/) in your Equip dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on and set your slug">
    Enable the careers page. Equip generates a URL **slug** for you, which you can edit to a cleaner name such as `your-company`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your public URL">
    Your page is live at `https://equip.co/companies/{slug}/hiring/`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What It Includes

The public page shows every job post with the **Published** status whose deadline has not passed.

| Job post status | Appears on careers page? |
|---|---|
| **Published** | Yes |
| **Hidden** | No, and the job's direct URL returns a 404 |
| **Archived** | No, the job is no longer accepting applications |

Candidates can search by keyword, filter by work arrangement, location, employment type, and minimum salary, then click through to the full job post to apply.

## Customization

You can brand the careers page so it feels like part of your own site.

- **Company logo**: the page uses the logo from your organization settings.
- **Custom CSS**: the settings page has a **Custom CSS** field where you can restyle any part of the page, for example `#careers-hero { background-color: ...; }`.

<Warning>
Write plain CSS rules only. Do not wrap your styles in `<style>` tags; Equip adds those for you.
</Warning>

## Embedding on Your Site

The settings page provides a copyable **Embed Code**, an HTML link that points visitors to your Equip careers page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the embed code">
    On [**Account → Careers Page**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/careers-page/), copy the provided embed code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into your site">
    Add the snippet wherever you want the careers link to appear, such as your site's Careers or Jobs section, then publish your site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The embed code includes a comment asking that the link stay crawlable by search engines and count as a backlink. Keeping it intact helps both your jobs and your site get discovered.
</Note>
