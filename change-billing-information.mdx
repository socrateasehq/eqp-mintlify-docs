---
title: "Changing Business Information After an Invoice"
description: "Update your company name, address, and tax details, and fix billing information on an already-issued invoice."
---

Equip's payment gateway collects your **business details** (company name, address, and tax information) at your first purchase, and they appear on every invoice.

## What Can Be Changed

You can update your business details yourself in the **Billing Portal**, and the change applies to all future invoices. An already-issued invoice cannot be edited from the dashboard, so the Equip team re-issues it manually.

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Details wrong on **future invoices** | Update them yourself in the Billing Portal |
| Details wrong on an **already-issued invoice** | Update the Billing Portal, then email [hello@equip.co](mailto:hello@equip.co) |

<Note>
The name your invoice is billed to comes from the **Legal Entity Name** field on your [Team page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/). For teams in India, your **GSTIN** appears on the invoice only if you enter it before purchasing.
</Note>

## How to Request a Change

### Update details for future invoices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Purchase Credits page">
    In your recruiter dashboard, click **Buy More**.
    {/* screenshot: Buy More button in the dashboard sidebar */}

    <Frame caption="Buy More sits next to your credit balance at the bottom of the sidebar">
      <img src="/images/change-billing-information/buy-more-button.png" width="255" alt="Bottom of the recruiter sidebar, with the Account group and the Helpdesk link above a balance of 100 credits and a green Buy More button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Billing">
    On the [Purchase Credits page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/topup-credits/), click **Go to Billing**. This opens your Billing Portal, hosted by Stripe.
    {/* screenshot: Go to Billing button on the Purchase Credits page */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Update Information">
    Click **Update Information**, then edit and save your details.
    {/* screenshot: Update Information link in the Billing Portal */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Fix an already-issued invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update the Billing Portal first">
    Follow the steps above so the portal holds the correct details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email hello@equip.co">
    Write to [hello@equip.co](mailto:hello@equip.co) and say which invoice needs correcting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Skipping the first step delays the fix. If the Billing Portal still shows the old details, the re-issued invoice will carry the same errors.
</Warning>
