---
title: "Changing Business Information After an Invoice"
description: "Update your company name, email, and GSTIN, and fix billing information on an already-issued invoice."
---

Two places control what appears on your invoices: the **Billing Portal** (Stripe) holds your company name, email, and payment method, and the **Billing** page in Account settings holds your **GSTIN**.

## What Can Be Changed

You can update these yourself, and the change applies to all future invoices. An already-issued invoice cannot be edited from the dashboard, so the Equip team re-issues it manually.

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Company name, email, or payment method wrong on **future invoices** | Update them in the Billing Portal |
| **GSTIN** wrong or missing on **future invoices** | Update it on the Billing page |
| Details wrong on an **already-issued invoice** | Fix the right place above, then email [hello@equip.co](mailto:hello@equip.co) |

<Note>
The name your invoice is billed to comes from the **Legal Entity Name** field on your [Team page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/), under the **Organization** tab.
</Note>

## How to Request a Change

### Update company name, email, or payment method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Billing page">
    From your recruiter dashboard sidebar, go to **Account** > [**Billing**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/billing/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Billing Portal">
    Click **Open billing portal**, at the top of the Invoices section. This opens Stripe's hosted Billing Portal.
    {/* screenshot: Open billing portal button on the Billing page */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Update Information">
    Under **Billing Information**, click **Update information**, then edit and save your details.
    {/* screenshot: Update information link in the Billing Portal */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Update your GSTIN

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Billing page">
    From your recruiter dashboard sidebar, go to **Account** > [**Billing**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/billing/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the GSTIN field">
    Under **Invoices**, edit the **GSTIN** field and save.
    {/* screenshot: GSTIN field on the Billing page */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Fix an already-issued invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update the correct place first">
    Follow the steps above so the Billing Portal or the Billing page holds the correct details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email hello@equip.co">
    Write to [hello@equip.co](mailto:hello@equip.co) and say which invoice needs correcting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Skipping the first step delays the fix. If the details are still wrong, the re-issued invoice will carry the same errors.
</Warning>
