<Tip>
Equip's **REST APIs** let you invite candidates and fetch their results from your own system, so you never have to manage the pipeline in the Equip dashboard. Access requires an API Key, a one-time $500 setup fee, and a minimum monthly purchase of 500 credits.
</Tip>

## What the API Offers

Say you want to run Equip assessments from inside your ATS, or you have an internal system and do not want to invite candidates and check results manually. Equip's **REST APIs** automate both ends of that workflow.

Candidates still attempt the assessment on the Equip website. You simply never need to open the Equip dashboard to send invitations or collect scores.

| You can | You cannot |
|---|---|
| Invite candidates to an assessment programmatically | Fetch question content directly (for example, "give me 100 Python MCQs") |
| Pull candidates' results into your own system | Have candidates attempt tests outside of Equip |

<Note>
The API does not expose Equip's question bank. If your assessment uses Equip content, questions are still picked automatically from the **Question Bank** and shown only to the candidate during their attempt.
</Note>

## Costs

There is no recurring platform fee for the integration itself. Enabling it has two components:

1. A one-time setup fee of **$500**, which converts to 500 credits in your balance.
2. A minimum monthly purchase of **500 credits**.

These are normal Equip **credits**: they never expire, and you spend them on your regular usage (assessments and AI interviews) exactly as described on the [Pricing Page](https://equip.co/pricing/). So the money you pay for the integration is consumed by the hiring you were already doing.

<Note>
Credits work the same way for API-driven invitations as for dashboard invitations. See [How Credits Work](/how-credits-work) for deduction and reclaim rules.
</Note>

## Getting Access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a team">
    Sign up for a recruiter account at [equip.co](https://equip.co/account/recruiter-onboarding/) with your work email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trial Equip with free credits">
    Use your free signup credits to confirm the product works for your use case before committing to the integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purchase $500 worth of credits">
    This means $500 actually paid, not necessarily 500 credits. For example, with a 25% [first purchase discount](/first-purchase-discount), 500 credits would cost you $375, so you would need to buy 667 credits to reach $500 paid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request your API Key">
    Email hello@equip.co or use the in-product chat. The Equip team issues your team's **API Key**; there is no self-serve page to generate one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep purchasing 500 credits per month">
    Continue buying at least 500 credits each month to retain API access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Explore the APIs

A public [Postman collection](https://www.postman.com/solar-comet-241985-1/equip-api-v3/collection/6tuspon/equip-api-v3-documentation) documents the available endpoints. You can browse it freely, but every request needs your team's API Key.

For a walkthrough of wiring the pieces together, follow this [integration guide](https://gist.github.com/siddharth-soc/255c00f83c35a9a40355189346c6fb19).

<Tip>
You do not always need the full API. If you only want results pushed to you when candidates finish, [webhooks](/webhooks) are simpler. If you use Lever, the native [Lever integration](/lever-integration) needs no code at all.
</Tip>
