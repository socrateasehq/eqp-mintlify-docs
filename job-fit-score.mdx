<Tip>
Every applicant to a job opening automatically gets a **Job Fit Score** from 0 to 100, along with a one-line AI explanation of why they scored that way. You can steer the score by choosing up to 3 ranking criteria, and re-rank existing candidates whenever you change them.
</Tip>

The **Job Fit Score** is part of Equip's free ATS. It ranks your applicants so you review the most promising ones first.

## How Scoring Works

When a candidate applies, Equip's AI reads their parsed application against your job description and produces the score and its explanation.

The score appears next to every candidate in the applications table, and you can sort the table by **Fit Score**.

Scoring is **relative, not absolute**: the AI compares each new applicant against benchmark candidates from the same job's applicant pool.

<Note>
A score of 75 means the candidate ranks well **within this job's applicant pool**. Scores are not comparable across different job openings.
</Note>

If a candidate does not have a score yet, for example one added through [bulk CV import](/bulk-cv-import), their row shows a **Calculate** button.

## Ranking Criteria

By default, the AI uses its own judgment about what makes a candidate a good fit for your job description. To steer it, select up to **3 ranking criteria** from these six options:

| Criterion | What the AI weighs | Requires this application form field |
|---|---|---|
| **Skills** | How relevant the candidate's skills are to the job description | Skills |
| **Experience** | Higher weightage for more years of experience | Work Experience |
| **Education** | Degrees and institutions | Educational Background |
| **Previous Companies** | Pedigree of employment history | Work Experience |
| **Seniority** | More managerial positions get higher weightage | Always available |
| **Industry** | Relevance of the candidate's industry experience to the role | Industries |

Drag your selected criteria to prioritize them: the one at the top carries the most weight. If an option appears disabled, enable the field it depends on in your [application form](/application-form) first.

You can also add **additional criteria** as free text, up to 200 characters, such as "Prefer candidates with AWS experience".

<Note>
Changing your ranking criteria affects **future applications only**. Candidates who already applied keep their scores, and [moving a candidate](/changing-stages) to a different job post does not re-score them either.
</Note>

## Using the Score in Your Workflow

The fit score is a prioritization signal, not a verdict. Confirm your read with the candidate's full profile, [scorecards](/scorecards), and assessment results before rejecting anyone.

The score also shows up beyond the applications table:

| Where | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Kanban cards** | The fit score on each candidate card |
| **CSV export** | The score and its AI explanation as columns |
| [**Insights**](/ats-insights) | A job-fit score distribution chart for the job's applicant pool |
