Every assessment offers two downloadable reports: a spreadsheet-style export of **all candidates' results**, and a detailed PDF report for a **single candidate**.

| Report | Scope | Formats | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Export Results** | All candidates in the assessment | Excel | Top right of the assessment results page |
| **Candidate Report** | One candidate, all tests | PDF | Inside the candidate's submission page |

## Export Results for All Candidates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assessment results">
    From your **Recruiter Dashboard**, click the assessment title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export Results">
    The button is at the top right of the results page. Clicking it downloads an **Excel file** with every candidate's results.

    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
See the exact layout in [this sample export](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/10d-b3PVxmKxa-W9OqG0gjVTE-8tPGIUUe9FygeeIGbI/edit#gid=0) with dummy data.
</Tip>

### What the export contains

The export has one row per candidate: name, email, start and end dates, start and end times in UTC, any Gather Additional Details fields you enabled, and the overall percentage. For every test in the assessment, it adds points scored, points available, the [Trust Score](/trust-score) percentage, and a URL to the candidate's photo.

<Note>
The **Plagiarism Score** column is always empty. It is kept for legacy reasons.
</Note>

## Download a Single Candidate's Report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate's submission">
    On the assessment results page, click the candidate's test score (the orange link).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click download PDF">
    The **download PDF** button is on the far right of the second section of the page, across from the test dropdown. It downloads the candidate's entire submission, across all tests, as a single PDF file.
    {/* screenshot: download PDF button on the candidate submission page */}

    <Frame caption="The PDF button is on the right, level with the test dropdown">

    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The PDF may take a moment to generate before the download begins. See a sample candidate report at [this link](https://drive.google.com/file/d/147C0J28M_E9hPzQXK51F-yr6mj31W_ut/view?usp=sharing).
</Note>

## Sharing Report Links

Each candidate's report page has a **copyable report link**. Copy it to share that candidate's results with hiring managers or teammates without exporting a file, which is often faster while scores are still being finalized through [manual grading](/manual-grading).

<Tip>
To have results flow into a spreadsheet automatically as candidates finish, see [Write Results to Google Sheets](/write-results-to-google-sheets).
</Tip>
