<Tip>
Previews are **free and unlimited** for you and your whole team. Inviting anyone, including yourself, deducts credits like a normal candidate attempt.
</Tip>

## Recruiter Preview vs Candidate Preview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your assessment">
    Go to the [Assessments Dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/assessments/dashboard/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Preview">
    Use the preview link on the dashboard, or open the assessment's settings and click **Preview** at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a preview mode">
    Pick **As a Recruiter** for a quick look, or **As a Candidate** for the full candidate experience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| | As a Recruiter | As a Candidate |
|---|---|---|
| **Test navigation** | Jump freely between tests | Must finish each test before the next one appears |
| **Proctoring** | Disabled, no camera or screen sharing | Enabled, you grant camera and screen-share access |

<Note>
Use **As a Candidate** at least once before sharing an assessment widely.
</Note>

## Sample Questions in Previews

Previews show the same questions candidates see, with one exception: quizzes that use Equip's content show questions from a separate **Sample Question Bank**. Sample questions are matched to the same skill and difficulty you configured.

This keeps the real question bank from being harvested through previews. See [Why You Cannot See Quiz Questions](/cannot-see-quiz-questions).

<Note>
If the Sample Question Bank has fewer questions than you configured, your preview shows fewer questions. Candidates still see every question you configured.
</Note>

<Warning>
The only way to see the real Equip quiz questions is to invite yourself as a candidate, and that deducts credits from your account. If you need full visibility into questions, consider a [custom quiz](/custom-tests) instead.
</Warning>

## Unlocking Locked Questions

On a new account, Equip-content quizzes let you add up to **4 questions per skill, per difficulty level**. See [Question Limits](/question-limits) for every cap.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subscribe to a plan">
    Any Equip plan, Standard, Premium, or Elite, unlocks up to 10 questions per skill per difficulty level. See [How Credits Work](/how-credits-work).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to the assessment builder">
    Open the Tests tab of your assessment and increase the question count.
  </Step>
</Steps>
