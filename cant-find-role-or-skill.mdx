---

<Tip>
If Equip's library doesn't cover your role or skill, you have two options: ask Equip to build the test for you (free if it is broadly useful, about 5 business days for most skills), or create your own **Custom Test** right away.
</Tip>

Equip publishes its pre-built library on the [Skills](https://equip.co/skills/) and [Roles](https://equip.co/roles) pages. If what you need is not there, there are two ways forward.

| Option | Who creates it | Cost | Turnaround |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Request new content** | Equip's content team | Free if other customers would also benefit | About 5 business days for most skills |
| **Custom Test** | You | No extra charge to create | Immediate, as soon as you save it |

## Requesting New Content

If the role or skill you need is one that other customers are likely to want too, Equip creates it for free.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reach out on live chat">
    Open the chat widget on the Equip dashboard or website and tell the team which role or skill you want to assess, along with the experience level of your candidates. You can also email hello@equip.co.

    The team confirms whether the test qualifies for free creation and shares a timeline, about **5 business days** for most skills.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the new test to your assessment">
    Once the content is live, add it from the builder like any other Equip test.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Missing a programming language rather than a whole skill? Additional languages can often be enabled on request. See [Programming Languages](/programming-languages).
</Note>

## Using Custom Content Instead

If you need the test immediately, or the questions are specific to your company, create a **Custom Test** with your own questions. Custom tests sit alongside Equip's library content, and a single assessment can mix both freely.

See [Custom Tests](/custom-tests) for the kinds of test you can build.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Custom Tests">
    On the [Assessments Dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/assessments/dashboard/), click the [**Custom Tests**](https://equip.co/recruiter/assessments/cms/home/) link in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and save your test">
    Choose a test type, add your questions, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to an assessment">
    In the assessment builder, click the test type's **Add** button and choose **My Content** to select your test.
  </Step>
</Steps>
