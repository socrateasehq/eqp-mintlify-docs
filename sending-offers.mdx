Sending an offer moves the candidate to the **Offered** stage and emails them a link to review and sign their documents.

## Send an Offer

Before you start, attach a packet to the job's Offered stage. See [Offer Documents and Packets](/offer-documents-and-packets).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Move the candidate to Offered">
    Choose **Offered** as the target stage, then click **Continue to the offer page**. Nothing is sent, and the candidate stays in their current stage, until you send the offer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the offer details">
    Enter a value for each field, such as salary or start date. Fields start with the sample values saved on the document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Read the email, then click **Render documents** to check each document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Confirm how many days the offer stands, then click **Send offer**. To check the real email and documents first, click **Send to myself**; the candidate is not contacted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What happens when you move a candidate to Offered depends on the stage's packet:

| Offered stage setup | What happens |
|---|---|
| **No packet** | The candidate moves and nothing is sent |
| **Packet with only an email** | A dialog lets you send the offer email to one or many candidates, with Reply-To and Schedule Send |
| **Packet with documents or variables** | You continue to the offer page, one candidate at a time |

<Warning>
An offer can only go to a verified email address. If the candidate has not verified theirs, copy the verification link from the email column and share it with them first.
</Warning>

## Offer Statuses

Open the candidate's details to see the **Current offer** card and its status.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sent** | The offer has gone out |
| **Viewed** | The candidate opened the signing page |
| **Signed by Candidate** | The candidate signed; a counter-signature may still be pending |
| **Counter-Signed** | Your counter-signer has signed |
| **Completed** | Everyone has signed and the signed copies are stored |
| **Declined** | The candidate declined, with a reason. The sender and the job opening's assignee are emailed. |
| **Voided** | You cancelled the offer, or replaced it with a new one |

Download each document from the card, as sent or as signed. Every step is also logged in the **Candidate Journey**.

<Note>
Signing does not move the candidate. When an offer is completed, move the candidate to **Hired** yourself; if it is declined, move them to Rejected or Withdrawn.
</Note>

## Manage a Sent Offer

The **Current offer** card carries the actions for a live offer:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Counter-sign** | Opens the signing session for your counter-signer once the candidate has signed |
| **Copy link** | Copies the signing link the candidate was emailed |
| **Send reminder** | Emails whoever the offer is waiting on. The expiry date does not change. |
| **Send a new offer** | Opens the offer page again. Sending replaces the current offer. |
| **Void** | Cancels the offer so the candidate can no longer sign. The candidate is not notified. |

An offer's expiry date is fixed when you send it, and the signing link stops working on that date. To extend an expired offer, send a new one.

<Tip>
The **Offer** section shows while the candidate is in the Offered stage. Download the signed copies before you move the candidate on to Hired.
</Tip>

## Counter-Signing

If the packet's **Who signs** setting is **Candidate and Recruiter**, the order you chose decides who is emailed when:

| | Candidate signs first | Company signs first |
|---|---|---|
| **On send** | The candidate is emailed the offer | The counter-signer is emailed |
| **Next** | The counter-signer is emailed once the candidate signs | The candidate is emailed once the counter-signer signs |
| **Completes when** | The counter-signer signs | The candidate signs |

Only the packet's designated counter-signer can sign. They can sign from the emailed link, or use **Counter-sign** on the Current offer card when the candidate has signed first.
