The **Ashby integration** lets your team run Equip without leaving Ashby. You add an Equip assessment or AI interview to a job's interview plan, start it for a candidate from Ashby, and Ashby shows the status and score on the candidate's profile as soon as Equip has them.

## Connecting Ashby to Equip

You need to be the **primary admin** of your Equip team, on a plan that includes integrations, and an admin in Ashby.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an API key for Equip in Ashby">
    In Ashby, open **Admin → Integrations** and find the Equip tile under Assessments. Create a key in the **Ashby API Key** field and copy it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the key into Equip">
    In Equip, go to [**Account → Integrations → Ashby**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/integrations/ashby/), paste the key, and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Equip secret">
    Equip shows your **Equip secret** together with the partner base URL. Copy the secret straight away: it is shown only once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the integration in Ashby">
    Back on the Equip tile in Ashby, paste the Equip secret into the **Equip API Key** field, choose the Ashby key you created in the first step, and enable the integration.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ashby-integration-ashby-tile.png" width="640" alt="The Equip tile in Ashby with its key fields filled in" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    In Equip, click **Test connection** on the Ashby page.
    <Frame caption="The Ashby page in Equip once connected">
      <img src="/images/ashby-integration-equip-connected.png" width="640" alt="Equip's Ashby integration page showing Connected" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
If you close the secret dialog without copying the secret, click **Rotate** on the Ashby page in Equip to get a new one. Rotating replaces the old secret, so paste the new one into Ashby before Ashby next calls Equip.
</Warning>

## Using Equip from Ashby

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Equip to an interview plan">
    Open the job's **Interview Plan** in Ashby, click **Add Activity** on the stage where the assessment belongs, and choose **Equip** under Integrations.
    <Frame caption="Equip in Ashby's Add Activity panel">
      <img src="/images/ashby-integration-add-activity.png" width="380" alt="Ashby's Add Activity panel with Equip listed under Integrations" />
    </Frame>
    Then pick the assessment or AI interview. Ashby lists everything active in your Equip account, named as **opening number · job title · stage**.
    <Frame caption="Picking the Equip assessment for the stage">
      <img src="/images/ashby-integration-assessment-picker.png" width="620" alt="Ashby's Add Equip Activity panel with the assessment picker open" />
    </Frame>
    <Frame caption="An Equip assessment on the Technical Quiz stage">
      <img src="/images/ashby-integration-interview-plan-stage.png" alt="An Ashby interview plan stage with an Equip Assessment activity" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start it for a candidate">
    When a candidate reaches that stage, start the assessment from their Ashby profile. Equip emails the candidate the invitation, and the status and score appear on the same profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The invitation is the email set up for that stage or assessment in Equip, otherwise Equip's default. Replies go to the first member of the job's hiring team in Ashby.
</Note>

## What Ashby shows on the candidate

Equip writes only to the candidate's Ashby profile.

| Status | When | What Ashby shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Invited** | You start the assessment from Ashby | The name of the Equip assessment |
| **Started** | The candidate opens the invitation link | The start time |
| **Completed** | Equip has scored the attempt | Overall score as a percentage, a score per test, the [Trust Score](/trust-score), time taken, and a link to the full report in Equip |

<Frame caption="A completed Equip assessment on a candidate's Ashby profile">
  <img src="/images/ashby-integration-candidate-result.png" width="560" alt="A completed Equip Assessment activity with scores on an Ashby candidate profile" />
</Frame>

The full report, with answers, recordings and proctoring evidence, stays in Equip behind the **View in Equip** link.

## Cancelling an assessment

Cancel from Ashby, not from Equip.

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| The candidate has not opened the link yet | Equip revokes the link, refunds the credit, and Ashby shows **Cancelled** |
| The candidate has already opened the link | It can no longer be cancelled. The attempt continues and the result still reaches Ashby |

<Note>
Starting the same assessment twice for the same application is refused while the first one is open. Cancel it first, or wait for it to complete.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Ashby says something went wrong when enabling the integration">
    The Equip secret in Ashby is missing or out of date. Open the Ashby page in Equip, click **Rotate** to get a new secret, and paste it into Ashby.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Results are not appearing in Ashby">
    Click **Test connection** on the Ashby page in Equip. If Ashby rejects the key, or the key cannot write to candidates, create a new key from the Equip tile in Ashby and click **Update key** in Equip. Results arrive within about a minute of scoring; an attempt that needs [manual grading](/manual-grading) is reported once you finalize the grade.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ashby cannot start the assessment">
    Ashby shows the reason. The usual ones are: the candidate has no email address in Ashby; the assessment was archived or deleted in Equip; your Equip plan no longer includes integrations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I disconnected Ashby by mistake">
    Disconnecting removes the stored keys. Reconnect by pasting a key from Ashby again; Equip issues a new secret, which you paste into Ashby.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
