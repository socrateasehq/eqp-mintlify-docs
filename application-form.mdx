The **Application Form** is the second step of the job creation wizard, between **Job Post** and **Candidate Pipeline**. It controls what information candidates provide when they apply.

You can edit the form later from the job's Settings page.

{/* screenshot: application form builder with field toggles on the left and live candidate preview on the right */}

<Tip>
When candidates upload their CV, Equip parses it and pre-fills the form. See [CV Parsing](/cv-parsing).
</Tip>

## Standard Fields

Toggle each **standard field** on or off, and mark any enabled field as mandatory.

| Default | Fields |
|---|---|
| Always on, mandatory and locked | First Name, Last Name, Email |
| On, mandatory | Phone Number, Work Experience, Educational Background, Industries, Skills, CV/Resume, Notice Period |
| Off | Current City, LinkedIn Profile, Year of Birth, Projects/Certifications, Salary Expectation Range, Portfolio URLs, Languages Known, Willing to Relocate |

Notice Period and Salary Expectation Range (when enabled) are **sortable** in the applications dashboard.

<Note>
The [Job Fit Score](/job-fit-score) ranking criteria depend on what you collect, such as Skills, Work Experience, Educational Background, and Industries.
</Note>

<Warning>
Every mandatory field adds friction, so only require fields you will actually use.
</Warning>

## Custom Fields

When the standard fields do not cover something you need, add **custom fields**. Each job opening supports up to 10.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a custom field">
    Give it a label, for example "Do you hold a valid driving license?".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the answer type">
    Choose Text, Boolean (yes/no), Integer (a number), or Date, then set whether the field is mandatory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Custom field answers appear on each [candidate's profile](/candidate-profile), and you can use them as **filters** in the applications dashboard.

{/* screenshot: adding a custom field with the Boolean / Integer / Date type selector */}

## Screening Questions

**Screening questions** are questions candidates answer as part of the application. Each job opening supports up to 5.

| Answer type | Best for | Filterable in dashboard |
|---|---|---|
| **Text** | Open-ended answers, such as "Why do you want this role?" | No, read on the candidate profile |
| **Boolean** | Yes/no eligibility checks, such as work authorization | Yes |
| **Integer** | Numeric answers, such as years with a specific tool | Yes |
| **Date** | Date answers, such as earliest joining date | Yes |
