<Tip>
Choose **Medium** for candidates with 0-2 years of experience, **Hard** for 3-4 years, and **Expert** for 5 or more years.
</Tip>

## Difficulty Levels

Every question in Equip's content library is tagged with a **difficulty level**, and you choose the level when you create an assessment. The available levels depend on the [test type](/test-types):

| Test Type | Difficulty Levels |
|---|---|
| Quiz, Coding (Programming, SQL, CSS), Excel | Medium, Hard, Expert |
| English Communication (CEFR) | Set by the recruiter, or adapts to candidate responses |

## Matching Difficulty to Experience

For Equip's quiz content, each difficulty level maps to a **years of experience** range and a question point value.

| Years of Experience | Difficulty Level | Question Points |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 yrs | Medium | 1 or 2 points |
| 3-4 yrs | Hard | 3 or 4 points |
| 5+ yrs | Expert | 5 or 6 points |

Equip picks questions of the matching point value **at random** for every candidate. See [Same or Random Questions](/same-or-random-questions) for details.

### Choosing the Right Difficulty

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with the role">
    In the assessment builder, enter the **Role** you are hiring for and select the **Years of Experience**. Equip suggests tests and skills at the appropriate difficulty, which you can adjust per skill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sample the content">
    Visit Equip's [Skills Page](https://equip.co/skills) to see sample questions for a skill.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Setting the difficulty too high for the experience range you are hiring for can reject good candidates.
</Warning>
