---
title: "What Is the Trust Score?"
description: "The 0-100% score that summarizes how reliable a candidate's proctored session was."
---

<Tip>
The **Trust Score** is a 0 to 100% rating shown at the top of every proctored assessment result and AI interview report. It summarizes how likely it is that the candidate attempted the test or interview fairly. As a rule of thumb, review the full proctoring evidence for any score below about 80%.
</Tip>

Every completed proctored **assessment** attempt and **AI interview** (one-way or conversational) receives a Trust Score. The ATS has none, since live interviews, applications and offers are not proctored. Instead of asking you to sift through photos, screenshots, and audio clips for every candidate, the score condenses all proctoring signals into a single number so you can focus your review where it matters.

## How the Trust Score Is Calculated

Equip's proctoring is powered by **AutoProctor**, and the Trust Score is computed automatically from the events recorded during the attempt. Violations detected by the active proctoring measures lower the score; a clean session keeps it high.

The signals that can generate violations depend on which [proctoring settings](/proctoring-settings) are enabled for each test. AI interviews have their own Proctoring Settings, in the interview's [settings](/interview-settings).

| Product | Scores per attempt | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Assessments** | One per test; per-test scores are in [downloaded results](/download-reports) | Every completed attempt |
| **AI interviews** | One for the whole interview | At least one tracking option other than session recording is on |

<Note>
On an AI interview report, the score reads **N/A** until the interview is finished, or when a finished attempt has no score.
</Note>

## Reading the Score

A higher score means fewer and less serious violations were recorded.

| Score | What it suggests | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Around 80% and above** | The session looks reliable | No action usually needed |
| **Below about 80%** | Enough violations to warrant a look | Review the full proctoring report |

<Warning>
A low score is a signal, not proof of cheating. Harmless events, such as background noise at home or an accidental tab switch, can register as violations. Always check the evidence before rejecting a candidate.
</Warning>

## Acting on Low Scores

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate's report">
    For an assessment, open the candidate from the Results page. For an AI interview, open the candidate's [interview report](/interview-reports).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Proctoring tab">
    Check whether the violations listed here look deliberate or incidental. AI interview reports show this tab next to the summary when proctoring is enabled.
    {/* screenshot: Proctoring tab showing a violations list with timestamps and evidence thumbnails */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the Session Recording">
    If [session recording](/session-recording) was enabled, play it back. Then weigh the evidence against the candidate's performance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If many candidates score low on the same assessment, revisit its proctoring setup: **Detect Audio** in a noisy room, for example, can produce frequent but harmless violations.
</Tip>
