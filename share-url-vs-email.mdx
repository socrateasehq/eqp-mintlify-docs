---

<Tip>
Use a **Unique URL** (sent via email invitations) when you already have candidates' email IDs and want to control exactly who attempts. Use a **Common URL** when you are posting the assessment somewhere open, like a job description, and do not know the email IDs in advance.
</Tip>

## The Two Link Modes

After you create an assessment or AI interview, Equip gives you two ways to put it in front of candidates:

1. **Unique URL**: You add candidates' email IDs on Equip, and Equip emails them on your behalf. Each candidate receives a **unique link** that works only once.
2. **Common URL**: A shared URL that looks like `equip.co/assessments/abcdefg`. You can paste it anywhere: a job description, a sourcing site, WhatsApp, or your careers page.

<Note>
The difference only affects how candidates arrive and when credits are deducted. Once a candidate authenticates and the assessment begins, both modes behave identically.
</Note>

## Common URL

<Steps>
  <Step title="Candidate opens the link">
    The candidate clicks the shared URL from wherever you posted it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Candidate enters their email ID">
    Equip sends a one-time verification code to that address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Candidate verifies and starts">
    After entering the code, the candidate is authenticated and the assessment begins.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Credits are deducted only when a candidate starts the first test**, not when you share the URL.

<Warning>
You cannot restrict who attempts through a Common URL, and you cannot cap how many people attempt. If you need control over the candidate list, use a Unique URL instead.
</Warning>

Each candidate is identified by their email ID, so the same person cannot attempt the assessment twice through the Common URL. See [Candidate Re-Attempts](/candidate-reattempts) if you need to allow a retake.

## Unique URL

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite candidates">
    Open the assessment and click **Invite**. Add email addresses or upload a CSV, then send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Candidate verifies and starts">
    The one-time verification code always goes to the address you invited, so a forwarded link is useless to anyone else.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Track Invitations** tab shows the delivery status of each invitation. See [Email Status](/email-status).

{/* screenshot: Track Invitations tab showing per-candidate delivery statuses */}

<Note>
A Unique URL deducts **1 credit per candidate**, charged at the moment you send the invitation. If a candidate never starts, you can reclaim that credit after 24 hours. See [Reclaim Credits](/reclaim-credits).
</Note>

Invitation links never expire unless you set a **Link Expiry (days)** value when sending.

## Which Should You Use

| | Unique URL | Common URL |
|---|---|---|
| **Best for** | Candidates whose email IDs you already have | Job descriptions and sourcing sites where you do not know email IDs beforehand |
| **Track invitation status** | Yes | No |
| **Credit deduction** | When the candidate is invited (reclaimable if they never attempt) | When the candidate starts the first test |
| **Control who attempts** | Yes, you provide the email ID | No, the candidate provides their own email ID |
| **Control how many attempt** | Yes, only invited candidates can attempt | No, anyone with the link can attempt |
