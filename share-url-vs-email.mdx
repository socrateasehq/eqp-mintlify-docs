title: "Sharing via URL vs Email Invitations"
description: "The two ways to get an assessment or AI interview in front of candidates, and when to use each."
---

<Tip>
Use a **Unique URL** (sent via email invitations) when you already have candidates' email IDs and want to control exactly who attempts. Use a **Common URL** when you are posting the assessment or AI interview somewhere open, like a job description, and do not know the email IDs in advance.
</Tip>

## The Two Link Modes

After you create an assessment or AI interview, Equip gives you two ways to put it in front of candidates:

1. **Unique URL**: You add candidates' email IDs on Equip, and Equip emails them on your behalf. Each candidate receives a **unique link** that works only once.
2. **Common URL**: A shared URL that looks like `equip.co/assessments/abcdefg`. Paste it anywhere, such as a job description or your careers page.

**AI interviews** offer both modes too; their Common URL follows the pattern `equip.co/ai-interviews/<label>`.

## Common URL

<Steps>
  <Step title="Candidate opens the link and enters their email ID">
    Equip sends a one-time verification code to that address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Candidate verifies and starts">
    After entering the code, the assessment or AI interview begins.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Credits are deducted only when a candidate starts**, not when you share the URL: at the first test of an assessment, or when the AI interview begins.

<Warning>
You cannot restrict who attempts through a Common URL, and you cannot cap how many people attempt. If you need control over the candidate list, use a Unique URL instead.
</Warning>

The same email ID cannot attempt twice through the Common URL. See [Candidate Re-Attempts](/candidate-reattempts) to allow a retake.

## Unique URL

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite candidates">
    Click **Invite** on the assessment or AI interview, and add email addresses or upload a CSV, then send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Candidate verifies and starts">
    The one-time verification code always goes to the address you invited, so a forwarded link is useless to anyone else.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Track Invitations** tab shows the delivery status of each invitation. See [Email Status](/email-status).

{/* screenshot: Track Invitations tab showing per-candidate delivery statuses */}

<Note>
A Unique URL deducts credits at the moment you send the invitation: **1 credit per candidate** for an assessment, or the AI interview's per-candidate rate (2 credits for Quick, 3 for Comprehensive, one-way tiered by question count). If a candidate never starts, you can reclaim that credit after 24 hours. See [Reclaim Credits](/reclaim-credits).
</Note>

<Note>
If the assessment or AI interview is a stage of a job opening, invite candidates by moving them to that stage in the [Job Applications Dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/job-openings/dashboard/). See [Changing Stages](/changing-stages).
</Note>

Invitation links never expire unless you set a **Link Expiry (days)** value when sending.

## Which Should You Use

| | Unique URL | Common URL |
|---|---|---|
| **Best for** | Candidates whose email IDs you already have | Job descriptions and sourcing sites where you do not know email IDs beforehand |
| **Track invitation status** | Yes | No |
| **Credit deduction** | When the candidate is invited (reclaimable if they never start). Assessments: 1 credit; AI interviews: the interview's per-candidate rate | When the candidate starts the first test or the AI interview, at the same per-candidate rate |
| **Control who attempts** | Yes, you provide the email ID | No, the candidate provides their own email ID |
| **Control how many attempt** | Yes, only invited candidates can attempt | No, anyone with the link can attempt |
