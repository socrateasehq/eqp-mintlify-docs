---
title: "Equip Content vs Custom Content"
description: "The difference between Equip's pre-built question library and tests you create with your own questions."
---

Every test type in the assessment builder offers two sources of questions: **Equip Content**, drawn from Equip's pre-built question banks, and **My Content**, which is any [Custom Test](/custom-tests) you create yourself.

## What Is Equip Content

**Equip Content** is Equip's own library of curated questions, organized by skill and difficulty. You pick a skill and an experience level, and Equip assembles the test for you, so you can create a complete assessment in seconds without writing a single question.

Equip Content is built for fairness and integrity:

- Quiz questions are **randomized per candidate**: each candidate gets different questions from a large pool, in a shuffled order, with shuffled options. See [Same or Random Questions](/same-or-random-questions).
- Quiz questions stay **hidden from recruiters**, both during creation and in results, so the question bank cannot leak. See [Why You Cannot See Quiz Questions](/cannot-see-quiz-questions).
- **Timers, points, and negative marking are fixed** and cannot be changed. Proctoring settings can still be adjusted.

<Note>
For non-quiz Equip Content, such as Programming and SQL challenges, you can select the exact question and see it in candidate results.
</Note>

## What Is My Content

**My Content** refers to Custom Tests: tests you build with your own questions, across every test type, Quiz, Coding Challenge, Video Response, Question Bank, and Question Bank Quiz.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Custom Test">
    Open [**Custom Tests**](https://equip.co/recruiter/assessments/cms/home/) from the [Assessments Dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/assessments/dashboard/) sidebar and build your test.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your assessment">
    Go to the assessment's settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your content">
    On the **Tests** tab, pick the matching test type and click **Add**, then choose My Content and select your Custom Test.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Custom Tests let you write your own questions across 15 [question types](/custom-quiz-question-types), set points, negative marking, time limits, and the [display mode](/quiz-display-modes), and see every response a candidate submitted.

<Note>
Custom Tests belong to your whole team. Teammates can view, edit, and reuse them across multiple assessments.
</Note>

## When to Use Each

| Feature | Equip Content | Custom Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Select questions** | No for quizzes; yes for other test types | Yes for all test types |
| **Set points and remove negative points for MCQs** | No | Yes |
| **Show all quiz questions at once** | No, always one-by-one | Yes |
| **See questions a candidate attempted** | No for quizzes; yes for other test types | Yes for all test types |
| **Set time per question or test** | No | Yes |

<Note>
Neither option supports a single timer for the whole assessment. Candidates can take breaks between tests, so [time limits](/time-limits) apply at the test or question level instead.
</Note>

Choose **Equip Content** when speed and reliability matter most.

Choose **Custom Content** when the role demands questions specific to your company, or when you need control over points, timers, display modes, or full visibility into what each candidate answered.

<Tip>
You do not have to pick one. An assessment can freely combine Equip Content and Custom Tests, for example an Equip quiz for screening plus a custom Video Response test for role-specific questions.
</Tip>

<Warning>
Custom coding challenges are easy to get wrong: a small mistake in test cases or boilerplate can mark every submission incorrect. For Programming and SQL, Equip strongly recommends its own questions, which are battle-tested by thousands of candidates. See [Issues with Custom Coding Challenges](/custom-coding-challenge-issues).
</Warning>
