---
title: "Candidate Login Methods"
description: "Sign in to an Equip assessment with a magic link, a 6-digit email code, or your Google account. No password required."
---

When you open an assessment link, Equip shows a **Sign in to continue** page with two options: **Sign In with Google** and **Sign In with Email**. There are no passwords and no account setup; verifying your email address is all it takes.

If you choose email, Equip sends a single verification email that contains both a **magic link** and a **6-digit login code**. Use whichever is more convenient.

{/* screenshot: candidate "Sign in to continue" page showing the Sign In with Google and Sign In with Email buttons */}

| Method | How it works | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Magic link** | Click the link in the verification email | Signing in on the same device where you read email |
| **Email login code** | Type the 6-digit code from the same email | Reading email on your phone but testing on a computer |
| **Google sign-in** | Authenticate with your Google account | Skipping the verification email entirely |

## Magic Links

Choose **Sign In with Email** and Equip emails you a login link. The sign-in page prompts you to "Click the link in the email to verify your account."

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assessment link">
    Use the invitation link from your recruiter, or the shared assessment URL, and enter your email address if asked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Look for the verification email from Equip. If it does not arrive within a minute or two, check your Spam and other folders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the login link">
    Clicking the link verifies your account and takes you straight into the assessment flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
The magic link is valid for **30 minutes**. If it expires, go back to the assessment link and sign in again to receive a fresh email.
</Warning>

## Email Login Codes

The same verification email also contains a **6-digit Equip Login Code**. This is handy when you read the email on one device but want to take the assessment on another.

On the sign-in page, follow the prompt "Enter the 6-digit Equip Login Code that was sent to your email", type the code, and click **Verify & Continue**.

{/* screenshot: email verification screen with the 6-digit code input and Verify & Continue button */}

<Note>
Like the magic link, the code expires in **30 minutes**. Both arrive in the same email, so you never have to choose in advance.
</Note>

<Warning>
If your recruiter invited you with a unique link, the code is always sent to the **invited email address**, no matter who opens the link or on which device. Forwarding your invitation to someone else will not let them sign in, because the code still goes to your inbox.
</Warning>

## Google Sign-In

**Sign In with Google** skips the verification email entirely. Authenticate with your Google account and Equip verifies your email address through Google directly.

Equip identifies candidates by **email address**, so sign in with the Google account that matches the email your recruiter invited. Using a different address makes Equip treat you as a different candidate, and a unique invitation link stays tied to the invited email.

<Tip>
Need to switch devices mid-attempt? Open the same assessment link on the new device and verify the same email address again. Your attempt resumes with the remaining time intact.
</Tip>
