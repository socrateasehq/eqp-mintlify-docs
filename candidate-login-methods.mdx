---
title: "Candidate Login Methods"
description: "Sign in to an Equip assessment, AI interview or job application with a 6-digit email code sent to your inbox. No password required."
---

When you open an assessment or AI interview link, or apply to a job, Equip shows a **Sign in to continue** page. There are no passwords and no account setup; verifying your email address is all it takes.

| Where you sign in | When |
|---|---|
| Assessment link | Before the assessment starts |
| AI interview link | Before the interview starts |
| Job application form | Before you can apply. Viewing the job post needs no sign-in. |

Enter your email address (for an invitation, the one your recruiter invited) and click **Sign In with Email**. Equip emails you a **6-digit login code**; type it in and click **Verify & Continue**.

{/* screenshot: candidate "Sign in to continue" page showing the email verification code entry */}

<Note>
The code expires in **10 minutes**. If it expires, go back to the same link to request a new one.
</Note>

<Warning>
If your recruiter invited you with a **unique link** (to an assessment or AI interview), the sign-in page opens with the invited email filled in and the code is always sent to that address. Forwarding your invitation will not let someone else sign in. If you are already signed in with a different email, you see **Incorrect Email ID**: log out and sign in with the invited email.
</Warning>

<Tip>
Need to switch devices mid-assessment? Open the same assessment link on the new device and verify the same email address again. Your attempt resumes with the remaining time intact.
</Tip>
