title: "Candidate Login Methods"
description: "Sign in to an Equip assessment with a 6-digit email code sent to your inbox. No password required."
---

When you open an assessment link, Equip shows a **Sign in to continue** page. There are no passwords and no account setup; verifying your email address is all it takes.

<Frame caption="The Sign in to continue page asks only for your email address">
  <img src="/images/candidate-login-methods/sign-in-page.png" width="528" alt="Candidate sign-in card reading Hello, Sign in to continue, with sarah.williams@example.com typed in the email field above a Sign In with Email button and links to the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy" />
</Frame>

Enter the email address your recruiter invited. Equip emails you a **6-digit login code**; type it in and click **Verify & Continue**.

<Frame caption="Enter the 6-digit code from your email, then select Verify &amp; Continue">
  <img src="/images/candidate-login-methods/verify-email-code.png" width="504" alt="Verify your email screen saying a verification email was sent to sarah.williams@example.com, with the prompt Enter the 6-digit Equip Login Code that was sent to your email, six code boxes, a Verify &amp; Continue button, a Return button, and a reminder to check the spam folder" />
</Frame>

<Note>
The code expires in **10 minutes**. If it expires, go back to the assessment link to request a new one.
</Note>

<Warning>
If your recruiter invited you with a unique link, the code is always sent to the **invited email address**, no matter who opens the link or on which device. Forwarding your invitation to someone else will not let them sign in, because the code still goes to your inbox.
</Warning>

<Tip>
Need to switch devices mid-attempt? Open the same assessment link on the new device and verify the same email address again. Your attempt resumes with the remaining time intact.
</Tip>
