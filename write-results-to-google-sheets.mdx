---
title: "Write Results to Google Sheets"
description: "Sync assessment results to a Google Sheet automatically, in real time, as candidates complete each test."
---

Equip can write assessment results directly to a **Google Sheet** as candidates finish each test. You set it up once per assessment and never need to re-export. For a snapshot once everyone has finished, use [Export to Excel](/download-reports) instead.

## Enabling the Sync

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Google Spreadsheet">
    Create a new, empty spreadsheet in your Google account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant Editor access to hello@equip.co">
    Share the spreadsheet with `hello@equip.co` as **Editor**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the spreadsheet URL in assessment settings">
    Open the assessment's Settings tab, find **Write results to Google Sheets** under Miscellaneous, and paste the spreadsheet URL. Click **Create/Update** to save.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/write-results-to-google-sheets-url-field.png" alt="Write results to Google Sheets field in assessment settings" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
If `hello@equip.co` does not have Editor permission on the spreadsheet, Equip cannot write to it and emails the assessment creator asking them to enable Editor access.
</Warning>

## What Gets Synced

Equip creates **one sheet per test** plus an overall assessment sheet inside your spreadsheet. Sheet names come from the assessment and test titles, so give each a distinct title.

| Sheet | Columns |
|---|---|
| **Assessment sheet** (overall) | Email, Name, Overall Score, Start Date, Start Time, End Date, End Time |
| **Test sheet** (one per test) | Email, Name, Points Scored, Percentage Scored, Trust Score, Start Date, Start Time, End Date, End Time |

See a [sample results spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1TmWMzzIb92SK4SKm_L7cjaNCJyXM747YPWRVN75pb_Y/edit#gid=428284911).

## Troubleshooting

- **Older attempts are missing.** Only candidates who attempt the assessment after you set up the sync are written to the sheet. Use [Export Results](/download-reports) for earlier attempts.
- **The sync stopped working.** Check that `hello@equip.co` still has Editor permission and that the spreadsheet URL saved in assessment settings is correct.
- **Rows stopped appearing after edits to the spreadsheet.** Do not rename the sheets Equip creates, and do not change their column names. Equip relies on both to find where to write.

<Note>
The full results, including per-question detail and proctoring evidence, always remain available on your Equip dashboard.
</Note>
