---
title: "How to Resume Your Attempt"
description: "Continue your assessment or AI interview after a disconnect, page refresh, or accidental tab close."
---

<Tip>
Click the same assessment or interview link again, on any device. Equip automatically resumes your attempt wherever possible. A conversational AI interview is the exception: once started, it cannot be reopened unless your recruiter allows it. If an attempt cannot be resumed, your recruiter must delete it and invite you again.
</Tip>

## When You Can Resume

Refreshing the page, or opening the link in another tab, browser, or device, all count as **resuming**. Whether you pick up where you left off depends on whether the time limit sits on each question or on the whole test. See [Time Limits](/time-limits).

| Timer is on | Tests | When you resume |
|---|---|---|
| Each question | Video Response, One-by-one Quiz | The question you were on is forfeited, and you continue from the next one. You can resume any number of times. |
| The whole test | All-at-once Quiz, Programming Test, CSS Test, SQL Test | The clock keeps running while you are disconnected. A 45-minute test started at 11:00 AM always ends at 11:45 AM. |

<Note>
If you were invited by email with a unique link, you can switch to a completely different device and your remaining time carries over.
</Note>

## How to Resume

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your assessment link on any device">
    Use the link you started with, from your invitation email or your recruiter. Sign in with the **same email ID**, because a different email would start a different candidate's attempt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue the assessment">
    Equip drops you back into the test you were on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
In a **one-by-one quiz** or a **video response** test, the question that was on screen when you disconnected cannot be answered again. You must skip it, and it earns no points.
</Warning>

## Tests That Cannot Be Resumed

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| The whole-test timer has run out | A 45-minute test that started at 11:00 AM cannot be reopened at 11:50 AM |
| The assessment is already finished | The link shows the completion screen |
| The invitation link is invalid, already used, or expired | An error page explains why the link no longer works |
| The recruiter deleted the attempt | The old link no longer resumes anything; a fresh invitation is needed |

If your attempt cannot be resumed, ask your recruiter to **delete the attempt** and send you a new invitation. See [Candidate Re-Attempts](/candidate-reattempts) for how that works.

<Note>
A re-invitation after a deleted attempt costs the recruiter another credit.
</Note>

## Resuming an AI Interview

| Interview type | What happens when you reopen the link |
|---|---|
| One-way | Your answers are saved automatically about every 20 seconds and when you switch away from or close the tab. Your text answers, multiple-choice selections and audio or video recordings are restored, and retakes already used carry over. On a timed interview, the clock keeps running from when you started. |
| Conversational | During a short connection drop, a **Connection lost, reconnecting** banner appears and Equip keeps retrying for up to 4 minutes. If you reconnect in time, the interview continues where it left off. Otherwise the interview ends, and you must contact your recruiter to retake it. |

<Warning>
A **conversational AI interview** cannot be reopened once it has started. The link shows **AI Interview Completed** instead. If your recruiter has turned on **Allow resuming unfinished attempt**, you can reopen an unsubmitted interview, but it restarts from the beginning; only the attempt you submit is graded.
</Warning>
