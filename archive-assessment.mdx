---
title: "Archiving an Assessment"
description: "Close an assessment to new attempts while keeping every result accessible."
---

## How to Archive

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the assessment">
    Open your [Assessments Dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/assessments/dashboard/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Archive">
    Click **Archive** in the Actions column for that assessment. It leaves your dashboard and no new attempts can be made.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Before you archive, check for candidates you invited who never started. Unstarted invitations are eligible for a credit refund. See [Reclaiming Credits](/reclaim-credits).
</Tip>

## What Candidates See

| | Active assessment | Archived assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **Candidate opens the link** | Can attempt the assessment | Sees a message that the assessment is closed |
| **Visible on your dashboard** | Yes | No, moved to the archived list |
| **Existing results** | Accessible | Accessible |

## Un-Archiving

You can restore an archived assessment to your dashboard at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Usage page">
    Go to your [Usage page](https://equip.co/recruiter/assessments/usage/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="View archived assessments">
    Click the link to your **archived assessments**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it active">
    Click the **three dots** next to the assessment and choose the option to make it active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: archived assessments list on the Usage page with the three-dots restore option */}

<Frame caption="The archived list, where the three-dots menu on a row offers Make Active">
  <img src="/images/archive-assessment/archived-list.png" width="996" alt="Archived Assessments page listing QA Engineer and Customer Support Associate with their started and finished counts and created dates, and the three-dots menu open on the last row showing Make Active" />

<Note>
You do not need to un-archive an assessment to review its results. Click it in the archived assessments list.
</Note>

<Warning>
Assessments cannot be deleted, only archived. This is by design, so that candidate results are never lost.
</Warning>
