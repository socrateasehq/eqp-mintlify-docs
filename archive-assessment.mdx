---
title: "Archiving an Assessment"
description: "Close an assessment to new attempts while keeping all results."
---

Once an assessment has served its purpose, you can **archive** it. Archiving closes the assessment to new attempts and removes it from your dashboard, while every result stays safely accessible.

## How to Archive

Recruiters typically archive an assessment for one of two reasons: the hiring round is over and no new candidates should attempt it, or the dashboard has too many assessments and needs decluttering.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the assessment">
    On your recruiter dashboard, find the assessment you want to close.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the three-dots menu">
    Click the **three dots** next to the assessment to open its options menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Archive">
    Select **Archive**. The assessment immediately disappears from your dashboard and stops accepting new attempts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<!-- screenshot: three-dots menu on the dashboard with the Archive option highlighted -->

<Note>
Archiving never deletes anything. All candidate submissions, scores, and reports are retained exactly as they were.
</Note>

## What Candidates See

If a candidate opens the link to an archived assessment, they see a message telling them that the **assessment has been closed**. They cannot start or resume an attempt.

| | Active assessment | Archived assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **Candidate opens the link** | Can attempt the assessment | Sees a message that the assessment is closed |
| **Visible on your dashboard** | Yes | No, moved to the archived list |
| **Existing results** | Accessible | Still accessible |

<Tip>
Archive an assessment as soon as a hiring round closes. It is the cleanest way to make sure late visitors to a shared link cannot submit new attempts.
</Tip>

## Un-Archiving

Archiving is fully reversible. You can restore an archived assessment to your dashboard at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Usage page">
    Go to your [Usage page](https://equip.co/recruiter/assessments/usage/) in the recruiter dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View archived assessments">
    Click the link to your **archived assessments** to see the full list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it active">
    Click the **three dots** next to the assessment and choose the option to make it active. It reappears on your dashboard and candidates can attempt it again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<!-- screenshot: archived assessments list on the Usage page with the three-dots restore option -->

<Note>
You do not need to un-archive an assessment to review its results. From the archived assessments list, click any assessment to view its results as usual.
</Note>

<Warning>
Assessments cannot be deleted, only archived. This is by design, so that candidate results are never lost.
</Warning>
