Standard [AI proctoring](/ai-proctoring) tells you how a candidate behaved during a test. These two add-ons answer a more fundamental question: is the person on camera actually the candidate you invited?

Both **ID Card Verification** and **Impersonation Detection** are paid add-ons that extend Equip's built-in proctoring.

## ID Card Verification

**ID Card Verification** confirms the test-taker's identity before any questions appear. The candidate photographs an official ID, and Equip matches it against the person taking the test.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Candidate photographs their ID">
    At the start of the test, the candidate holds up a government-issued or institution-issued ID card to the camera and captures a photo of it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Equip matches the face">
    The photo on the ID card is compared with the face of the person on camera.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Equip matches the name">
    The name printed on the ID is checked against the candidate taking the test.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: candidate holding an ID card up to the camera during the verification step */}

<Note>
Even without this add-on, the **Capture Photo Before Start Test** proctoring setting takes a photo of the candidate's face before every test. ID Card Verification goes further by checking that face and name against an official document.
</Note>

## Impersonation Detection

A check at the start cannot catch someone who swaps in after the test begins. **Impersonation Detection** uses AI to watch the camera feed across the entire session and raises a flag when the person on camera does not match, for example when a different person takes over partway through the attempt.

The two add-ons cover different moments of the attempt:

| Add-on | When it runs | What it catches |
|---|---|---|
| **ID Card Verification** | Once, at the start of the test | A test-taker whose face or name does not match the ID |
| **Impersonation Detection** | Continuously, throughout the session | A different person appearing on camera after the test has started |

<Tip>
Treat these flags like any other proctoring signal. If an attempt is flagged, open the **Proctoring** tab of the candidate's report to review the photo evidence alongside the [Trust Score](/trust-score) before making a decision.
</Tip>

## Enabling These Add-Ons

Both features are paid add-ons and are switched off by default. They are not part of the standard per-test proctoring toggles that you configure yourself.

To add them to your assessments, reach out to the Equip team through the in-app chat or write to hello@equip.co. The team will confirm pricing and enable the checks for you.

For context, here is how they sit alongside the identity measures Equip already runs at no extra cost:

| Identity measure | Cost | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Device fingerprinting | Included | Every attempt, always on |
| IP recording with geolocation | Included | Every attempt, always on |
| Multi-session detection | Included | Every attempt, always on |
| Capture Photo Before Start Test | Included | Optional per-test proctoring toggle |
| ID Card Verification | Paid add-on | Enabled on request |
| Impersonation Detection | Paid add-on | Enabled on request |

<Note>
Want to see these checks in action first? Equip has a self-serve public demo of ID verification and impersonation detection that runs without logging in. Ask the team for the demo link when you get in touch.
</Note>
