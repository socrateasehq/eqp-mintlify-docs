Standard [AI proctoring](/ai-proctoring) tells you how a candidate behaved during a test. **ID Card Verification** and **Impersonation Detection** are paid add-ons that check whether the person on camera is the candidate you invited.

## ID Card Verification

**ID Card Verification** confirms the test-taker's identity before any questions appear.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Candidate photographs their ID">
    At the start of the test, the candidate holds a government-issued or institution-issued ID card up to the camera.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Equip matches the face and the name">
    The photo on the ID is compared with the face on camera, and the printed name is checked against the candidate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: candidate holding an ID card up to the camera during the verification step */}

<Note>
Without this add-on, the **Capture Photo Before Start Test** proctoring setting still takes a photo of the candidate's face before every test.
</Note>

## Impersonation Detection

**Impersonation Detection** uses AI to watch the camera feed across the entire session and raises a flag when the person on camera does not match.

| Add-on | When it runs | What it catches |
|---|---|---|
| **ID Card Verification** | Once, at the start of the test | A test-taker whose face or name does not match the ID |
| **Impersonation Detection** | Continuously, throughout the session | A different person appearing on camera after the test has started |

<Tip>
If an attempt is flagged, open the **Proctoring** tab of the candidate's report to review the photo evidence alongside the [Trust Score](/trust-score).
</Tip>

## Enabling These Add-Ons

Both add-ons are switched off by default, and they are not among the per-test proctoring toggles you configure yourself.

To add them, reach out to the Equip team through the in-app chat or write to hello@equip.co. The team will confirm pricing and enable the checks for you.

At no extra cost, Equip already runs device fingerprinting, IP recording with geolocation, and multi-session detection on every attempt. See [Proctoring Settings](/proctoring-settings).

<Note>
Equip has a self-serve public demo of ID verification and impersonation detection that runs without logging in. Ask the team for the demo link when you get in touch.
</Note>
