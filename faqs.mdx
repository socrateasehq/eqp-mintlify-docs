## Pricing and Credits

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does Equip cost?">
    Equip runs on subscription plans (**Standard**, **Premium**, or **Elite**), each with a monthly credit allowance and a seat limit. Credits are spent only when candidates actually take an assessment or an AI interview, and unused credits never expire while you're subscribed.

    | What you use | Cost |
    |---|---|
    | **ATS** (job posts, pipeline, careers page) | Free forever |
    | **Assessment** | 1 credit per candidate per test |
    | **One-way AI interview** (up to 20 questions) | 1 credit per candidate |
    | **One-way AI interview** (21 to 40 questions) | 2 credits per candidate |
    | **Conversational AI interview** | 2 credits (Quick) or 3 credits (Comprehensive) |
    | **Job Fit Score re-ranking** | 0.5 credits per candidate |
    | **Bulk CV import** | 0.01 credits per CV |

    1 credit costs **\$1**. See [How Credits Work](/how-credits-work) for the full billing model.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial or demo account?">
    There is no separate demo account. Every new team gets **10 free trial credits** on signup. Even without credits you can create unlimited assessments, invite unlimited teammates, and preview unlimited tests.

    To explore before signing up, open the [sample recruiter dashboard](https://equip.co/demo/recruiter/home/).

    <Note>
    Recruiter accounts require a **work email**. Personal domains such as gmail.com or outlook.com cannot create a team. See [Demo or Trial Account](/demo-or-trial-account) for details.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When are credits deducted?">
    Assessments cost **1 credit per test the candidate actually starts**. The first credit is deducted immediately when you invite the candidate, even if the email bounces or is never opened. Each later test deducts another credit when the candidate starts it.

    See [Credit Deduction Policy](/credit-deduction-policy) for a worked example.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if a candidate never attempts, or I invited them twice?">
    Use [**Reclaim Credits**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/reclaim-credits/). Any invitation sent more than 24 hours ago where the candidate has not started the first test is eligible.

    If you invite the same candidate twice, Equip warns you first. Proceeding deducts 2 credits, and you can reclaim the unused invitation after 24 hours.

## Assessments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I test multiple skills in one assessment?">
    Yes. An assessment is a sequence of **tests**, each targeting a skill, and a single quiz can also cover up to 6 skills on its own. See [Testing Multiple Skills](/testing-multiple-skills) and [Assessment Hierarchy](/assessment-hierarchy).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What test types are available?">
    Equip offers nine test types:

    | Test type | Graded by |
    |---|---|
    | **Quiz** (MCQ, multiple-correct, essay, and more) | Auto or AI |
    | **Programming** (17+ languages in a browser editor) | Auto |
    | **SQL** | Auto |
    | **Excel** (simulated spreadsheet) | Auto |
    | **Typing** | Auto |
    | **Communication (CEFR)** for Reading, Writing, Speaking, Listening | AI |
    | **Psychometric** (DISC, OCEAN/Big Five) | Unscored by design |
    | **Video Response** | Recruiter |
    | **CSS** (replicate a target design) | Recruiter |

    See [Test Types](/test-types) for details on each.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use my own questions?">
    Yes. Every test type offers **Equip Content** (pre-built library tests) and **My Content** (your own custom tests). You can author questions directly or bulk-import them from Excel.

    See [Equip vs Custom Content](/equip-vs-custom-content) to decide which fits your role.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does Equip prevent cheating?">
    Assessments run with [**AI proctoring**](/ai-proctoring), including face detection, tab-switch detection, session recording, and optional ID verification.

    Each completed attempt gets a [**Trust Score**](/trust-score) from 0 to 100% summarizing how reliable the session was.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I try an assessment before inviting candidates?">
    Yes, previews are **free and unlimited**, either as a recruiter or as a candidate. See [Preview an Assessment](/preview-assessment).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I archive an assessment?">
    Candidates see an archived assessment as closed, but **all results are retained** and you can un-archive it at any time. Assessments are never deleted.

    See [Archive an Assessment](/archive-assessment).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## AI Interviews

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between one-way and conversational interviews?">
    | | **One-Way** | **Conversational** |
    |---|---|---|
    | Format | Fixed questions, recorded video, audio, or text answers | Live spoken interview with an AI interviewer that asks follow-ups |
    | Questions | All visible at once, answered in any order | Adaptive, based on the candidate's answers |
    | Length | Single session | Quick (about 15 minutes) or Comprehensive (about 30 minutes) |
    | Cost | 1 credit per candidate | 2 credits (Quick) or 3 credits (Comprehensive) |
    | Best for | High-volume screening (30+ candidates) | Deeper evaluation with follow-up probing |

    See [One-Way vs Conversational](/one-way-vs-conversational) for guidance on choosing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are conversational interviews created?">
    Paste a job description (up to 7,500 characters) and Equip drafts the interview topics automatically. See [Create a Conversational Interview](/create-conversational-interview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What languages can the AI interviewer speak?">
    Conversational interviews run in the language you choose: English (US, India, UK), Hindi, Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali, Spanish (Spain, US), French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), or Italian.

    See [Interview Languages](/interview-languages).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can a candidate restart an interview?">
    You can optionally allow candidates to **restart an unfinished attempt**; only the submitted attempt is graded. Once a conversational interview is submitted, it cannot be resumed or retaken.

    See [Candidate Reattempts](/candidate-reattempts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do interview reports include?">
    Each candidate gets an AI-generated report with an **overall recommendation** and per-topic scores, plus the full transcript and a session recording. Reports can be downloaded as PDFs.

    See [Interview Reports](/interview-reports).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Candidates

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do candidates need to create an account?">
    No account setup is needed beyond **email verification**. Candidates open the emailed or shared link and sign in with a 6-digit email code that expires in 10 minutes.

    See [Candidate Login Methods](/candidate-login-methods).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can candidates take tests on a phone?">
    It depends on the test type:

    | Works on mobile | Desktop only |
    |---|---|
    | Quizzes, psychometric tests, CEFR communication tests, video responses | Programming, SQL, CSS, Excel, and typing tests |

    Google Chrome is recommended. Multiple-monitor detection works only on Chromium-based browsers, and video interviews on iOS require Safari. See [Device Compatibility](/device-compatibility).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if a candidate loses internet connection?">
    Assessments **resume after disconnects** or accidental tab closes wherever the test type allows it. Conversational AI interviews reconnect automatically with a grace window for brief drops, but cannot be resumed once submitted.

    See [Resume Your Attempt](/resume-your-attempt).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do candidates see their scores?">
    No. Results are **recruiter-gated**: candidates see a completion confirmation and are directed to contact the recruiter for outcomes. Equip does not provide candidate-facing scores, certificates, or badges.

    Recruiters can verify that an attempt went through in [Live Progress](/live-progress).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is candidate privacy protected?">
    Before starting, candidates see exactly which **proctoring measures** are active and consent to the proctoring report being shared with the recruiter.

    Candidates are also offered a skippable one-minute **demo test** to check that their device and camera work before the real attempt. See [Demo Tests](/demo-tests).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
