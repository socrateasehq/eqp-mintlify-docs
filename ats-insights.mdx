The [**Insights** page](https://equip.co/recruiter/job-openings/insights/) turns your ATS activity into charts automatically. Every application, stage change, scorecard, and offer your team records feeds the dashboard, so there is nothing to configure.

## The Five Sections

| Section | What it answers | Charts |
|---|---|---|
| **Key Metrics** | How is hiring going overall? | Active openings, Applications, In pipeline, Hires, Avg time to hire, Offer acceptance |
| **Pipeline & Funnel** | Where are candidates, and where do they drop off? | Application funnel, Pipeline snapshot, Stage drop-off, Pipeline-stage funnel |
| **Velocity** | How fast are we moving? | Time-to-hire trend, Avg time in stage, Time to offer, Offer turnaround |
| **Team** | Who is doing what, and how do they rate candidates? | Team performance, Avg scorecard by recruiter |
| **Quality & Source** | Are we attracting the right candidates? | Applications by source, Job-fit score distribution, Rejections by reason, Offer outcomes |

A few charts behave in ways worth knowing:

- Click any step of the **Application funnel** to drill down into the candidates behind the number.
- **In pipeline** reflects right now, so it ignores the selected date range.
- **Avg scorecard by recruiter** averages the [scorecards](/scorecards) each teammate has submitted, which helps you spot unusually harsh or lenient evaluators.

<Note>
Some KPIs need a minimum amount of data. Avg time to hire appears after your first hire, and Offer acceptance (signed offers divided by resolved offers) appears once at least 3 offers are resolved.
</Note>

## Filters, Date Ranges, and Grouping

The filter bar at the top of the page controls what every chart shows.

| Control | Options |
|---|---|
| **Date range** | Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days (default), Last 12 months, Custom range |
| **Scope** | Solo or Squad (see below) |
| **Job openings** | All openings, or only the specific openings you pick |

Individual charts have their own display controls on top of the global filters:

- **Group by**: break a chart down by Source, Job opening, Recruiter, Stage type, or Rejection reason. For example, group Applications by Source to see which channel fills your pipeline.
- **Trend granularity**: view trend charts Weekly or Monthly.

## Solo vs Squad Scope

The **Solo / Squad** toggle decides whose hiring activity the dashboard covers.

| Scope | What it includes |
|---|---|
| **Solo** | Only your own job openings and activity |
| **Squad** | You plus your direct and indirect reports |

Squad scope relies on your team's **reporting structure**, the manager-and-reportee mapping defined in your [organization settings](/organization-settings). Until managers and reportees are assigned, there is no squad to aggregate.
