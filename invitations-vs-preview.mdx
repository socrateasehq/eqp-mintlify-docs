tl;dr: To test an assessment or AI interview, **Preview** it for free, or **Invite** yourself or a teammate as a candidate, which costs credits.
</Tip>

## What Previews Are For

**Preview** lets you or anyone on your team walk through the assessment without spending credits, as many times as you like.

Choose **As a Recruiter** (proctoring off) or **As a Candidate** (the exact candidate flow). See [Preview an Assessment](/preview-assessment).

<Note>
Previews show **sample questions** for quizzes built from Equip's question bank, never the actual ones. See [Why can't I see quiz questions?](/cannot-see-quiz-questions)
</Note>

## What Invitations Are For

**Invitations** are the real thing. Invite yourself or a teammate when you need to verify end-to-end behavior: real questions, actual proctoring evidence, grading, and the final report.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite page">
    Click **Invite** under the assessment on your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    On the **Send Invitations** tab, enter your own or your teammate's email address. See [Email Invitations](/email-invitations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attempt the assessment">
    Open the unique link from the invitation email and complete the assessment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Invitations **cost credits**, even when the invitee is you or a team member. If you only need to check the flow or the questions, use Preview instead.
</Warning>

## Previewing and Inviting for AI Interviews

The [**AI Interviews** dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/) shows the same **Invite** and **Preview** links under each interview, and the same choice applies.

| | Preview | Invitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Credits** | Free | Per-candidate cost, deducted before emails go out |
| **Modes** | **As Recruiter** to see how the AI evaluates responses; **As Candidate** for the full candidate view | Real candidate attempt |
| **Questions shown** | The interview's own questions, no sample substitution | Actual questions |
| **Proctoring** | Off | Enabled |
| **Time window** | Ignores the interview's start time and deadline | Applies |

The per-candidate cost is in the dashboard's credits column.

<Note>
You must be logged in with your work email and be a team member to preview. For an interview that is a stage of a job opening, **Invite** opens on the **Track** tab, because candidates are invited from the pipeline.
</Note>

## Credit Differences

| | Preview | Invitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Credits** | Free | Deducted when the invitation is sent |
| **Repeatable** | Unlimited | Each invitation deducts credits again |
| **Questions shown** | Sample questions for Equip quizzes; real content otherwise | Actual questions |
| **Proctoring** | Disabled in recruiter mode; candidate mode asks for camera and screen access | Fully enabled |
| **Produces a result** | No | Yes, with scores and a proctoring report |

<Tip>
If you invited yourself just to test and never started the assessment, you can reclaim the credit once 24 hours have passed since the invitation. See [How Credits Work](/how-credits-work) for the full policy.
</Tip>
