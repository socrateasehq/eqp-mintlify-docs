title: "Invitations vs Previews"
description: "When to preview and when to invite, and what each costs."

<Tip>
tl;dr: **Invitations** are for candidates and cost credits. **Preview** is for you and your team members, and it is free.
</Tip>

Once you create an assessment, you will probably want to check that it works the way you expect. There are two ways to test it: **Preview** the assessment, or **Invite** yourself or a teammate as if you were a candidate.

If you only want to see how the assessment flows, always use Preview. It costs nothing, and you can run it any number of times.

{/* screenshot: Invite and Preview options shown under an assessment on the dashboard */}

## What Previews Are For

**Preview** lets you or anyone on your team walk through the assessment without spending credits. You can preview an assessment as many times as you like.

Previews come in two modes:

| Preview mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| **As a Recruiter** | Jump between tests freely. Proctoring measures such as camera tracking and screen sharing are disabled. |
| **As a Candidate** | The exact candidate flow: you grant camera and screen access, and you must finish each test before moving to the next. A "Previewing as a Candidate" banner reminds you that you are in Preview Mode. |

Use the recruiter mode for a quick sanity check of your tests and settings. Use the candidate mode when you want to experience the proctoring prompts and the flow exactly as a candidate would.

{/* screenshot: preview mode picker showing As a Recruiter and As a Candidate options */}

<Note>
Previews show **sample questions** for quizzes built from Equip's question bank. The samples are representative and matched by difficulty, but the actual quiz questions are never shown to recruiters, which protects the question bank. If the sample bank has fewer questions than you configured, the preview shows fewer, while candidates still see the full count.

Custom content and Equip's non-quiz tests appear in previews exactly as candidates see them. See [Why can't I see quiz questions?](/cannot-see-quiz-questions) for details.
</Note>

## What Invitations Are For

**Invitations** are the real thing. When you invite yourself or a teammate, the invitee goes through the full candidate experience: the actual questions are shown, proctoring is enabled, and the attempt produces a real result with scores and a proctoring report.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite page">
    Click **Invite** under the assessment on your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    On the **Send Invitations** tab, enter your own or your teammate's email address and send. Equip confirms how many credits will be deducted before you send. See [Email Invitations](/email-invitations) for all the sending options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attempt the assessment">
    Open the unique link from the invitation email and complete the assessment exactly as a candidate would.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Inviting yourself is useful when you need to verify end-to-end behavior: real questions, actual proctoring evidence, grading, and the final report. For everything else, Preview is enough.

<Warning>
Invitations **cost credits**, even when the invitee is you or a team member. If you only need to check the flow or the questions, use Preview instead.
</Warning>

## Credit Differences

Here is how the two options compare:

| | Preview | Invitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Credits** | Free | Deducted when the invitation is sent |
| **Repeatable** | Unlimited | Each invitation deducts credits again |
| **Questions shown** | Sample questions for Equip quizzes; real content otherwise | Actual questions |
| **Proctoring** | Disabled in recruiter mode; candidate mode asks for camera and screen access | Fully enabled |
| **Produces a result** | No | Yes, with scores and a proctoring report |

<Tip>
If you invited yourself just to test and never started the assessment, you can reclaim the credit once 24 hours have passed since the invitation. See [How Credits Work](/how-credits-work) for the full policy.
</Tip>
