If you have a folder of CVs from a job board, an agency, or an old ATS, or a spreadsheet of candidate details, you do not need to add candidates one by one. **Bulk Import** lets you upload up to 100 CVs or one CSV file in a single batch, and Equip turns each one into a job application automatically.

<PlanGate plan="Premium" />

## Starting a Bulk Import

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the job opening and click Bulk Import">
    In your [job opening dashboard](/candidates-and-cvs), open the job you want to add candidates to and click **Bulk Import**. Every file or row becomes an application in that job's pipeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose CVs or a CSV, and start the import">
    The same drop zone accepts either PDF CVs or a single CSV file, not both in the same batch.

    | | PDF CVs | CSV |
    |---|---|---|
    | Per batch | Up to 100 files, 10 MB each | 1 file, up to 200 rows |
    | Cost | 0.01 credit per CV | Free |
    | How it's read | Equip's AI parses each CV | Equip reads the columns you filled in |

    <Frame caption="The Bulk Import drop zone, accepting either PDF CVs or a CSV file">
      <img src="/images/bulk-cv-import/bulk-import-dropzone.png" width="760" alt="Bulk Import page for a job opening, with a drop zone reading Drop CVs or a CSV file here, a Choose files button, hints for PDF CVs (up to 100, 10 MB each, AI reads them, 0.01 credit per CV) and one CSV (up to 200 rows, free, with a Download template link), and a note that imported candidates start unverified" />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
    Click **Download template** to get a sample CSV with the expected columns filled in as examples. **First Name** and **Email** are the only required columns; the rest, including **Last Name**, **Phone**, **LinkedIn URL**, **Current City**, **Total Experience (years)**, **Notice Period (days)**, **Expected Salary Min/Max**, **Current Company**, **Current Job Title**, **Current Role Start (YYYY-MM)**, **Skills** (semicolon-separated), and **Notes**, are optional.
    </Tip>

    Confirm the upload. The batch is processed in the background, so you can leave the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Parsing and Costs

A PDF CV batch costs **0.01 credits per CV**, so a full batch of 100 CVs costs 1 credit. A CSV import is **free**, regardless of row count. The cost is checked against your team's credit balance before the batch starts.

Each CV goes through the same **AI parsing** as a direct application. See [CV Parsing](/cv-parsing) for the list of extracted fields. A CSV import skips parsing entirely: Equip reads the columns you filled in directly.

### Tracking your batch

The batch detail page shows the batch status and a result for each file. Every failed file shows a reason.

<Frame caption="A finished batch with its counts, where View on each card opens that list of files">
  <img src="/images/bulk-cv-import/batch-detail.png" width="776" alt="Bulk Import detail page for Full Stack Developer, import ID 1, marked Completed with 12 of 12 processed at 100 percent, cards showing 8 Success, 3 Failed and 1 Skipped, and a link to view applications for the 8 CVs processed successfully" />

<Note>
Common failure reasons are a missing email, a missing name, a [duplicate application](/duplicate-applications), or a CV the parser could not read. Fix the file or row, or resolve the duplicate, then re-upload just the affected CVs or rows in a new batch.
</Note>

## Unverified Candidates

Candidates added through Bulk Import, whether from a CV or a CSV row, never confirmed their email address themselves, so Equip marks them as **unverified**.

Unverified candidates receive **no stage-change emails**. You can still move them through the pipeline, but invitation and rejection emails are held back until the email is verified.

<Warning>
If you invite an unverified candidate to an assessment or AI interview stage, the invite dialog shows a warning and no email goes out. Verify the candidate's email first if you want them to receive the invitation.
</Warning>

<Tip>
To verify a candidate, open their profile and copy the **verification link** from the email popover, then share it with the candidate directly.
</Tip>

Each application imported from a CV records its **source** as "Bulk CV Upload", which the [ATS Insights](/ats-insights) dashboard reports on.
