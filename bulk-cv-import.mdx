title: "Bulk CV Import"
description: "Upload up to 100 CVs at once and let AI parse them into applications."
---

If you have a folder of CVs from a job board, an agency, or an old ATS, you do not need to add candidates one by one. **Bulk CV Import** lets you upload up to 100 CVs in a single batch. Equip parses each file with AI and creates a job application automatically.

## Uploading CVs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the job opening and click Bulk Import CVs">
    In your [job opening dashboard](/candidates-and-cvs), open the job you want to add candidates to and click **Bulk Import CVs**. Every parsed CV becomes an application in that job's pipeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your PDF files and start the import">
    The uploader accepts PDF files only, up to 100 files per batch and 10 MB per file. For more than 100 CVs, upload several batches.
    {/* screenshot: bulk CV upload dialog showing "Select PDF files to upload" with the "PDF only · Max 100 files · Max 10MB each" hint */}

    Confirm the upload. The batch is processed in the background, so you can leave the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Parsing and Costs

Bulk CV Import comes with the **Premium** plan and above, and costs **0.01 credits per CV**, so a full batch of 100 CVs costs 1 credit. The cost is checked against your team's credit balance before the batch starts.

Each CV goes through the same **AI parsing** as a direct application. See [CV Parsing](/cv-parsing) for the list of extracted fields.

### Tracking your batch

The batch detail page shows the batch status and a result for each file. Every failed file shows a reason.

{/* screenshot: batch detail page with progress counts and per-file success/failed/skipped lists */}

<Note>
Common failure reasons are a missing email, a missing name, a duplicate application for the same job, or a CV the parser could not read. Fix the file or resolve the duplicate, then re-upload just the affected CVs in a new batch.
</Note>

## Unverified Candidates

Candidates added through Bulk CV Import never confirmed their email address themselves, so Equip marks them as **unverified**.

Unverified candidates receive **no stage-change emails**. You can still move them through the pipeline, but invitation and rejection emails are held back until the email is verified.

<Warning>
If you invite an unverified candidate to an assessment or AI interview stage, the invite dialog shows a warning and no email goes out. Verify the candidate's email first if you want them to receive the invitation.
</Warning>

<Tip>
To verify a candidate, open their profile and copy the **verification link** from the email popover, then share it with the candidate directly.
</Tip>

Each imported application records its **source** as "Bulk CV Upload", which the [ATS Insights](/ats-insights) dashboard reports on.
