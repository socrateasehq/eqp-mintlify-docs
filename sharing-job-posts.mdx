Every job post on Equip has a single shareable **application link**. Candidates who apply through it land directly in your applications dashboard.

## The Application Link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Job Openings dashboard">
    Go to [**Job Openings**](https://equip.co/recruiter/job-openings/dashboard/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">
    **Invite** on the opening's row copies the application link to your clipboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link anywhere">
    Paste it wherever you reach candidates. Every application flows into the same pipeline, with the source **Direct Application**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The dashboard also tracks views and application counts per post.

<Tip>
Click **Preview** on the same row to see the post as candidates see it, without affecting your applications.
</Tip>

<Note>
Equip does not automatically publish your post to LinkedIn, Indeed, or any other job board. Paste the application link into those platforms yourself; see [Job Boards](/job-boards) for the recommended setup.
</Note>

## Job Post Statuses

A job post is always in one of three statuses. Only **Published** posts are visible to candidates, so check the status before you share the link.

| Status | Visible to candidates? | Accepting applications? |
|---|---|---|
| **Published** | Yes | Yes |
| **Hidden** | No, the public link returns a 404 page | No |
| **Archived** | Returns a "Position Closed" page | No |

Change the status from the dropdown on the [**Job Openings** dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/job-openings/dashboard/). Published posts also appear automatically on your Equip [careers page](/careers-page).

## Hiding and Archiving

Both statuses stop new applications. Use **Hidden** to pause a role temporarily or work on it privately, and **Archived** for roles that are filled or permanently closed.

<Warning>
If you shared the application link widely before hiding a post, candidates clicking it will hit a 404 page with no explanation. Prefer **Archived** for filled roles so late visitors understand the position existed but is closed.
</Warning>

Changing status never deletes anything. Your existing applications and pipeline history stay intact, and you can switch a post back to Published at any time.
