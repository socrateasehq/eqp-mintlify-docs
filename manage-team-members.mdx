title: "Adding and Removing Team Members and Admins"

---

A **team** on Equip shares one workspace: everyone sees the same job openings, candidates, results, and credit balance.

Your plan sets a **seat limit**: Standard includes 2 seats, Premium 5, and Elite is unlimited. Each team member you add counts against this limit.

## Inviting Members

Any team member can invite new teammates from the [**Team** page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team page">
    Go to [**Account > Team**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/) in the left sidebar.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/manage-team-members-open-team-page.png" alt="Account sidebar section with the Team link" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite members">
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/manage-team-members-invite-button.png" alt="Invite members button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter email addresses one by one">
    Press **Enter** after each email ID. Each invitee receives an email with a link to join your team.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/manage-team-members-invite-modal.png" alt="Invite members dialog with an email address entered" style={{maxWidth: "320px"}} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Invitations are restricted to your team's **email domain**, and Equip allows one team per domain. Check **Seats Remaining** on the [Billing page](/how-credits-work#subscribing-to-a-plan) before inviting, or upgrade your plan for more room.
</Note>

### Pending Invites

Invitations that have not been accepted yet appear under **Pending Invites** on the [Team page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/). Click **Remove invite** to cancel one.

## Joining via Email

Colleagues with your team's **email domain** don't always need an invite. They can sign in on the [login page](https://equip.co/account/login/role/recruiter/) with their work email, and Equip adds them straight to your existing team.

<Note>
To turn this off, go to [Account > Team](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/), open the [Organization tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/organization/), and set **Allow Auto-join** to No. Colleagues will then need an invite from the Team Admin.
</Note>

## Removing Members

Only the **Team Admin** can remove a member.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the actions menu">
    On the [Team page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/), click the **three dots** next to the member's name and choose Remove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm in the **Remove Team Member** dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The member actions menu">
  <img src="/images/manage-team-members-remove-option.png" alt="Team member actions menu with Make Admin and Remove options" style={{maxWidth: "290px"}} />
</Frame>

<Warning>
The Team Admin cannot be removed from the team. To remove the person who is currently the admin, transfer the admin role to someone else first, then have the new admin remove them.
</Warning>

If the admin is the only member, they invite a teammate first, then make that teammate the admin.

## The Team Admin Role

Every team has exactly **one Team Admin**. The person who creates the team becomes the admin by default.

All members see the same data and can do the same day-to-day work. The admin role only adds a few responsibilities:

| Action | Member | Team Admin |
|---|---|---|
| Create job openings, assessments, and AI interviews | Yes | Yes |
| Invite candidates and view results | Yes | Yes |
| Invite new team members | Yes | Yes |
| Remove team members | No | Yes |
| Transfer the admin role | No | Yes |
| Receive invoices and low-balance email alerts | No | Yes |

### Changing the Team Admin

Only the current admin can hand over the role.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team page">
    Go to [**Account > Team**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/) in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the new admin">
    Click the **three dots** next to the member who should become the admin and select Make Admin. You become a regular member.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The [Team page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/) also supports an optional reporting structure. A member can be assigned a **manager**, which Equip's analytics use to group activity.
</Note>
