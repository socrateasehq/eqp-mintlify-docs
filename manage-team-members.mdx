---
title: "Members and Seats"
description: "Invite recruiters to your organization, remove people who leave, and buy extra seats."
---

Everyone who works in Equip with you is a **member** of your organization, managed on the [**People** tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/organization/people/) of **Account > Organization**. Each member and each pending invite uses one **seat**. See [Roles and Permissions](/roles-and-permissions) for who can invite and remove people.

## Invite Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Invite members">
    On the People tab, click **Invite members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter email addresses and send">
    Add one or more work email addresses and click **Send Invite**. If you use [departments](/departments), pick one, or leave the invite org-wide so the person sees every job opening.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Invites must use one of your organization's **email domains**; see [Organization Settings](/organization-settings). For any other address, click **Request an exception** in the dialog.
</Note>

Invites that have not been accepted yet appear under **Pending invites**. Click the bin icon on a row to cancel one and free its seat.

## Seats

The **Seats** section on the People tab shows how many seats are in use. The Owner clicks **Buy or remove seats** to add extra seats or remove them; removals apply at your next renewal. See [Compare Plans](/compare-plans) for the seats each plan includes and the price of extra seats.

<Warning>
If your organization has more members than seats, for example after a downgrade, access is blocked until you remove members or buy seats.
</Warning>

## Joining Without an Invite

Colleagues on your email domain can sign in at the [login page](https://equip.co/account/login/role/recruiter/) with their work email and join your organization directly. To require an invite instead, set **Allow auto-join** to No on the [Company tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/organization/company/).

## Remove a Member

On the People tab, click the **three dots** on the member's row and choose **Remove**.

<Note>
Removing a member frees a seat but does not lower your bill. To pay for fewer seats, also remove the extra seat.
</Note>
