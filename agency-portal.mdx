This article is for recruiters at an agency that a company on Equip works with. The **agency portal** shows the jobs that company gave your agency, and is where you submit candidates and follow how they are doing.

## Submit Candidates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to the agency portal">
    Open the link in your invitation email, or go to the [agency portal](https://equip.co/portal/agency/). Sign in with the email address you were invited with; Equip sends you a one-time code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the company and the job">
    If you recruit for more than one company on Equip, choose the company first. Then click **Submit candidates** on a job.
    {/* screenshot: company page in the agency portal listing assigned jobs */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload CVs or a spreadsheet">
    Under **Submit candidates**, choose **PDF CVs** or **Spreadsheet (CSV)**, then upload your file or files.
    {/* screenshot: agency job page with About the job, Submit candidates and Your candidates */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Method | Limit | Required |
|---|---|---|
| **PDF CVs** | Up to 100 files at a time, 10 MB each | A PDF per candidate; Equip reads each CV |
| **Spreadsheet (CSV)** | Up to 100 candidates per file | First name and email; use **Download template** for the other columns |

A spreadsheet is checked before anything is imported. Rows with errors are listed and left out; fix them in the file and upload it again.

<Note>
Anyone already in the job's pipeline is skipped, and the portal never says who brought them. The company's terms decide how long your submission holds a candidate.
</Note>

## Follow Your Candidates

Each job page lists **Your candidates**, with who on your team submitted each one. Progress shows as a simple stage, not the company's internal pipeline.

| Progress | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Submitted** | Received, not yet reviewed |
| **In review** | In screening, an assessment or an AI interview |
| **Interviewing** | In live interviews |
| **Offered** | Has an offer |
| **Hired** | Hired |
| **Not progressed** | Rejected or withdrew |

The job page also shows the job's public details, any **Notes from** the company, and the fee agreed for that job.

## When You Can't Submit

The **Submit candidates** section says why when a job is closed to you.

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| Submissions paused | The company paused submissions for this job; you can still see your candidates |
| No longer assigned | The job was removed from your agency's list; your past candidates stay visible |
| Not accepting applications | The job itself is closed |
| Can't accept CV uploads right now | The company's account cannot process CVs; contact the person named in the message |

<Warning>
If the company archives your agency, you can no longer sign in to its portal. Contact the company directly.
</Warning>
