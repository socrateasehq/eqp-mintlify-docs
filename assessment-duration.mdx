## How Duration Is Calculated

Every question in Equip's question bank has a timer. Equip adds up these timers across all tests and shows the **Estimated Assessment Duration** in the Test Order tab of the assessment builder.

<Note>
There is no **assessment-level time limit**. Candidates can take breaks between tests, so the exact end time of an assessment cannot be controlled. Timers apply per test or per question, depending on the [test type](/time-limits#time-limits-by-test-type). See [Time Limits](/time-limits) for details.
</Note>

<Warning>
If you add a **Custom Test** to your assessment without setting a timer for every question, the Estimated Assessment Duration may not match how long candidates actually take.
</Warning>

## Planning Assessment Length

Some tests have predictable durations:

| Test | Typical duration |
|---|---|
| DISC Psychometric | 24 questions, about 12 minutes |
| OCEAN (Big Five) Psychometric | 50 questions, about 25 minutes |
| English Communication (CEFR) | • Fixed-duration: 1 set, about 15 minutes<br />• Adaptive: 2 to 6 sets, about 45 minutes |

For quizzes, duration scales with the number of questions you configure per skill. See [Question Limits](/question-limits) for the caps per test type.

<Note>
The **start window** in [Assessment Settings](/assessment-settings) controls when candidates may begin, not how long they get once they start.
</Note>

## Changing Test Durations

Timers on **Equip Content** are fixed and cannot be edited. Timers on **Custom Tests** are yours to set, and you can mix both in the same assessment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assessment builder">
    From the Recruiter Dashboard, open your assessment's settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or remove content in the Tests tab">
    With Equip Content, change the duration by adding or removing tests, or by adjusting the number of questions per skill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit timers on Custom Tests">
    Set the timer on each question, or on the whole test for all-at-once quizzes. Saved changes apply to candidates who attempt after the change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the new estimate">
    Open the **Test Order** tab to see the updated Estimated Assessment Duration.
  </Step>
</Steps>
