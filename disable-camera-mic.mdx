---
title: "Disabling Camera and Mic Tracking"
description: "Turn off camera and microphone proctoring on any test when you don't need it."
---

## When to Disable

Camera and mic tracking is not always necessary, for example on low-stakes screens or when candidates are uneasy granting camera access.

<Note>
Equip's **Psychometric** tests already keep camera and mic tracking off by default, and video response tests keep the camera options off by default.
</Note>

## How to Disable

Camera and mic tracking can be turned off on **any test**, whether it uses Equip's content or your own.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assessment's settings">
    Open the assessment's settings and select the **Test Order** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the test's proctoring settings">
    Click **Edit Settings** on the test you want to change.
    {/* screenshot: proctoring settings panel with camera and mic toggles highlighted */}

    <Frame caption="The proctoring settings for one test, with the camera and mic options switched off">
      <img src="/images/disable-camera-mic/camera-mic-toggles-off.png" width="692" alt="Proctoring Settings for Quiz dialog with Enable All and Disable All links and a list of toggles. Detect Audio, Detect Face, Detect Suspicious Objects, Take Random Photos and Capture Photo Before Start Test are unchecked, while Detect Switched Tab, Switched Tab Screenshot, Prevent Multiple Monitors and Enforce Full Screen stay checked, with the list scrolling under a footer of Cancel and Save Settings buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off the camera and mic toggles">
    Switch off **Detect Audio**, **Detect Face**, **Take Random Photos**, and **Capture Photo Before Start Test**. Click **Save Settings**, then **Update**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
These settings apply per test, not per assessment. If your assessment has multiple tests, repeat the steps for each test where you want camera and mic tracking off.
</Warning>

## Impact on the Proctoring Report

The **Proctoring** tab of the candidate report will not contain face-detection photos, random photos, a pre-test photo, or audio recordings for that test, because that evidence is never captured. Everything else you left enabled keeps working.

<Note>
The report still shows a **Trust Score** for the attempt. With fewer signals being tracked, review the score together with the remaining evidence rather than in isolation.
</Note>
