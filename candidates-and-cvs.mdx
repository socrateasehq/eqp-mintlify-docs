Every job opening in Equip has an **applications dashboard** that lists all of its candidates. From this one screen you can review profiles, open CVs, filter and sort applicants, and act on many candidates at once.

## Table and Kanban Views

The dashboard offers two views, and you can switch between them with the **view toggle** in the filter bar.

| View | Best For | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Table** | Scanning and comparing many candidates | One row per candidate with sortable columns. Defaults to the **Relevant** filter preset. |
| **Kanban** | Visualizing your pipeline | One column per stage, one card per candidate. Cards and columns are collapsible. Defaults to the **All** filter preset. |

Both views show each candidate's **Job Fit Score**, a 0 to 100 score with a one-line AI explanation of how well they match the role.

<!-- screenshot: applications dashboard with the Table/Kanban view toggle highlighted -->

<Tip>
Use Kanban when you want a quick sense of where candidates are stuck, and Table when you are shortlisting and need to compare fields side by side.
</Tip>

## Filter Presets

**Filter presets** are one-click filters in the filter bar that narrow the list to the candidates you care about right now.

| Preset | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every candidate in the pipeline |
| **Relevant** | Everyone except candidates in Hired, Rejected, or Withdrawn stages |
| **Mine** | Candidates assigned to you |
| **Unassigned** | Candidates with no assignee yet |
| **Starred** | Candidates you or your team have starred |
| **Recent** | Candidates who applied in the last 7 days |

Beyond presets, you can sort the list by **Fit Score**, **Max Salary**, **Min Salary**, **Notice Period**, **Experience**, or **Applied Date**.

<Note>
Each candidate also shows an **Action pending on** marker: Team, Candidate, or Both. It tells you at a glance whether the next move is yours or theirs.
</Note>

## Columns

Table columns cover the details recruiters compare most often: the candidate (with a LinkedIn link where available), pipeline stage, fit score, experience, industries, current role, education, expected salary, notice period, languages, and applied date.

Two more attributes appear alongside the stage:

| Attribute | Values | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **Candidate status** | Applied, Maybe, Shortlisted, Rejected, Withdrawn | Your verdict on the candidate, independent of which pipeline stage they are in |
| **Source** | Direct Application, Bulk CV Upload, Equip Sourcing Platform, Recruitment Agency, ATS Import | How the candidate entered your pipeline |

You can also **star** any candidate from their row to bookmark them for the Starred preset. Each application additionally carries an **assignee** and per-candidate **recruiter notes**, so your team always knows who owns the next step.

## Viewing CVs

Every candidate's CV lives on their profile, alongside the structured details Equip parsed from it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate">
    Click a candidate in the table or on a Kanban card to open their detail panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Candidate Details">
    The **Candidate Details** tab shows parsed information such as Preferences, Experience, and Education.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Attachments section">
    Find the CV under **Attachments**. Candidates can have up to 3 attachments of 10 MB each, uploaded by either the recruiter or the candidate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a candidate sends you an updated resume, you can replace the one on file. Replacements must be **PDF, DOC, or DOCX** files of at most 10 MB, and the re-upload is logged in the candidate's journey.

<Note>
Exporting the dashboard to CSV or Excel includes a resume URL column, so your export always links back to each candidate's CV.
</Note>

## Bulk Actions

Select multiple candidates in either view to act on all of them at once.

| Action | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Change stage** | Moves the selected candidates to another pipeline stage, with a contextual dialog for invitations or rejections |
| **Move to Job** | Transfers the applications to another job post; they land in the first stage of that pipeline and keep their fit score |
| **Star / Unstar** | Bookmarks or unbookmarks all selected candidates |
| **Delete** | Removes the selected candidates |

<!-- screenshot: bulk action bar shown with multiple candidates selected -->

You can also export the full list to **CSV or Excel**. The export includes contact details, stage, fit score with its AI reason, salary expectations, resume URL, recruiter notes, and starred status, plus one column per custom field and screening question.

<Warning>
Deleting candidates removes their applications from the job opening. If you only want them out of your active view, move them to a terminal stage like Rejected instead; the Relevant preset then hides them automatically.
</Warning>
