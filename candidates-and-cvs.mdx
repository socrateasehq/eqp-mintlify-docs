Every job opening in Equip has an **applications dashboard** that lists all of its candidates.

## Table and Kanban Views

Switch between the two views with the **view toggle** in the filter bar.

| View | Best For | Default Preset |
|---|---|---|
| **Table** | Scanning and comparing many candidates | Relevant |
| **Kanban** | Visualizing your pipeline, one column per stage | All |

Both views show each candidate's [Job Fit Score](/job-fit-score).

## Filter Presets

**Filter presets** are one-click filters in the filter bar: All, Relevant, Mine, Unassigned, Starred, and Recent. Relevant hides candidates in Hired, Rejected, or Withdrawn stages, and Recent shows those who applied in the last 7 days.

You can also sort the list by Fit Score, Max Salary, Min Salary, Notice Period, Experience, or Applied Date.

<Note>
Each candidate also shows an **Action pending on** marker: Team, Candidate, or Both.
</Note>

## Columns

Alongside the pipeline stage, each candidate carries a status:

| Attribute | Values | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **Candidate status** | Applied, Maybe, Shortlisted, Rejected, Withdrawn | Your verdict on the candidate, independent of which pipeline stage they are in |

You can also **star** any candidate from their row to bookmark them for the Starred preset. Each application additionally carries an **assignee** and per-candidate recruiter notes.

## Viewing CVs

Every candidate's CV lives on their profile, alongside the structured details Equip parsed from it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate">
    Click a candidate in the table or on a Kanban card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the CV from Candidate Details">
    On the **Candidate Details** tab, find the CV under Attachments and click **View Resume**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To replace the resume on file with an updated one, see [Editing Contact Information](/candidate-profile#candidate-details-tab).

## Bulk Actions

Select multiple candidates in either view to act on all of them at once.

| Action | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Change stage** | Moves the selected candidates to another pipeline stage |
| **Move to Job** | Transfers the applications to another job post; they land in the first stage of that pipeline and keep their fit score |
| **Delete** | Removes the selected candidates |

You can also export the full list to **CSV** and choose which details to include, such as fit score, resume URL, recruiter notes, custom fields, and screening questions.

<Warning>
Deleting candidates removes their applications from the job opening. If you only want them out of your active view, move them to a terminal stage like Rejected instead; the Relevant preset then hides them automatically.
</Warning>
