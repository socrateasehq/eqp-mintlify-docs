Depending on the stage you pick, Equip shows a simple move, a rejection flow, or an invitation flow with an email preview.

<Note>
When you move a candidate yourself, no email goes out without your say. Emails are sent on their own only if you set up [Stage Automation](/stage-automation) rules.
</Note>

## Moving Candidates Between Stages

You can change stages from the applications dashboard in both **Table** and **Kanban** views, for one candidate or many at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the candidates">
    Open a candidate, or select several in the table to use the bulk **change stage** action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the target stage">
    Choose it from the stage dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    For invitation stages, review the email preview, set an optional Reply-To and schedule, then send. For a simple move, just confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The dialog depends on the target stage:

| Target stage | Dialog you see |
|---|---|
| Phone Screening, Hired, Withdrawn, other stages without an email step | Simple move, no email |
| Rejected | Rejection reason and optional rejection email |
| Assessment, One-Way AI Interview, Conversational AI Interview | Invitation with email preview and a credit notice |
| Live Interview | Interview slot and panel, no credits. See [Scheduling Live Interviews](/scheduling-live-interviews). |
| Custom | Invitation with email preview, no credits |
| Offered | Offer flow when the stage has an offer packet, otherwise a simple move. See [Sending and Tracking Offers](/sending-offers). |

Assessment and AI interview invitations consume **credits**. The dialog states the cost up front, and credits are reclaimable if the invitation is not attempted. See [How Credits Work](/how-credits-work) for prices.

<Note>
Assessments with more than 2 tests can carry an extra per-test deduction. The invitation dialog spells out the exact policy before you send.
</Note>

<Warning>
If your team's balance cannot cover the invitations, the move is blocked with "Insufficient credits (need X, have Y)". Top up your balance and try again.
</Warning>

## Rejections and Statuses

Moving candidates to the **Rejected** stage opens the **Reject candidate(s)** dialog. Pick a rejection reason from the dropdown; these reasons power the "Rejections by reason" chart in [ATS Insights](/ats-insights).

{/* screenshot: Reject candidate(s) dialog with reason dropdown and email checkbox */}

The **Send rejection email to candidates** checkbox is off by default. Tick it only when you want the rejection email to go out.

<Tip>
Use **status** as a lightweight triage layer: mark a candidate Shortlisted or Maybe at any stage, without touching the pipeline. See [Pipeline Stages](/pipeline-stages) for how stage and status differ.
</Tip>

## The Email That Gets Sent

Invitation and rejection dialogs render the email from your **email templates**, filled in with the candidate's details, and show a preview before anything is sent. Each stage can have its own template attached in the pipeline builder.

{/* screenshot: invitation dialog showing the rendered email preview, Reply-To and Schedule Send */}

To change the wording, edit the template rather than the individual email. See [Email Templates](/email-templates).

<Warning>
Candidates with an **unverified email** trigger a warning in the invite dialog and receive no stage-change emails until their email address is verified. This applies to bulk-imported candidates in particular; you can copy a verification link from their profile to share with them.
</Warning>

Every stage change and email is logged in the **Candidate Journey** tab.

## Reply-To and Scheduling

**Reply-To** sets who receives candidate replies, and defaults to you. If no Reply-To is set, the email is sent from **no-reply@equip.co** and replies go nowhere.

**Schedule Send** delays delivery by minutes, hours or days, up to **7 days** in advance.

<Tip>
Schedule late-night rejection emails for the next morning so they arrive during business hours.
</Tip>
