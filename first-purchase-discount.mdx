<Tip>
Buy credits within **7 days** of creating your team and get **50% off** your first purchase. Miss that window and you still get **25% off** for the next 2 weeks. The discount is applied automatically at checkout.
</Tip>

## Discount Tiers

Equip offers a one-time discount on your team's **first credit purchase**. The size of the discount depends on how quickly you buy after creating your team.

| Offer | When it applies | Discount cap |
|---|---|---|
| **50% off** | First purchase made within 7 days of team creation | $60 (₹5,000 for Indian customers) |
| **25% off** | First purchase made within 21 days of team creation, after the 50% window ends | $60 (₹5,000 for Indian customers) |

The two offers cannot be combined. If you use the 50% offer, the 25% offer never appears; if you are seeing the 25% offer, the 50% window has already expired.

<Note>
Equip sends reminder emails before each offer expires, so you will not lose the discount without warning.
</Note>

## Eligibility and Caps

The discount is only for teams that have **never purchased credits** before. It applies once, to a single purchase, and cannot be saved for later: whichever purchase happens first is the one it is applied to.

The quickest way to check your eligibility is the **countdown timer** in your dashboard navbar. If you see a timer counting down to the offer's expiry, you are eligible. No timer means you are not.

<!-- screenshot: dashboard navbar showing the first purchase discount countdown timer -->

A few limits to keep in mind:

- The **minimum purchase** of 10 credits ($10 / ₹900) still applies.
- The discount is capped at **$60** (or **₹5,000** in India), so very large first purchases are discounted up to that amount rather than a full 50% or 25%.
- Because the discount can only be used once, avoid spending it on a small trial purchase. Once used, it will not be offered again.

<Warning>
The discount is auto-applied to your very first payment. You cannot make a small test purchase first and then use the discount on a bigger purchase later.
</Warning>

## How the Discount Is Applied

You do not need a promo code or any extra steps. If your team is eligible, checkout applies a coupon named `FIRSTPURCHASE50` or `FIRSTPURCHASE25` automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Purchase Credits page">
    Go to **Purchase Credits** from your dashboard and enter the number of credits you want to buy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the discounted amount">
    The page shows the cost without the discount, the discount amount, and the net cost, so you can confirm the exact saving before paying.
    <!-- screenshot: Purchase Credits page showing cost with first purchase discount applied -->
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the payment">
    Pay as usual. Your credit balance updates automatically, and the invoice reflects the discounted amount.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Only one discount applies per purchase. If your order also qualifies for a **volume discount**, Equip applies whichever discount saves you more money, and the Purchase Credits page tells you when the volume discount has replaced the first purchase discount. For Indian customers, 18% GST is added on the discounted cost.
</Note>

## Fair Use and Duplicate Accounts

The discount exists to encourage companies that have **never used Equip** to make their first payment. It is not automatically owed to every new account, even when the website displays it.

If the same company, or the same or related individuals, create additional accounts with different emails or domains to claim the discount again, Equip revokes the discounted credits after the purchase. As a rule of thumb: if you have already used Equip on another account, whether or not you redeemed the discount there, the new account is not entitled to it.

<Warning>
Discounts claimed through duplicate or related accounts are cancelled retroactively. In any dispute about eligibility, the decision of the Equip team is final and binding.
</Warning>
