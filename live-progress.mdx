If you are running a large-scale assessment in a short window, the **live progress view** shows how your candidates are doing in real time.

## The Live View

The view shows **overall progress** across your candidate pool and completion for each test inside the assessment. You can also set the **time window**, which is useful when you only care about today's drive rather than the assessment's full history.

## Statuses You Will See

Candidates attempt the tests in an assessment **one by one**, and they can take breaks between tests. During a drive, a candidate is therefore not yet started, attempting a test, between tests, or completed.

Once a candidate completes every test, their scores and [Trust Score](/trust-score) appear on the results page.

<Note>
Candidates who are partway through do not appear on the main results page by default. Turn on the **Show Unfinished** filter to include them. See [Missing Results](/missing-results) for other reasons a candidate may not be listed.
</Note>

## Tracking Credit Usage

Equip deducts **1 credit per test** a candidate starts. The first credit is deducted when you invite the candidate, and each further credit as they start the next test.

The live view shows how many candidates have reached each test, so you can calculate the credits deducted for an assessment.

<Tip>
Invitations that were never started can be [reclaimed for credit](/reclaim-credits).
</Tip>
