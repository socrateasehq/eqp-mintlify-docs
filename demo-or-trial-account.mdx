<Tip>
Equip has no separate demo or trial account. Every new team gets a real account with **10 free credits**, enough to assess up to 10 candidates.
</Tip>

## Free Trial Credits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up with your work email">
    Create your account at [equip.co](https://equip.co/account/recruiter-onboarding/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore for free">
    Build assessments, invite teammates, preview tests with sample content, and use the ATS. None of this consumes credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assess up to 10 candidates">
    Your **10 free trial credits** cover real candidate attempts at 1 credit per candidate per test, so you see actual reports and proctoring results before spending anything. See [How Credits Work](/how-credits-work) for what each action costs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Recruiter accounts require a **work email address**, and each email domain gets exactly one team. Personal domains such as gmail.com or outlook.com cannot create an account, and a second signup from your company domain joins the existing team instead of creating a fresh one.
</Note>

When you are done experimenting, **archive** the practice assessments and continue with the same team and remaining credits.

## Booking a Walkthrough

Book a slot at [equip.co/schedule-demo](https://equip.co/schedule-demo/). Equip also runs **weekly webinars**: a 30-minute product demo followed by 30 minutes of open Q&A with the team.

To join a webinar or arrange a team walkthrough, reach out:

- **Live chat** in the product or on [equip.co](https://equip.co), answered within minutes
- **Email** at hello@equip.co, answered within 24 hours

<Note>
Equip is fully self-serve. The walkthrough is optional and free.
</Note>
