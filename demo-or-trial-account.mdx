<Tip>
Equip has no separate demo or trial account. Every new team gets a real account with **10 free credits**, enough to assess up to 10 candidates. Explore everything, and buy credits only when you are ready.
</Tip>

## Free Trial Credits

Signing up creates your team's one and only account. There is no sandbox to outgrow: the account you experiment with is the account you keep using.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up with your work email">
    Create your account at [equip.co](https://equip.co/account/recruiter-onboarding/). Signup is passwordless and takes under a minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore for free">
    Build assessments, invite teammates, and preview tests with sample content. None of this consumes credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assess up to 10 candidates">
    Your **10 free trial credits** cover real candidate attempts, so you can see actual reports and proctoring results before spending anything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Here is what the free credits do and do not gate:

| Activity | Cost |
|---|---|
| Creating assessments (unlimited) | Free |
| Inviting teammates (unlimited) | Free |
| Previewing tests with sample content | Free |
| Using the ATS (job posts, pipeline, CVs) | Free |
| Candidate assessment attempts | 1 credit per candidate per test |

<Note>
Recruiter accounts require a **work email address**. Personal domains such as gmail.com or outlook.com cannot create an account.
</Note>

When your trial experiments are done, simply **archive** the practice assessments. Your account is instantly clean, and you continue with the same team, teammates, and remaining credits. Credits you purchase later never expire.

## Booking a Walkthrough

Prefer a guided tour? Equip runs **weekly webinars**: a 30-minute product demo followed by 30 minutes of open Q&A with the team.

To join one, or to arrange a walkthrough for your team, reach out through either channel:

- **Live chat** in the product or on [equip.co](https://equip.co), answered within minutes
- **Email** at hello@equip.co, answered within 24 hours

<Note>
Equip is fully self-serve, so you never need a sales call to start. The walkthrough is optional and free.
</Note>
