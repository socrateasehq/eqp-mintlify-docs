Equip is built on a simple principle: candidates should always know how they are being monitored, and recruiters should be able to trust that assessment content and candidate data stay protected.

## Candidate Consent

Equip never proctors a candidate silently. Before an assessment or AI interview begins, every candidate sees exactly which **proctoring measures** are active for their attempt, such as camera monitoring, audio detection, tab-switch tracking, or session recording.

You can turn individual measures off, including camera and microphone access, in the assessment's proctoring settings.

## Session Recording Privacy

When **Record User Session** is enabled, Equip records the candidate's screen and interactions (mouse and keyboard) for playback in the candidate report.

| Safeguard | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Disclosed upfront** | Session recording appears in the list of active measures the candidate consents to before starting. |
| **Question content is masked** | Recordings mask the question content on screen, so leaked footage cannot expose the question bank. |
| **Recruiter-gated access** | Recordings are available only inside the candidate's report on the recruiter dashboard, not to candidates or the public. |

{/* screenshot: Session Recording tab inside a candidate report */}

<Note>
Candidates do not receive scores, reports, or recordings from Equip; they are directed to contact the recruiter for outcomes.
</Note>

## Data Protection

### Account Access

Recruiter accounts require a **work email address**. Signups from personal domains such as gmail.com or outlook.com are rejected, which keeps candidate data and question content inside verified companies.

Login is **passwordless**: recruiters sign in with a 6-digit code sent to their email, and each code expires in 10 minutes.

<Tip>
Each email domain maps to exactly one team, so colleagues join your existing team instead of creating parallel accounts with separate copies of candidate data.
</Tip>

### Candidate Data Deletion

Equip supports permanent deletion of a candidate's data. Deletion removes the candidate's test attempts, job applications, email records, and personal information, and it propagates to the systems that store attempt content.

If a candidate asks you to erase their data, or you need a deletion for your own retention policy, contact Equip over chat or at hello@equip.co with the candidate's email address.

<Warning>
Deletion is permanent. Reports, scores, and proctoring evidence for the deleted candidate cannot be recovered, so download anything you need first.
</Warning>

### Question Content Security

Equip protects its question bank in layers:

- **Access is gated.** Real questions are visible only to recruiter accounts or to candidates invited to a specific assessment. Only sample content is public, and search engines cannot index real questions.
- **Quiz questions are hidden from everyone.** Recruiters cannot see Equip's quiz questions during assessment creation or in candidate reports. Questions are also picked and ordered randomly per candidate, so two candidates starting together rarely see the same questions.
- **Coding questions rely on pool size.** Recruiters can view coding questions, and the large pool across languages makes memorizing them impractical.
- **Questions are refreshed.** Overexposed questions are retired so new candidates never see them.
- **Suspicious accounts are blocked.** Recruiter accounts showing suspicious activity, such as harvesting questions, are shut down.

## Compliance

Every customer is covered by the same standard legal framework. See [Custom Service Agreements (SLAs)](/custom-slas) for what can be customized.

| Your annual contract value | What applies |
|---|---|
| **Under \$25,000** | Standard Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and public pricing. No custom MSAs, SLAs, DPAs, or bespoke security questionnaires. |
| **\$25,000 or more** | Custom paperwork is considered, subject to review. |

For questions about Equip's security practices or documentation, reach out over chat or email hello@equip.co.
