description: "How to get your Equip job posts in front of candidates on LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri, and other job boards."
---

<Tip>
tl;dr: None. Equip does not automatically publish to any job board. Instead, copy your job post's **Application Link** and paste it on any job board yourself. Candidates who click it land on your Equip job post and apply there.
</Tip>

## Job Board Publishing

There is no automatic push to LinkedIn, Indeed, Monster, Naukri, or any other board. Instead, every Published job post has a public **Application Link** that you can share anywhere.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the Application Link">
    Open your [**Job Openings** dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/job-openings/dashboard/) and click **Invite** on the job you want to share. The copied link looks like `equip.co/job-posts/...`.
    {/* screenshot: Job Openings dashboard row showing the Invite, Preview, and Settings links */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the link on your job boards">
    Post the link on any board, or share it over email or WhatsApp. Click **Preview** first to see what candidates will see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Only **Published** job posts are visible to candidates. If the job is Hidden or Archived, the Application Link will not accept new applications.
</Note>

### LinkedIn External Apply

While Equip has no LinkedIn integration, LinkedIn's own **External Apply** option gets you the same result.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the job on LinkedIn">
    Post the job on LinkedIn as you normally would.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Equip Application Link">
    In LinkedIn's **Manage applicants** settings, choose to receive applicants at an external site and paste the Application Link. Candidates who click Apply on LinkedIn are redirected to your job post on Equip.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: LinkedIn job creation screen with the external apply URL field in Manage applicants settings */}

### Your Equip Careers Page

Equip also generates a hosted **Careers Page** listing all your Published job posts.

<Note>
You can link to the Careers Page from your website or embed it there, so it acts as a job board of its own. See [Careers Page](/careers-page) for setup.
</Note>

## Application Sources

Every application records a **source**:

| Source | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Direct Application** | The candidate applied themselves through your Application Link, wherever you shared it. |
| **Bulk CV Upload** | You imported the candidate's CV through [Bulk CV Import](/bulk-cv-import). |
| **Recruitment Agency** | The candidate was submitted by a recruitment agency. |

<Note>
Applications from all job boards share the **Direct Application** source, because candidates apply through the same link. Equip does not break down which specific board a candidate clicked from.
</Note>

To compare sources over time, open [**Insights**](https://equip.co/recruiter/job-openings/insights/) and check the **Applications by source** chart. See [ATS Insights](/ats-insights) for more.
