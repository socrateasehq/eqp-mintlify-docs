A **job requisition** is a request for headcount. Hiring managers raise one, a recruiter approves it, and the job opening is created from the approved request, so every opening traces back to a decision.

<PlanGate plan="Premium" />

## Add Your Hiring Team

Hiring managers raise requisitions without a recruiter seat. They never see candidates.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open People">
    Go to [**Account > Organization > People**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/organization/people/) and scroll to **Hiring team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add people by email">
    Each person gets an email with a link to a portal where they raise requisitions and follow their progress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review and Approve Requisitions

Open **Requisitions** from the [Job Openings dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/job-openings/dashboard/). Recruiters can also raise a requisition themselves with **+ Job Requisition**.

| Status | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | **Approve**, or **Reject** with an optional note that is emailed to the requester |
| **Approved** | **Create Job** to open the job opening, or **Close** |
| **Open** | **Mark filled** once you have hired, or **Close** |

Any recruiter can approve a requisition they can see. The person who raised it can edit or withdraw it while it is still Pending.
