Every job opening in Equip has its own **candidate pipeline**, built from stages you choose and order. You set up the pipeline in step 3 of the job creation wizard, labelled **Candidate Pipeline**, and you can edit it later from the same tab in the job's Settings.

<Tip>
There are **11 stage types**. Applied always comes first, Offered and the terminal stages (Hired, Rejected, Withdrawn) are managed by Equip, and the rest are yours to add, rename, and reorder by dragging.
</Tip>

## The 11 Stage Types

{/* screenshot: pipeline builder showing the add-stage cards with all stage types */}

<Frame caption="The stage types you can add to a pipeline">
  <img src="/images/pipeline-stages/add-stage-menu.png" alt="Add stage menu open in the pipeline builder, listing Phone Screening, Assessment, One-Way AI Interview, Conversational AI Interview, Video Interview, and Custom, each with a short description" />
</Frame>

| Stage | Kind | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Applied** | System | Every candidate starts here when they apply. Always the first stage. |
| **Phone Screening** | Configurable | Call candidates, then rank and review them. |
| **Assessment** | Configurable | Invite candidates to a skill test: quiz, coding, communication, and more. |
| **One-Way AI Interview** | Configurable | Fixed questions. Candidates record subjective responses via text, audio, or video. |
| **Conversational AI Interview** | Configurable | An AI interviewer auto-generates questions and engages in a back-and-forth conversation. |
| **Live Interview** | Configurable | Hold a video, phone, or in-person round, then schedule, score, and review candidates. |
| **Custom** | Configurable | Any step that does not fit the above, with optional ranking. |
| **Offered** | System | Supports sending an offer email and documents for the candidate to sign. |
| **Hired** | Terminal | The candidate accepted and joined. |
| **Rejected** | Terminal | You turned the candidate down. |
| **Withdrawn** | Terminal | The candidate dropped out of the process. |

**Terminal stages** (Hired, Rejected, Withdrawn) mark the end of a candidate's journey and cannot be deleted. Candidates in terminal stages are excluded from the dashboard's default **Relevant** filter, so your working view stays focused on active candidates.

<Note>
When you add an **Assessment**, **One-Way AI Interview**, or **Conversational AI Interview** stage, Equip automatically creates the underlying assessment or interview for you. You can then review and adjust its content.
</Note>

## Per-Stage Configuration

<Frame caption="A stage has a name candidates see, an internal name, and its own email templates">
  <img src="/images/pipeline-stages/stage-configuration.png" alt="Assessment stage in the pipeline builder with Stage Name set to Assessment, Internal Name set to SQL take-home test, and Invitation Email and Rejection Email template dropdowns" />
</Frame>

Each stage carries two names. The **internal name** (up to 30 characters) is a short dashboard label your team sees, for example "July Hiring". The **candidate-facing stage name** (up to 40 characters) is used in filters and stage-change menus, for example "Interview 2"; candidates themselves see only the job title, except that Live Interview and Custom invitation emails can include the stage name.

Beyond naming, what you can attach to a stage depends on its type:

| Capability | Available on |
|---|---|
| **Scorecard** | Phone Screening, Live Interview, Custom |
| **Invitation email template** | Assessment, One-Way AI Interview, Conversational AI Interview, Live Interview, Custom |
| **Rejection email template** | Every non-terminal stage except Offered |
| **Assignee** | Every stage except Hired, Rejected, Withdrawn |

An **assignee** is the team member responsible for candidates in that stage.

Attached email templates pre-fill the invitation or rejection dialog when you move a candidate, and you confirm each email before it goes out. To have a stage act on its own, add [Stage Automation](/stage-automation) rules to it.

<Tip>
Attach a **scorecard** to interview-style stages so every reviewer rates candidates on the same criteria. See [Scorecards](/scorecards) for how templates work.
</Tip>

## Status vs Stage

Alongside its pipeline stage, every application also carries a **candidate status**. The two are independent, and mixing them up is a common source of confusion.

| | Pipeline stage | Candidate status |
|---|---|---|
| **Answers** | Where is the candidate in the process? | What is your current verdict? |
| **Values** | The stages you configured for this job | Applied, Maybe, Shortlisted, Rejected, Withdrawn |
| **Defined by** | You, per job opening | Fixed list, same for every job |

For example, a candidate can sit in the Assessment stage with a status of **Shortlisted**, or in Phone Screening with a status of **Maybe** while you decide. Use stages to drive the process and statuses to record your read on each candidate.

## Archiving Stages

You can delete a stage from the pipeline builder, but Equip protects your data. A stage is either **Active** or **Archived**, and deletion behaves accordingly.

<Warning>
If you delete a stage that still holds candidates, Equip archives it instead of deleting it. You will see the message "Stage has candidates and was archived instead of deleted".
</Warning>

Archived stages keep their candidates and history intact. When you need one back, restore it and Equip confirms with "Stage has been restored successfully".

<Note>
Terminal stages (Hired, Rejected, Withdrawn) cannot be deleted. Every pipeline keeps them so candidate journeys always have a clear end point.
</Note>
