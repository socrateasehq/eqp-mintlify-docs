Every job opening in Equip has its own **candidate pipeline**, built from stages you choose and order. You set it up in step 3 of the job creation wizard, labelled **Candidate Pipeline**, and can edit it later from the same tab in the job's Settings.

## The 11 Stage Types

{/* screenshot: pipeline builder showing the add-stage cards with all stage types */}

<Frame caption="The stage types you can add to a pipeline">
  <img src="/images/pipeline-stages/add-stage-menu.png" width="262" alt="Add stage menu open in the pipeline builder, listing Phone Screening, Assessment, One-Way AI Interview, Conversational AI Interview, Video Interview, and Custom, each with a short description" />
</Frame>

| Stage | Kind | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Applied** | System | Every candidate starts here when they apply. Always the first stage. |
| **Phone Screening** | Configurable | Call candidates, then rank and review them. |
| **Assessment** | Configurable | Invite candidates to a skill test: quiz, coding, communication, and more. |
| **One-Way AI Interview** | Configurable | Fixed questions. Candidates record subjective responses via text, audio, or video. |
| **Conversational AI Interview** | Configurable | An AI interviewer auto-generates questions and engages in a back-and-forth conversation. |
| **Live Interview** | Configurable | Hold a video, phone, or in-person round, then schedule, score, and review candidates. |
| **Custom** | Configurable | Any step that does not fit the above, with optional ranking. |
| **Offered** | System | Supports sending an offer email and documents for the candidate to sign. |
| **Hired** | Terminal | The candidate accepted and joined. |
| **Rejected** | Terminal | You turned the candidate down. |
| **Withdrawn** | Terminal | The candidate dropped out of the process. |

The Configurable stages are yours to add, rename, and reorder by dragging. Offered and the terminal stages are managed by Equip.

**Terminal stages** (Hired, Rejected, Withdrawn) mark the end of a candidate's journey. Candidates in them are excluded from the dashboard's default **Relevant** filter.

<Note>
When you add an Assessment, One-Way AI Interview, or Conversational AI Interview stage, Equip automatically creates the underlying assessment or interview for you.
</Note>

## Per-Stage Configuration

<Frame caption="A stage has a name candidates see, an internal name, and its own email templates">
  <img src="/images/pipeline-stages/stage-configuration.png" width="1000" alt="Assessment stage in the pipeline builder with Stage Name set to Assessment, Internal Name set to SQL take-home test, and Invitation Email and Rejection Email template dropdowns" />
</Frame>

Each stage has two names. The **internal name** (up to 30 characters) is the dashboard label your team sees, for example "July Hiring". The **candidate-facing stage name** (up to 40 characters) is used in filters and stage-change menus, for example "Interview 2".

Candidates see only the job title, except that Live Interview and Custom invitation emails can include the stage name.

What else you can attach depends on the stage type:

| Capability | Available on |
|---|---|
| [**Scorecard**](/scorecards) | Phone Screening, Live Interview, Custom |
| **Invitation email template** | Assessment, One-Way AI Interview, Conversational AI Interview, Live Interview, Custom |
| **Rejection email template** | Every non-terminal stage except Offered |
| **Assignee**, the team member responsible for candidates in that stage | Every stage except Hired, Rejected, Withdrawn |

Attached email templates pre-fill the invitation or rejection dialog when you move a candidate, and you confirm each email before it goes out. To have a stage act on its own, add [Stage Automation](/stage-automation) rules to it.

## Status vs Stage

Alongside its pipeline stage, every application also carries a **candidate status**. The two are independent.

| | Pipeline stage | Candidate status |
|---|---|---|
| **Answers** | Where is the candidate in the process? | What is your current verdict? |
| **Values** | The stages you configured for this job | Applied, Maybe, Shortlisted, Rejected, Withdrawn |
| **Defined by** | You, per job opening | Fixed list, same for every job |

For example, a candidate can sit in the Assessment stage with a status of **Shortlisted**.

## Archiving Stages

You can delete a stage from the pipeline builder.

<Warning>
If you delete a stage that still holds candidates, Equip archives it instead of deleting it. You will see the message "Stage has candidates and was archived instead of deleted".
</Warning>

Archived stages keep their candidates and history intact, and you can restore them when you need one back.

<Note>
Terminal stages (Hired, Rejected, Withdrawn) cannot be deleted.
</Note>
