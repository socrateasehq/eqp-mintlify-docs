---
title: "Why Psychometric Tests Don't Have a Score"
description: "Psychometric results describe personality, not skill, so they are deliberately unscored. Here is how to read them instead."
---

Equip offers two standardized psychometric evaluations: **DISC** and **OCEAN (Big Five)**. Unlike a quiz or coding test, a psychometric test does not produce a percentage or a pass mark.

## Why There Is No Score

Psychometric tests are **subjective tests**: they measure personality traits, not skills. There are no correct answers, so there is nothing to grade.

Consider a typical question: "Do you agree that you are more creative than analytical?" Neither answer is right or wrong. The answer you prefer depends on the role you are hiring for.

| Role needs | Preferred answer |
|---|---|
| Creative thinking (design, content, marketing) | Yes |
| Analytical thinking (data, finance, engineering) | No |

Because there are no points to award, you cannot assign a psychometric test a **weightage**, so it is excluded from the [Overall Assessment Score](/test-scores-and-overall-score).

<Note>
Psychometric tests are standardized, so you cannot add or edit their questions. For a scored questionnaire with your own questions and points, build a [Custom Test](/custom-tests) instead.
</Note>

## Reading Psychometric Results

Instead of a score, each candidate's results page shows a **personality graph** of where they fall on each trait.

{/* screenshot: DISC results summary graph on the candidate results page */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate's results">
    Open the candidate from the assessment results page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hover over each trait">
    Hover on the graph to see a description of each trait and how to interpret the candidate's position on it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The traits shown depend on whether the assessment includes [DISC or OCEAN](/psychometric-tests). A sample full report is available on [Equip's psychometric test page](https://equip.co/test-types/psychometric-personality-tests-disc-and-ocean-for-hiring-and-recruitment).

<Warning>
Do not use psychometric results as an automatic filter or cutoff. Read them against the role, not against other candidates, and combine them with skill tests and interviews before making a decision.
</Warning>

To give the psychometric test only to candidates who clear your skill tests, invite your shortlist to a second assessment. See [Psychometric Tests](/psychometric-tests) for the steps.
