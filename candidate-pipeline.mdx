The **Candidate Pipeline** is the third and final step of the job creation wizard, after Job Post and Application Form. Here you define the stages candidates move through, from application to hire, along with the automations attached to each stage.

After the job is live, you can edit stages at any time from the **Candidate Pipeline** tab of the job's Settings page.

{/* screenshot: pipeline builder in step 3 of the job creation wizard, showing stage cards */}

## Choosing Stages

Every pipeline starts with the **Applied** stage, where every new application lands. From there, add the stages that match your hiring process: Phone Screening, Assessment, One-Way AI Interview, Conversational AI Interview, Live Interview, or Custom. [Pipeline Stages](/pipeline-stages) explains what each one is for.

<Note>
When you add an Assessment, One-Way AI Interview, or Conversational AI Interview stage, Equip automatically creates the underlying assessment or interview for that stage. You can review and adjust it before inviting candidates.
</Note>

The three **terminal stages** (Hired, Rejected, Withdrawn) cannot be deleted. Together with Applied and Offered, they are the fixed endpoints of every pipeline.

## Reordering and Naming

Drag any stage card to **reorder** the pipeline. Each stage carries two names:

| Name | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Candidate-facing stage name** | Shown in filters and stage-change dropdowns internally; candidates only see it in Live Interview and Custom invitation emails |
| **Internal name** | Your team, in dashboards and reports |

<Warning>
If you delete a stage that already holds candidates, Equip archives it instead of deleting it. You will see the message "Stage has candidates and was archived instead of deleted". Archived stages can be restored at any time, so no candidate data is lost.
</Warning>

## Attaching Templates and Assignees

Each stage can carry its own automations: email templates, a scorecard, and an assignee. What you can attach depends on the stage type:

| Attachment | Available on |
|---|---|
| **Invitation email template** | Assessment, One-Way AI Interview, Conversational AI Interview, Live Interview, Custom |
| **Rejection email template** | All non-terminal stages except Offered |
| **Scorecard** | Phone Screening, Live Interview, Custom |
| **Assignee** | All stages except Hired, Rejected, and Withdrawn |

These automations are visible only after the job opening is saved; you will not find them while still creating it.

To configure a stage:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the stage">
    Click the stage card in the pipeline builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach templates and a scorecard">
    Pick an **invitation email template** and a **rejection email template**. Defaults are provided, and you can manage your own under Templates. On stages that support one, select a **scorecard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an assignee">
    Click the circular icon on the top right of the stage card and pick a team member. Assignees power the "Mine" filter on the applications dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Attaching an email template does not mean emails go out automatically. When you move a candidate to a stage, you choose whether to send the email. To have Equip move candidates or send emails for you, set up [Stage Automation](/stage-automation).
</Note>
