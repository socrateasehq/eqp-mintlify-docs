<Tip>
Choose a **One-Way Interview** when you need to screen a large pool with the same fixed questions, and a **Conversational Interview** when you want a live, adaptive voice interview that digs deeper with follow-ups.
</Tip>

## The Two Interview Types

Equip offers two families of **AI Interviews**, both attached to a job opening and both graded by AI.

A **One-Way Interview** presents a fixed set of questions. It is built from reusable templates, so you can run the same interview across multiple openings.

A **Conversational Interview** is a live, spoken interview with the **Equip AI Interviewer**, which asks follow-up questions based on what it hears.

| | One-Way Interview | Conversational Interview |
|---|---|---|
| **Format** | Pre-set questions, recorded answers | Real-time voice conversation |
| **Adaptive follow-ups** | No | Yes, the AI adapts to each answer |
| **Response types** | Video, audio, text, or MCQ per question | Spoken; optional written or code answers via the Workspace |
| **Built from** | Reusable question templates | A job description, from which Equip drafts topics |
| **Questions** | Up to 40 per interview | Quick: 5 to 10; Comprehensive: 10 to 25 |
| **Grading** | AI-graded per question | AI report with recommendation, strengths, and topic scores |

<Note>
Conversational interviews come in two formats: **Quick** runs about 15 minutes with 3 to 5 topics, while **Comprehensive** runs about 30 minutes with 5 to 10 topics.
</Note>

## When to Use Each

Use a **one-way interview** for high-volume screening, typically when you have 30 or more candidates. Candidates can complete it whenever it suits them.

Use a **conversational interview** for deeper evaluation. The AI asks up to 5 follow-ups per topic. It can also present **Scenarios**, reference material shown in a side panel for the candidate to respond to; Scenarios are rolling out gradually and may not be available to every team yet.

| Situation | Recommended type |
|---|---|
| Screening a large applicant pool | One-Way |
| Comparing candidates on identical questions | One-Way |
| Evaluating communication with video responses | One-Way |
| Second-round or in-depth evaluation | Conversational (Comprehensive) |
| A short, focused screen with follow-ups | Conversational (Quick) |
| A spoken interview in a language other than English | Conversational |

<Note>
Conversational interviews can be conducted in 15 languages. See [Interview Languages](/interview-languages) for the list.
</Note>

## Credit Costs

AI Interviews use Equip's **credits** (1 credit = \$1), included in your subscription plan's monthly allowance.

| Interview type | Credits per candidate |
|---|---|
| **One-Way Interview** (up to 20 questions) | 1 |
| **One-Way Interview** (21 to 40 questions) | 2 |
| **Quick Conversational Interview** | 2 |
| **Comprehensive Conversational Interview** | 3 |

<Tip>
If a candidate never starts the interview, you can reclaim the credits. See [Reclaim Unused Credits](/reclaim-credits).
</Tip>

## What Candidates Experience

Both interview types start with the same three-step **pre-interview wizard**: candidates review the format, test their camera and microphone, then start the interview.

In a **one-way interview**, candidates work through the question list at their own pace within one session. You set the retakes for audio and video questions, up to 5, and the word limits for text questions.

In a **conversational interview**, candidates join a live video call with the Equip AI Interviewer, a self-view, and a real-time transcript.

{/* screenshot: candidate view of a conversational interview showing the AI interviewer, self-view, and live transcript */}

<Warning>
Once a candidate submits a conversational interview, it cannot be resumed or retaken from the same invitation. You can optionally allow candidates to restart an unfinished attempt, in which case only the submitted attempt is graded.
</Warning>

The same [proctoring options](/proctoring-settings) available for assessments apply to AI interviews, except **Detect Audio** and **Show Webcam Preview** for conversational interviews, which do not apply. Candidates see the active measures and consent before starting.
