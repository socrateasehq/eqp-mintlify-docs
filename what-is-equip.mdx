New to Equip? This page gives you the entire platform in ten sentences, then a four-step path to your first assessment, and finally the doors into each part of the help center.

## The Ten Sentences

1. **Equip** is a self-serve hiring platform where recruiters shortlist the best candidates and candidates get a fair, skill-based chance to prove themselves.
2. It has three pillars, usable together or on their own: a free AI-native **ATS**, online skill **Assessments**, and **AI Interviews**.
3. The ATS is free forever and includes job posts, application forms, a configurable candidate pipeline, AI resume screening with a 0 to 100 **Job Fit Score**, and hiring analytics.
4. An **assessment** is a set of tests a candidate takes one after another, built from 9 test types including Quizzes, Programming, Communication (CEFR), Psychometric, and Video Response.
5. Every test type offers **Equip Content**, pre-built tests from our question library, and **My Content**, custom tests you author yourself.
6. AI Interviews come in two families: **one-way** interviews where candidates answer pre-set questions, and **conversational** interviews where a real-time voice AI adapts its questions and asks follow-ups.
7. Cheating prevention is built in: attempts run under **AI proctoring** powered by AutoProctor, and every completed attempt receives a 0 to 100% **Trust Score**.
8. Pricing is pay-per-use with **credits** (1 credit equals \$1 or ₹90, depending on your team's country): 1 credit per candidate per test, 1 credit per one-way interview, and 2 or 3 credits per conversational interview, with no subscriptions, no seat fees, and credits that never expire.
9. New teams get **10 free credits** on signup, previewing assessments is always free and unlimited, and you can add unlimited team members at no cost.
10. Sign up with your work email at [equip.co](https://equip.co), create a job opening or assessment, preview it, and invite your first candidates within minutes.

<Note>
Want to look around before creating an account? Explore the sample recruiter dashboard at [equip.co/demo/recruiter/home](https://equip.co/demo/recruiter/home/) or try the test types yourself at [equip.co/demo/test-types](https://equip.co/demo/test-types/).
</Note>

## Your First Assessment in Four Steps

The fastest way to feel how Equip works is to create one assessment and preview it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an assessment">
    Select the job role and the experience level you are hiring for. Equip suggests relevant skills, and you can add more skills or attach your own [custom tests](/custom-tests).
    {/* screenshot: assessment creation, role and skill selection */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview it for free">
    Previews are unlimited and never cost credits. For quizzes that use Equip's library, the preview shows **sample questions** that represent what candidates will see, which protects the question bank; custom tests preview with your actual questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite candidates">
    Share the assessment link directly, or enter candidate email addresses to send invitations. Each invited candidate receives a **unique link** to their attempt.
    {/* screenshot: invite page with email input */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch results come in live">
    As candidates start and submit, your dashboard updates in real time with progress, scores, and Trust Scores.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Recruiter signup requires a **work email**; personal domains like Gmail are not accepted. If a team with your company's domain already exists, ask its admin to invite you instead of signing up again.
</Tip>
