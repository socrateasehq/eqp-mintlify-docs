An **interview panel** is a named, reusable group of people who interview and score candidates together. Pick a panel when you schedule a live interview, and every member gets the candidate's CV and a link to the interview.

## Create a Panel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Interviews tab">
    Go to [**Account → Team**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/) and open the [**Interviews** tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/interviews/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a panel and its people">
    Click **Add panel**, name it, and create it. Then add each person by email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: Interviews tab showing an interview panel with member chips */}

Pick the panel in the **Panel** dropdown on a Live Interview stage card, or when you [schedule a live interview](/scheduling-live-interviews).

## Who Can Be on a Panel

Panel members can be anyone with an email address. They do not need to be on your Equip team, and being on a panel does not add them to it or change their role.

| | Teammates | People outside your team |
|---|---|---|
| **How they open an interview** | From the Live Interviews page or the panel brief email | From the link in the panel brief email, signing in with their email address |
| **What they can see** | The candidate's CV, profile, prior results, and report links | The candidate's CV, profile, and prior results |

<Tip>
Use panels to bring in a hiring manager or a client who does not have an Equip seat. They get access only to the candidates they are asked to interview.
</Tip>

## How Panels Behave

A panel is a live group. Every interview that uses it follows the panel's current membership.

| Change you make | Effect on open interviews |
|---|---|
| **Add a member** | They gain access and are expected to submit feedback |
| **Remove a member** | They lose access. Feedback they already submitted is kept. |
| **Delete the panel** | Members lose access to every candidate shared through it |

<Warning>
A panel with upcoming interviews cannot be deleted. Move those interviews to another panel, or cancel them, first.
</Warning>

## Interview Locations

**Interview locations** are saved addresses offered when you schedule an in-person interview. Add them on the same [**Interviews** tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/interviews/) with **Add location**.

Each location has a name and full address. A maps link and a reception note, for example who to ask for at the front desk, are optional.

{/* screenshot: Interview locations table with name, address, map, and reception note */}

<Note>
Deleting a location only removes it from the list. Interviews already scheduled keep their address.
</Note>
