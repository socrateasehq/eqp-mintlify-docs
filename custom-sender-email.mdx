You can control the **sender name** candidates see on invitation emails and the **reply-to address** their responses go to, but not the "from" address itself.

## The Default Sender

Every invitation email has three sender-related parts, and each behaves differently:

| Part | Default value | Can you change it? |
|---|---|---|
| **From address** | no-reply@equip.co | No, it is fixed for all accounts |
| **Sender name** | Your first and last name | Yes, via your [Profile page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/profile/) |
| **Reply-to address** | Your email address | Yes, via the Sender dropdown |

## Customizing the Sender

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Profile page">
    Go to your [Profile page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/profile/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your name">
    Edit your first and last name. New invitations sent with you selected as the Sender use the updated name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reply-To Addresses

The **reply-to address** is the email ID of the team member who sends the invitation. When a candidate hits Reply, their message lands in that person's inbox.

Your login email cannot be changed, so to use a different reply-to address, send as a teammate. Open the Invite link under your assessment and pick them in the **Sender** dropdown on the Send Invitations tab.

The **Sender** dropdown only shows people already on your Equip team. If the address you need is missing, invite that person first. See [Manage Team Members](/manage-team-members).
