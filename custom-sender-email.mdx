---
title: "Changing the Sender Name and Email"
description: "Customize who assessment, AI interview and hiring pipeline emails appear to come from."
---

You can control the **sender name** candidates see on assessment and AI interview invitations, and on emails from your hiring pipeline, plus the **reply-to address** their responses go to. The "from" address itself is fixed.

## The Default Sender

Every email sent from the **Invite** page of an assessment or AI interview has three sender-related parts:

| Part | Default value | Can you change it? |
|---|---|---|
| **From address** | no-reply@equip.co | No, it is fixed for all accounts |
| **Sender name** | The selected sender's first and last name | Yes, via your [Profile page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/profile/) |
| **Reply-to address** | Your email address | Yes, via the Sender dropdown |

## Customizing the Sender

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Profile page">
    Go to your [Profile page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/profile/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your name">
    Edit your first and last name. New invitations sent with you selected as the Sender use the updated name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
If the name is empty or contains punctuation or a double space, emails go out under the name **Team Equip** instead.
</Warning>

## Reply-To Addresses

The **reply-to address** is the email of the team member who sends the invitation, so candidate replies land in their inbox.

Your login email cannot be changed, so to use a different reply-to address, send as a teammate. Open the Invite link under your assessment or AI interview and pick them in the **Sender** dropdown on the Send Invitations tab.

{/* screenshot: Send Invitations tab with the Sender dropdown expanded */}

<Tip>
The **Sender** dropdown only shows people already on your Equip team. If the address you need is missing, invite that person first. See [Manage Team Members](/manage-team-members).
</Tip>

## Emails Sent from the Hiring Pipeline

When you [move candidates between stages](/changing-stages) in a job opening, the email dialog (assessment or AI interview invite, live interview, custom stage, offer, rejection) sets the sender differently:

| Part | Invite page emails | Pipeline stage-change emails |
|---|---|---|
| **Sender name** | Selected sender's full name | Your team's **Brand name** |
| **Reply-to address** | Selected sender's email | no-reply@equip.co, unless set per send |
| **Where to change** | Profile page and Sender dropdown | **Brand name** on the [Organization tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/organization/); **Set email receiving replies** in the dialog |

Check **Set email receiving replies** in the dialog to pick the team member whose email receives replies.
