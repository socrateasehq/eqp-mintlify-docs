<Tip>
tl;dr: A candidate can attempt an assessment only once. To let them try again, delete their existing attempt from the Results page and re-invite them; a full re-attempt costs one extra credit. AI interviews use their own settings instead: see [Re-Attempting an AI Interview](#re-attempting-an-ai-interview).
</Tip>

## One Attempt Per Candidate

Every candidate can attempt an assessment **only once**, and no setting raises this limit. AI interviews work differently; see [Re-Attempting an AI Interview](#re-attempting-an-ai-interview).

An unfinished assessment is **always resumed** for the same email ID. Even a fresh invitation with a brand-new link continues the original attempt from where the candidate left off.

<Note>
Equip identifies a candidate by their **email ID**. The same person using two different email IDs counts as two candidates.
</Note>

Many requests are really resume situations, so check before deleting anything:

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Device or connection issue, assessment not finished | Candidate clicks the same link again and continues from the first test they have not finished |
| Invitation link expired | Re-invite them, then reclaim the credit from the previous invitation |
| You want to give another chance after they finished | Delete their attempt and re-invite them |

## Allowing a Re-Attempt

Delete the candidate's existing attempt, finished or not, and invite them again.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Delete the attempt from the Results page">
    On the assessment's Results page, select the candidate's checkbox and click **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-invite the candidate">
    Send a new invitation. The candidate starts the assessment from scratch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: Results page with a candidate row selected via checkbox and the Delete action in the bulk actions bar */}

<Warning>
Deleting an attempt permanently removes the candidate's scores and answers. If you may need the results later, download the candidate's report before deleting.
</Warning>

If the candidate says they never received the invite, check its [Email Status](/email-status) before re-inviting.

## Credits and Re-Attempts

Equip **always deducts a credit on each invitation**, including re-invitations. What changes is whether you can reclaim the earlier credit:

| Candidate's progress | Previous credit | What you pay |
|---|---|---|
| Has not started the first test | Reclaimable | Only the new invitation's credit |
| Started or finished | Not reclaimable | One more credit |

<Note>
A credit is reclaimable only when the invitation was sent more than 24 hours ago and the candidate has not started the first test. Reclaiming a credit also revokes the candidate's link and deletes any test data tied to it. See [Reclaim Credits](/reclaim-credits).
</Note>

## Re-Attempting an AI Interview

For **AI interviews**, re-attempts depend on a setting that differs by interview type:

| | Conversational interview | One-way interview |
|---|---|---|
| Setting | **Allow resuming unfinished attempt**, in the interview's Settings (section 4), off by default | **Max Retakes** per question, default 5, max 5 |
| What it allows | Restart the whole interview from scratch if the candidate leaves before submitting; only the submitted attempt is graded | Re-record an answer, not retake the whole interview |
| After a reload | Without the toggle, the candidate sees "Conversational AI interviews cannot be resumed once started" | Used retakes are remembered |

Open the conversational interview's Settings from the [**AI Interviews** dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/).

<Note>
If a candidate's connection drops during a conversational interview and cannot reconnect, they see "Your interview has ended" and are told to contact their recruiter to retake it.
</Note>
