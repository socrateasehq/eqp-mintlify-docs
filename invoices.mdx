Every subscription payment and credit top-up on Equip generates an invoice automatically. Once your payment succeeds, the invoice is emailed to the person who **created the account** (the Team Admin), and any team member can retrieve it later from the Billing Portal.

## Downloading Invoices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Purchase Credits page">
    From your recruiter dashboard, click the **Buy More** button to open the [**Purchase Credits** page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/topup-credits/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Billing">
    Click the **Go to Billing** button to open the Billing Portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the purchase and download">
    Under **Invoice History**, click the date of the purchase you need, then view or download its Invoice and Receipt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Billing Portal

Equip uses a self-serve **Billing Portal**, powered by Stripe, to store your payment history. All team members can open the portal, not just the admin.

<Note>
Invoices are billed to your team's **Legal Entity Name**, set on the [Team page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/), on the [**Organization** tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/organization/). Confirm it is correct before you pay.
</Note>

<Tip>
Need to correct the company name, address, or tax details on future invoices? Click **Update Information** inside the same Billing Portal. See [Change Billing Information](/change-billing-information) for details, including how to fix an invoice that has already been issued.
</Tip>

## GST Details

For teams in India, **18% GST** is added to your credit purchase.

To have your **GSTIN** printed on the invoice, enter it in the optional GSTIN field on the [**Purchase Credits** page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/topup-credits/) before you pay.

If your team must deduct TDS on its payments, see [TDS for Purchases](/tds-for-purchases).

<Warning>
GST details only appear on invoices for purchases made after you entered your GSTIN. Already-issued invoices cannot be edited in-product; email **hello@equip.co** if you need one re-issued with updated details.
</Warning>
