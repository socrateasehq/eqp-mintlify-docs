Every subscription payment and credit top-up on Equip generates an invoice automatically. Once your payment succeeds, the invoice is emailed to the person who **created the account**, and any member can retrieve it later from the **Billing** page.

## Downloading Invoices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Billing page">
    From your recruiter dashboard sidebar, go to **Account** > [**Billing**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/billing/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the invoice and download it">
    Under **Invoices**, each row lists a date and amount. Click **Download** on the row you need.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## GST Details

For teams in India, **18% GST** is added to your subscription and credit purchases.

To have your **GSTIN** printed on invoices, enter it in the GSTIN field under **Invoices** on the Billing page.

If your team must deduct TDS on its payments, see [TDS for Purchases](/tds-for-purchases).

<Warning>
GST details only appear on invoices for purchases made after you entered your GSTIN. Already-issued invoices cannot be edited in-product; email **hello@equip.co** if you need one re-issued with updated details.
</Warning>

## The Billing Portal

For your payment method and the company name and email shown on invoices, Equip uses a separate **Billing Portal**, powered by Stripe. Click **Open billing portal**, at the top of the Invoices section on the Billing page, to reach it.

<Note>
Invoices are billed to your team's **Legal Entity Name**, set on the [**Company** tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/organization/company/) of **Account > Organization**. Confirm it is correct before you pay.
</Note>

<Tip>
Need to correct the company name or email on future invoices? Click **Open billing portal**, then **Update information**. See [Change Billing Information](/change-billing-information) for details, including how to fix an invoice that has already been issued.
</Tip>
