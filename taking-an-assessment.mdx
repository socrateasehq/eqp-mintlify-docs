<Tip>
The short version: sign in with your email, attempt the free **demo tests** first, complete every test in order, and only close the tab after you see the **Assessment Completed** screen.
</Tip>

## Before You Start

### Sign in and verify your email

Equip does not ask you to create an account or set a password. You verify your identity with your email address.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your assessment link">
    Open the link from your invitation email or your recruiter, and enter your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Enter the **6-digit code** Equip sends to that address. The code expires in 10 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your name and details">
    Confirm your name and email address, and fill in any details your recruiter asks for, such as your phone number or LinkedIn URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **instructions page** shows how the assessment is structured and the rules for each test. If your recruiter set a start window, you cannot begin outside it.

### Attempt the demo tests

Before the actual tests, Equip shows a **Demo Tests** page. [Demo tests](/demo-tests) take about a minute, are completely free, and let you get comfortable with the interface and proctoring setup before anything counts. If you have already attempted them, you can select **Skip to Assessment**.

<Warning>
Demo tests are clearly labelled "Not Actual Assessment - Just Demo Tests". Finishing only a demo test does not submit your assessment. Make sure you continue into the real tests.
</Warning>

### Check your device

Google Chrome on a desktop or laptop is the safest choice for any assessment. Some test types also work on phones, while others require a desktop.

| Works on mobile | Desktop only |
|---|---|
| Quiz (MCQs, essays) | Programming |
| Psychometric | SQL |
| English Communication (CEFR) | CSS |
| Video Response | Excel, Typing |

Your recruiter may also block phones and tablets entirely. If a test will not load, see [Device Compatibility](/device-compatibility).

## During the Test

### Proctoring and consent

Most assessments are proctored by AI, and Equip may access your camera, microphone, and screen while a test is running. Before each test starts, you see which proctoring measures are active, and you consent to the proctoring report being shared with your recruiter. Grant camera and microphone permissions when your browser asks, or the test cannot begin.

### Timers

There is no single countdown for the whole assessment. Time limits apply **per test** or **per question**.

<Warning>
Tests with a per-test timer run on the clock, not on your activity. A 45-minute test started at 11:00 ends at 11:45 even if your internet drops in between, so reconnect and resume as quickly as you can.
</Warning>

### Quiz display modes

Quizzes behave differently depending on how your recruiter set them up. The instructions on screen always tell you which mode you are in.

<Note>
In a **one-by-one** quiz, you cannot return to a question you skip. Reloading the page blocks your current question and moves you to the next one, so avoid refreshing unless you have to.
</Note>

### Other test types

Coding tests run in a browser-based editor where you pick your programming language. For audio and video response questions, the allowed retakes and answer durations are shown on screen.

### If you get disconnected

Losing your connection or accidentally closing the tab does not end your assessment. Click the same assessment link again from any device and Equip resumes your attempt wherever possible.

With per-question timers, the question you were on is forfeited. With per-test timers, the clock keeps running while you are away. See [Resume Your Attempt](/resume-your-attempt) for details.

## Submitting

### Submit each test

You complete and submit each test in order before moving to the next. After audio and video answers, stay on the page until the upload finishes.

### The completion screen

When you finish the final test, you see the **Assessment Completed** screen. At this point you can safely close the tab.

<Warning>
If you are unsure whether you finished, open your assessment link again. Seeing the Assessment Completed screen confirms your submission. Seeing an option to continue means some tests are still unfinished, and you can complete them right away.
</Warning>

### Results

Equip shares your scores and reports with the recruiter who invited you. Contact your recruiter directly for your results and next steps.
