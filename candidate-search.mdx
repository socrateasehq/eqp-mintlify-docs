**Candidate Search** is a quick lookup tool for your whole account. Instead of opening each job post or assessment and searching separately, you type a name or email once and see every place that candidate appears.

## Searching Your Candidates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Candidate Search">
    In the sidebar, go to **ATS** and click **Candidate Search**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a name or email">
    Type into the search box labeled **Search by name or email**. An email address gives the most precise match; a name works when you do not have the email handy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow with filters (optional)">
    Use the **Activity type** filter to limit results to a specific kind of activity, and the **Activity date** filter to limit results to a time period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Search">
    Results load only after you click **Search**. Typing alone does not run the query.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<!-- screenshot: Candidate Search page with the search box, Activity type and Activity date filters, and the Search button -->

<Note>
Candidate Search is part of the Equip ATS, which is free. Running searches never consumes credits.
</Note>

## Search Scope

Candidate Search covers your team's entire account, across every job opening. Each result represents an activity the candidate was involved in, not just an application.

The **Activity type** filter accepts these values:

| Activity type | What it matches |
|---|---|
| **Job Application** | The candidate applied to one of your job posts |
| **Assessment** | The candidate attempted an assessment |
| **AI Interview** | The candidate attempted an AI interview |
| **Assessment Invitation** | The candidate was invited to an assessment |
| **AI Interview Invitation** | The candidate was invited to an AI interview |

Because invitations are tracked separately from attempts, you can quickly check whether an invited candidate has actually started, or find everyone you invited during a given week using the **Activity date** filter.

<Tip>
Candidate Search finds a specific person you already know. To mine your past applicants for a new role using skills and experience filters, use [Talent Rediscovery](/talent-rediscovery) instead.
</Tip>

## Managing Results

Each result row is a shortcut, not a dead end. Clicking a row deep-links to the dashboard that owns that activity: the job post's applications dashboard, the assessment's results page, or the AI interview's results page.

When the destination page opens, the candidate's email is already pre-filled in that page's search filter. You land directly on the matching record, with all of that dashboard's tools available: the full candidate profile, scores, stage controls, and journey history.

<!-- screenshot: search results list with a row expanded to show the deep link into a job post's applications dashboard -->

<Note>
Detailed candidate information lives on the destination dashboards. Candidate Search is the fastest route to them, so use it as your starting point whenever you only have a name or email.
</Note>
