**Candidate Search** is a quick lookup tool for your whole account. Type a name or email once and see every place that candidate appears.

## Searching Your Candidates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Candidate Search">
    In the sidebar, go to **ATS** and click [**Candidate Search**](https://equip.co/recruiter/candidate-search/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search by name or email">
    Type at least 3 characters, set the filters if you want to narrow the results, then click **Search** or press Enter. Typing alone does not run the query.

    Invitations do not store a name, so invitation-only candidates can only be found by email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Search Scope

Each result is an **activity** the candidate was involved in, not just an application: a job application, an assessment or AI interview attempt, or an invitation to either. The **Activity type** filter limits results to one of these, and the Activity date filter to a time period.

Invitations are tracked separately from attempts, so you can check whether an invited candidate has actually started.

<Tip>
Candidate Search finds a specific person you already know. To mine your past applicants for a new role using skills and experience filters, use [Talent Rediscovery](/talent-rediscovery) instead.
</Tip>

## Managing Results

Clicking a result row **deep-links** to the dashboard that owns that activity: the job post's applications dashboard, the assessment's results page, or the AI interview's results page.
