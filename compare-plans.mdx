Equip has three subscription plans: **Standard**, **Premium** and **Elite**. Each includes a monthly credit allowance and a number of recruiter seats.

## Plans at a Glance

| | Standard | Premium | Elite |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly price** | \$30 | \$90 | \$250 |
| **Annual price** | \$330 | \$1,000 | \$2,700 |
| **Credits included** | 30 a month | 90 a month | 250 a month |
| **Recruiter seats** | 2 | 5 | Unlimited |

Annual plans add the whole year's credits at the start. On Standard and Premium, each extra seat costs 5 credits a month.

<Tip>
To price a plan against your own hiring volume, use the calculator on the [Pricing page](https://equip.co/pricing/).
</Tip>

## Free Trial

New teams get a **30-day free trial** with **10 free credits**, and no card is needed. During the trial you can use Elite features, except the API, webhooks and integrations.

<Warning>
When the trial ends, choose a plan on the [**Billing** page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/billing/) to keep using your account and your remaining credits.
</Warning>
