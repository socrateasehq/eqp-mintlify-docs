---
title: "Why Are Some Assessment Results Missing?"
description: "Reasons a candidate's attempt may not appear on the results page, and how to find it."
---

<Tip>
A candidate's attempt is usually missing for one of four reasons: the **Show Unfinished** filter is off, the attempt was deleted, the assessment's tests changed after the attempt (a **Delinked Test Attempt**), or your credit balance has gone negative. Only deleted attempts are unrecoverable.
</Tip>

## Common Causes

If you cannot find a candidate on an assessment's results page, work through these causes in order:

| Cause | What Happened | Can You See the Result? |
|---|---|---|
| **Unfinished attempt** | The candidate started but has not submitted every test, and the **Show Unfinished** filter is not selected. | Yes. Select **Show Unfinished** in the results filters. |
| **Deleted attempt** | You or a teammate deleted the candidate's attempt. | No. Deleted attempts cannot be restored. |
| **Delinked Test Attempt** | The assessment's set of tests changed after the candidate attempted it. | Yes, via the delinked attempts link on the results dashboard. |
| **Negative credit balance** | Your team's balance dropped below zero. | Yes, after you top up. |

<Warning>
Deleting an attempt is permanent and it cannot be restored. Confirm with your team before removing any candidate's attempt.
</Warning>

### Delinked Test Attempts

When you change which tests an assessment contains, earlier results are no longer comparable with later ones. Equip removes the older attempts from the main results page and calls them **Delinked Test Attempts**.

### Viewing Delinked Test Attempts

On the assessment's results page, a delinked test shows **"Delinked Test"** in red instead of the test's name. The results dashboard includes a link to the delinked test attempts, where each candidate's test submissions remain viewable.

<Note>
Restoring an **Equip-content quiz** to its earlier settings does not relink old quiz attempts. Re-adding a removed **Custom Test** does bring those attempts back to the results page.
</Note>

## Credit Balance and Hidden Results

Equip lets your team's credit balance run up to 25 credits into the negative, with two limits.

| Balance | Effect on Results and Candidates |
|---|---|
| **Below 0** | New results are hidden until you top up your credits. |
| **Below -25** | Candidates can no longer start attempts. |

Once you purchase credits and the balance is positive again, the hidden results become visible.

<Tip>
Equip sends **low-balance email alerts** to your team admin. See [How Credits Work](/how-credits-work) for the full deduction policy.
</Tip>

## What to Check

Run through this checklist before contacting support:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the Show Unfinished filter">
    On the assessment's results page, select **Show Unfinished** in the filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for delinked test attempts">
    If the assessment's tests were edited after some candidates attempted it, open the delinked attempts link on the results dashboard to find their submissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your credit balance">
    If your balance is negative, purchase credits and the hidden results reappear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your team about deletions">
    Check whether a teammate deleted the attempt. Deleted attempts cannot be restored, so the candidate would need to be invited again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Still cannot find the attempt after all four checks? Share the assessment name and the candidate's email address over live chat. See [Contact Support](/contact-support).
</Note>
