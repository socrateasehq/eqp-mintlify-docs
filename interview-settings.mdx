Every AI interview has a **Settings** page where you control when candidates can start, whether they can restart an unfinished attempt, and how the session is proctored.

## Open the Settings Page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the AI Interviews dashboard">
    Open [**AI Interviews**](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/) from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Settings">
    Each interview in the list has a **Settings** link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Availability Window

When set, candidates can start the interview **only inside this window**.

On conversational interviews this appears as **Availability window (optional)** in the Settings section. On one-way interviews the same pickers live under **Timer Settings**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the start of the window">
    Use the **Cannot Start Before** date and time picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the end of the window">
    Use the **Cannot Start After** picker. The end must be after the start.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove a limit, click **Clear date & time** under either field. Leaving both fields empty means candidates can start any time.

## Restart Policy

The **Allow resuming unfinished attempt** toggle controls what happens when a candidate leaves before submitting. It is **off by default**, so a candidate who leaves mid-interview cannot come back and restart.

When it is on, candidates can restart if they leave before submitting. Restarts are unlimited until they submit, and only the submitted attempt is graded.

<Warning>
Once a candidate submits, the attempt is final. A conversational interview cannot be resumed or retaken after submission, regardless of this toggle.
</Warning>

## Proctoring Options

The **Proctoring Settings** section is collapsed by default. AI interviews use the same proctoring options as Equip assessments, explained in [Proctoring Settings](/proctoring-settings).

<Note>
**Detect Audio** is shown but disabled for AI interviews, since interviews are already audio or video by nature.
</Note>

The section also includes **Disable Copy Paste**, which prevents copying and pasting during the session. You cannot turn off **Detect Switched Tab** while **Switched Tab Screenshot** is enabled. See [AI Interview Reports](/interview-reports) for how the evidence appears.

## Other Settings

A few more options live in the settings section. Some are being rolled out gradually and may not appear for every team yet.

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Candidate instructions** | Rich-text notes shown to candidates, up to 1,000 characters. On one-way interviews this field is labeled **Additional Instructions**. |
| **Require Camera** | Available only in conversational interviews. When off, the interview is audio-only and no video is recorded. |
| **Enable Workspace** | Available only in conversational interviews. Adds a Workspace panel where candidates can write answers in code or plain text. Off by default and available to teams in the gradual rollout. |
| **Report Template** | Available only in conversational interviews. Attaches a custom report template. Included with annual Premium and Elite plans. |
