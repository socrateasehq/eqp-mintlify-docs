<Tip>
Equip's proctoring is fully automated and driven by AI. It monitors the candidate's camera, microphone, and screen during every test, then gives you a **Trust Score** and a timestamped list of violations with evidence. Most proctoring features are free and require no browser extension or installation.
</Tip>

## How AI Proctoring Works

Candidates see which proctoring measures are active before they begin. They must consent to the monitoring, and to the proctoring report being shared with you, before the test starts.

Try most of the measures yourself in Equip's [self-serve proctoring demo](https://equip.co/demo/proctoring/). No login is needed.

## What Gets Tracked

Equip offers proctoring measures across three areas, all configurable per test in [Proctoring Settings](/proctoring-settings).

| Area | Measures |
|---|---|
| **Candidate activity** | Face detection, audio cues, tab or application switch, external monitor detection, random photos |
| **Test-taking environment** | Full-screen enforcement, copy-paste disabled, Auxiliary Device, Session Recording |
| **Question rendering and selection** | Shuffle question order, randomize MCQ options, shuffle coding questions, question banks, question display mode |

The question rendering and selection options are configured as [anti-cheating settings](/anti-cheating-settings) and quiz options rather than proctoring toggles.

## Preventing ChatGPT and AI Tool Usage

With **copy-paste disabled**, the candidate cannot move the question text into a tool like ChatGPT. Opening another tab breaks full-screen mode and triggers tab-switch detection, both of which are flagged with evidence.

On-screen overlay assistants such as Cluely or InterviewCoder, or an AI app on a second phone, evade regular proctoring. **Auxiliary Device** proctoring catches both.

<Tip>
Auxiliary Device proctoring is free but disabled by default because it can feel intrusive. Enable it if you suspect candidates are cheating with AI tools. See [Detecting AI Usage](/ai-usage-detection) for the full setup.
</Tip>

## Reviewing Evidence

Every completed attempt gets a **Trust Score** from 0 to 100%. The lower the score, the more likely Equip thinks the candidate cheated, so you can focus your review on low-scoring candidates.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate's report">
    From the results page, open the candidate. If the Trust Score is below about 80%, review the full proctoring report rather than relying on the number alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the violations">
    Open the **Proctoring** tab to see each violation with its timestamp and evidence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the Session Recording">
    If Session Recording was enabled, play back the candidate's screen activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
The Trust Score is calculated by AI, and AI can make mistakes. Never disqualify a candidate on the Trust Score alone. Always review the underlying evidence before making a decision.
</Warning>
