title: "Why Can't I See Quiz Questions?"
description: "Why Equip library questions stay hidden while creating assessments and viewing results."
---

<Tip>
Equip hides its library quiz questions from everyone, including recruiters, so candidates can never find them in advance. Results give you skill-wise and difficulty-wise breakdowns instead. If you need full question visibility, use a Custom Quiz.
</Tip>

## Question Protection

When you use **Equip Content**, quizzes draw questions at random from Equip's **question banks**. You choose the skills and difficulty level; Equip picks the exact questions for each candidate.

Equip is self-serve, so if questions were visible in the builder or in reports, a candidate could create a recruiter account and uncover the question bank. [Security, Privacy & Candidate Data](/security-privacy) lists the other ways Equip protects its questions.

<Note>
Each candidate gets different questions, in a different order, with shuffled answer options. See [Same or Random Questions](/same-or-random-questions).
</Note>

## What You Can See

Candidate reports include a **skill-wise and difficulty-wise breakdown** for every Equip quiz, so you can see where a candidate was strong or weak.

{/* screenshot: skill-wise and difficulty-wise breakdown in a candidate's quiz report */}

The strict policy applies only to Equip quizzes:

| Test type (Equip Content) | Select questions while creating? | See questions in results? |
|---|---|---|
| **Quiz** | No | No |
| **Programming, SQL, CSS** | Yes | Yes |

Coding challenges can stay visible because the risk of leaks is much lower.

<Warning>
Equip does not share its quiz questions even on request. This applies to all teams equally.
</Warning>

## Custom Content Is Different

If your process requires seeing every question a candidate answered, create a **Custom Quiz**. The content is yours, only your team can see it, and none of the restrictions above apply.

You write every question yourself, and results show a **question-wise breakdown** of each candidate's responses and points. See [Custom Tests](/custom-tests) for what else you control.

<Note>
You can mix Equip Content and Custom Tests freely in one assessment.
</Note>
