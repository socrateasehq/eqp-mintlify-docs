A **Live Interview** stage is a video, phone, or in-person round that you hold outside Equip. Equip books the slot, briefs the candidate and the interview panel by email, and collects each panellist's feedback afterwards.

## Schedule an Interview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Move the candidate into the stage">
    Move one candidate into a Live Interview stage, and the schedule dialog opens. For a candidate already in the stage, click **Schedule** in the candidate's details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the slot, mode, and panel">
    Pick the date and time, then choose **Video**, **Phone**, or **In-person** and say where the interview happens: a meeting link, a phone number, or an address, typed or picked from a saved location. A panel is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the emails and schedule">
    Choose who gets an email, then click **Schedule**. You can set a Reply-To team member and use **Schedule send** to delay the emails.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: schedule live interview dialog showing slot, mode tiles, panel, and email options */}

<Note>
Equip does not create the meeting for you; paste a link from your own video tool. Both emails include **Add to calendar** links for Google Calendar and Outlook.
</Note>

Candidates move into a Live Interview stage one at a time, so each gets their own slot. To move someone now and book later, click **Move without scheduling**; they wait under **To schedule** on the [**Live Interviews** page](https://equip.co/recruiter/live-interviews/).

## Set Up the Stage

Each Live Interview stage carries defaults that pre-fill the schedule dialog, and you can change them per interview. Set them on the stage card in the job's **Settings**, under the **Candidate Pipeline** tab.

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Mode** | The default mode: Video, Phone, or In-person |
| **Minutes** | The default interview length |
| **Suggested Qs** | Questions and topics panellists see while they interview |
| **Panel** | The interview panel picked by default |
| **Scorecard** | The scorecard panellists fill in |
| **Invitation Email** | The email template sent to the candidate |

## The Emails That Go Out

Scheduling can send two emails. Edit both in [Email Templates](/email-templates).

| Email | Goes to | Based on |
|---|---|---|
| **Candidate invite** | The candidate | The stage's **Live Interview Invitation** template |
| **Panel brief** | Every panel member | Your team's **Live Interview Panel Brief** template, with a link to the candidate's CV and interview details |

<Warning>
A candidate with an **unverified email** is still scheduled, and the panel is still briefed, but the candidate receives no invite. Share their verification link first.
</Warning>

## Reschedule or Cancel

You can change a booked interview from the [**Live Interviews** page](https://equip.co/recruiter/live-interviews/) or from the candidate's details.

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Reschedule** | Change the slot, the mode, or the panel |
| **Cancel** | Cancels the interview and returns the candidate to **To schedule**. The panel loses access to the candidate through that interview. |

Both actions can notify the candidate and the panel, and the reason you enter is included in the email.

<Warning>
Moving or rejecting a candidate does not cancel their interview. Equip warns you that it stays scheduled; cancel it from the candidate's details if it is no longer needed.
</Warning>

## The Live Interviews Page

[**Live Interviews**](https://equip.co/recruiter/live-interviews/), under **ATS** in the sidebar, lists interviews across all your job openings.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Scheduled** | The interview is booked and the slot has not ended |
| **Awaiting feedback** | The slot has ended and panellists still need to submit |
| **Completed** | The panel's feedback is in, or a recruiter closed the round |
| **Cancelled** | The interview was cancelled, or the candidate did not attend |
