---
title: "Candidate Says They Submitted, But I Can't See It"
description: "Steps to diagnose an assessment or AI interview submission that isn't showing up in your results."
---

<Tip>
tl;dr: For an assessment, the candidate most likely finished only the **demo test**, or did not finish **all the tests**. Ask them to click their assessment link again: if they truly finished, they see an "Assessment Completed" screen; if not, the assessment resumes and they can complete it. For an AI interview, check the results page, where unsubmitted attempts are listed as **Unsubmitted**.
</Tip>

## What to Check First

Ask the candidate to click their **assessment link** again and record a short video of what they see.

| What the candidate sees | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Assessment Completed** screen | They genuinely finished the assessment | Share the video, their email ID, and the assessment number with Equip support |
| The assessment resumes | They had not finished it | Ask them to complete the remaining tests now |

Meanwhile, if your assessment has multiple tests, change the **Results page** filters to include **Unfinished attempts** and look for the candidate there.

{/* screenshot: Results page with the Unfinished attempts callout and filter */}

## Why the Submission Is Missing

### They only finished the demo test

A candidate who skims the instructions may complete a **demo test** and assume they finished the real assessment, despite the "Not Actual Assessment - Just Demo Tests" banner.

{/* screenshot: candidate demo tests page with the "Not Actual Assessment - Just Demo Tests" banner */}

### They did not finish every test

In a multi-test assessment, a candidate who completed only some tests sits in the **Unfinished** state.

<Note>
Demo tests and multiple tests apply only to assessments, not AI interviews.
</Note>

## If It Was an AI Interview

Ask the candidate to open their **AI interview link** again, and check the interview's **AI Interview Results** page from the [**AI Interviews** dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/). Its **Status** filter separates Finished from Not Finished.

| Where | What you see | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Candidate's link | **AI Interview Completed** with "You may contact your recruiter for the results." | They finished the interview |
| Results page, score column | **Unsubmitted** (Candidate hasn't finished) | They started but did not submit |
| Results page, score column | **Submitted** (Processing not complete) | They submitted; grading is still running |

<Warning>
A **conversational interview** that was started but not submitted cannot be resumed by default. Reopening the link shows the same **AI Interview Completed** page, with "Conversational AI interviews cannot be resumed once started." Turn on **Allow resuming unfinished attempt** in the interview's settings to let candidates restart from scratch until they submit.
</Warning>

## Re-inviting the Candidate

It is always safe to invite the candidate again: an attempt is tied to their email ID, so a fresh invitation never creates a duplicate submission.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send a fresh invitation">
    Invite the candidate from the assessment as usual. One credit is deducted for the new invitation.

    If they had finished, they see the completed screen. If they had not, any of their links resumes the original attempt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reclaim the unused credit">
    The new link goes unused, so you can reclaim its credit 24 hours after the invitation. Verify the deduction on your [**Credit Usage**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/credit-usage/) page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Contacting Support

If none of the above resolves it, escalate to Equip via chat or **hello@equip.co**.

<Warning>
Support cannot verify anything without the candidate's **email ID**, the **assessment number**, and the candidate's **video or screenshot** of the "Assessment Completed" screen. Collect these before reaching out.
</Warning>
