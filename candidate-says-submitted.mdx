<Tip>
tl;dr: The candidate most likely finished only the **demo test**, or did not finish **all the tests** in the assessment. Ask them to click their assessment link again: if they truly finished, they will see an "Assessment Completed" screen; if not, the assessment resumes and they can complete it.
</Tip>

## What to Check First

Ask the candidate to click their **assessment link** again and record a short video of what they see. There are only two possible outcomes:

| What the candidate sees | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Assessment Completed** screen | They genuinely finished the assessment | Share the video, their email ID, and the assessment number with Equip support |
| The assessment resumes | They had not finished it | Ask them to complete the remaining tests now |

<Note>
When a candidate finishes an assessment, the final screen clearly tells them they may close the tab. A screenshot or video of that screen is proof of completion.
</Note>

While the candidate does this, check your own dashboard. If your assessment has multiple tests, the **Results page** shows a callout when there are **Unfinished attempts**: change the filters to include unfinished attempts and look for the candidate there.

## Why the Submission Is Missing

Assuming the candidate is telling the truth, there are two common explanations.

### They only finished the demo test

Before the actual assessment, Equip encourages candidates to attempt short **demo tests** to get familiar with the interface and confirm proctoring works on their device. A candidate who skims the instructions may complete a demo test and assume they finished the real assessment, even though the demo page carries a "Not Actual Assessment - Just Demo Tests" banner.

{/* screenshot: candidate demo tests page with the "Not Actual Assessment - Just Demo Tests" banner */}

### They did not finish every test

If your assessment contains multiple tests, the candidate may have completed some but not all of them. Their attempt then sits in the **Unfinished** state instead of appearing alongside your completed results.

<Note>
Could something be wrong at Equip's end? In principle yes, but this question comes up a few times a month, and since 2022 it has never been an issue on Equip's side. If the results system failed, it would fail for every candidate, not selectively.
</Note>

## Re-inviting the Candidate

It is always safe to invite the candidate again, whatever their actual state.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send a fresh invitation">
    Invite the candidate from the assessment as usual. One credit is deducted for the new invitation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the candidate click through">
    If they had finished, they simply see the completed screen. If they had not, clicking any of their links resumes the original attempt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reclaim the unused credit">
    Because the new link never gets used, you can reclaim its credit once 24 hours have passed since the invitation. Verify the deduction on your **Credit Usage** page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
An attempt is always tied to the candidate's email ID, so a fresh invitation never risks a duplicate submission: any of their links resumes the same original attempt.
</Tip>

## Contacting Support

If none of the above resolves it, escalate to Equip via chat or **hello@equip.co**. Include all three of the following, since support cannot investigate without them:

- The candidate's **email ID**
- The **assessment number**
- The candidate's **video or screenshot** of the "Assessment Completed" screen

<Warning>
Without the video or screenshot, the candidate's email ID, and the assessment number, there is nothing support can verify. Collect these from the candidate before reaching out.
</Warning>
