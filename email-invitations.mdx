description: "Invite candidates by email with unique one-time links, editable templates, and delivery tracking."
---

Email invitations are the most controlled way to share an assessment or AI interview. Each candidate receives a **unique, one-time link**, so only the people you invite can attempt, and you can track every email's delivery status. To compare this with sharing a common public link, see [Share URL vs Email Invitations](/share-url-vs-email).

## Sending Invitations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation page">
    On your dashboard, click **Invite** under the assessment. AI interviews have the same **Invite** link on the AI Interviews dashboard.
    {/* screenshot: Invite button under an assessment on the recruiter dashboard */}

    <Frame caption="Each assessment on the dashboard has its own Invite link">

    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add recipients">
    Paste email addresses, or click **Upload CSV** for longer lists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    Check the email, then click send and confirm in the **Invite Candidates** dialog.
    {/* screenshot: Send Invitations tab with recipients filled in and the confirmation dialog */}

    <Frame caption="The Invite Candidates dialog states the credits deducted now and the cost per submission">
      <img src="/images/email-invitations/invite-confirmation.png" width="532" alt="Invite Candidates dialog asking to confirm emailing three candidates, stating that 3 credits are deducted now and that each submission costs 2 credits in total, with No and Yes buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can send to a maximum of **100 recipients per send**.

<Note>
If the assessment or interview is linked to a job opening, the **Invite** button is disabled. Move the candidate into the linked stage instead and the invitation goes out automatically. See [Changing Stages](/changing-stages).
</Note>

Follow each email in the **Track Invitations** tab. See [Email Status](/email-status) for what each status means.

## Invitation Templates

You can choose the **Sender**, whose email becomes the reply-to address, and edit the subject and body in any language. Body edits are saved per assessment or AI interview. Set **Link Expiry (days)** if links should expire; leave it empty and they never do.

<Warning>
Do not delete the assessment link from the email body. If you do, Equip blocks sending with a **No Link Found** dialog, and restoring it with **Reset to default** loses your current edits to the body.
</Warning>

Emails are always sent from **no-reply@equip.co**. See [Custom Sender Email](/custom-sender-email).

<Note>
Forwarding a link to someone else does not work, because the verification code still goes to the invited email address. See [Invitation Links](/invitation-links).
</Note>

## Credit Costs

Email invitations deduct credits at send time, not when the candidate starts.

### Assessments

| Event | Credits |
|---|---|
| Sending an invitation | 1 credit per candidate, deducted immediately |
| Candidate takes each test in an assessment | 1 credit per test, with the invitation credit covering the first test |
| Re-inviting the same candidate | Another 1 credit, reclaimable after 24 hours if the extra link goes unused |

### AI interviews

Each invited candidate costs the interview's rate below, with no extra charge when they start.

| Interview type | Credits per candidate |
|---|---|
| **One-way Interview** | 1 credit per 20 questions, rounded up (maximum 40 questions, so 1 or 2 credits) |
| **Quick Interview** | 2 credits |
| **Comprehensive Interview** | 3 credits |

<Note>
For both products, if a candidate never starts, you can reclaim the credits once the invitation is more than 24 hours old. Reclaiming also revokes their link and deletes any test data. See [Reclaim Credits](/reclaim-credits).
</Note>

Sending is blocked with an **Insufficient Credits** dialog unless your balance covers every invitation.

<Warning>
With a negative balance, results are hidden until you top up, and below minus 25 credits candidates cannot start their attempts. Keep enough balance for everyone you invite.
</Warning>
