---
title: "Sending Email Invitations"
description: "Invite candidates by email with unique one-time links, editable templates, and delivery tracking."
---

Email invitations are the most controlled way to share an assessment or AI interview. Each candidate receives a **unique, one-time link**, so only the people you invite can attempt, and you can track every email's delivery status. To compare this with sharing a common public link, see [Share URL vs Email Invitations](/share-url-vs-email).

## Sending Invitations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation page">
    On your dashboard, click **Invite** under the assessment.
    {/* screenshot: Invite button under an assessment on the recruiter dashboard */}

    <Frame caption="Each assessment on the dashboard has its own Invite link">
      <img src="/images/email-invitations/invite-link-on-dashboard.png" width="424" alt="Name column of the Assessments dashboard listing three assessments, each with its owner's initials beside the name and Invite, Preview and Settings links under it" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add recipients">
    Type or paste email addresses separated by commas or new lines. For longer lists, click **Upload CSV** and use the **Download sample CSV** template to format your file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    Check the sender, subject, and email body, and set a link expiry if you want one. Then click send and confirm in the **Invite Candidates** dialog.
    {/* screenshot: Send Invitations tab with recipients filled in and the confirmation dialog */}

    <Frame caption="The Invite Candidates dialog states the credits deducted now and the cost per submission">
      <img src="/images/email-invitations/invite-confirmation.png" width="532" alt="Invite Candidates dialog asking to confirm emailing three candidates, stating that 3 credits are deducted now and that each submission costs 2 credits in total, with No and Yes buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can send to a maximum of **100 recipients per send**.

<Note>
If the assessment or interview is linked to a particular job opening, the **Invite** button is disabled here instead. Go to that job opening and change the candidate's stage; the invitation email goes out automatically when they move into the linked stage. See [Changing Stages](/changing-stages).
</Note>

After sending, follow each email in the **Track Invitations** tab. See [Email Status](/email-status) for what each status means.

## Invitation Templates

The **Send Invitations** tab includes these fields:

| Field | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Sender** | Dropdown of your team members' emails. The chosen member's email becomes the reply-to address. |
| **Link Expiry (days)** | How long invitation links stay valid, from 1 to 365 days. Leave it empty and links never expire. |
| **Email Subject** | The subject line. Fully editable. |
| **Email Template** | Rich-text email body, saved per assessment so your edits persist for future sends. |

You can write the email in **any language** your candidates prefer.

<Warning>
The email body must contain the assessment link, and you should not edit the link itself while customizing the text around it. If you delete it, Equip blocks sending with a **No Link Found** dialog and asks you to restore the link using the **Reset to default** button under the email template. Your current edits to the body are lost.
</Warning>

Emails are always sent from **no-reply@equip.co**; this address cannot be changed. See [Custom Sender Email](/custom-sender-email) for details.

<Note>
Forwarding a link to someone else does not work, because the verification code still goes to the invited email address. See [Invitation Links](/invitation-links).
</Note>

## Credit Costs

Email invitations deduct credits at send time, not when the candidate starts:

| Event | Credits |
|---|---|
| Sending an invitation | 1 credit per candidate, deducted immediately |
| Candidate takes each test in an assessment | 1 credit per test, with the invitation credit covering the first test |
| Re-inviting the same candidate | Another 1 credit, reclaimable after 24 hours if the extra link goes unused |

<Note>
If a candidate never starts, you can reclaim the credit once the invitation is more than 24 hours old. Reclaiming also revokes their link and deletes any test data. See [Reclaim Credits](/reclaim-credits).
</Note>

Your account can go up to 25 credits negative, so the number of open invitations is capped at your balance plus 25.

<Warning>
With a negative balance, results are hidden until you top up, and below minus 25 credits candidates cannot start their attempts. Keep enough balance for everyone you invite.
</Warning>
