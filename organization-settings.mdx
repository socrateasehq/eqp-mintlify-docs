Organization settings live on the [**Team** page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/). In the sidebar, go to **Account > Team** and open the [**Organization** tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/organization/).

<Note>
Fields on the Organization tab **save automatically** as you edit them. There is no Save button, so your changes take effect right away.
</Note>

These settings apply to your whole team. Personal details like your name, phone number, and profile photo live separately under [**Account > Profile**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/profile/); your login email cannot be changed.

## Brand and Legal Names

Equip keeps your public-facing name and your legal name as two separate fields, because they appear in different places:

| Field | Where it appears | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand Name** | Shown to candidates on test instructions and emails | Required, up to 80 characters |
| **Legal Entity Name** | Appears on your invoices | Required, up to 100 characters |

Use your **Brand Name** for the name candidates recognize, even if it differs from your registered company name. Your **Legal Entity Name** should match your official records so invoices are valid for accounting and tax purposes.

<Warning>
Update the **Legal Entity Name** before you purchase credits. Invoices that have already been issued cannot be edited in the product; see [Change Billing Information](/change-billing-information) if you need one re-issued.
</Warning>

## Timezone and Defaults

The Organization tab also holds these team-wide defaults:

| Setting | What it controls | Options |
|---|---|---|
| **Timezone** | The timezone used for email invitations | Standard IANA timezones shown with their UTC offsets |
| **Size** | Your organization's headcount range | 1 - 10, 11 - 50, 51 - 200, 201 - 1000, 1000+ |
| **Industry** | The default industry for new job openings | Select from Equip's industry list |
| **Default Dashboard** | The dashboard you land on after signing in | Job Openings, Assessments, AI Interviews |
| **Allow Auto-join** | Whether colleagues with your email domain can join without an invite | Yes, No; see [Joining via Email](/manage-team-members#joining-via-email) |

<Tip>
If your team spends most of its time in one part of Equip, switch the **Default Dashboard** to match. For example, choose Assessments if you mainly run skill tests, and you will skip a click on every sign-in.
</Tip>

## Organization Logo

Your **Organization Logo** is shown to candidates who apply, so uploading one makes your job posts and candidate-facing pages look professional and on-brand.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team page">
    In the sidebar, go to [**Account > Team**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/) and open the [**Organization** tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/organization/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    Under **Organization Logo**, choose an image file from your computer. The change saves automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The file must be **500 KB or smaller**, in **JPEG, PNG, WebP, or SVG** format.

<Tip>
An SVG file stays sharp at any size and usually has the smallest file size, so it is the best choice if you have one.
</Tip>
