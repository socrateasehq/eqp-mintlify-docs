Company details live on the [**Company** tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/organization/company/) of **Account > Organization**, and organization-wide defaults on the [**Hiring rules** tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/organization/hiring-rules/). Fields on both tabs **save automatically**.

## Company Details

| Field | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| **Brand name** | Candidate instructions and emails |
| **Legal entity name** | Your invoices |
| **Timezone** | Candidate emails |
| **Logo** | Shown to candidates who apply. Up to 500 KB, in JPEG, PNG, WebP or SVG. |

<Warning>
Update the **Legal entity name** before you pay. Invoices that have already been issued cannot be edited in the product; see [Change Billing Information](/change-billing-information) if you need one re-issued.
</Warning>

## Other Settings

| Setting | Tab | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Allow auto-join** | Company | Lets colleagues on your email domain [join without an invite](/manage-team-members#joining-without-an-invite) |
| **Email domains** | Company | The domains members and invites must use. To change them, write to hello@equip.co. |
| **Industry** | Hiring rules | The default industry for new job openings |
| **Default dashboard** | Hiring rules | Where everyone lands after signing in: Job Openings, Assessments or AI Interviews |

The Hiring rules tab also holds the rules for [duplicate applications](/duplicate-applications).
