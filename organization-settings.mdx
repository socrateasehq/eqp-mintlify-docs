---
title: "Organization Settings"
description: "Manage your brand name, legal entity name, team size, timezone, default dashboard, and organization logo."
---

Organization settings live on the [**Team** page](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/). In the sidebar, go to **Account > Team** and open the [**Organization** tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/organization/).

<Note>
Fields on the Organization tab **save automatically**. There is no Save button.
</Note>

These settings apply to your whole team. Personal details live under [**Account > Profile**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/profile/); your login email cannot be changed.

{/* screenshot: Team page with the Organization section visible */}

## Brand and Legal Names

Your public-facing name and your legal name are two separate fields:

| Field | Where it appears | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand Name** | Shown to candidates on test instructions and emails | Required, up to 80 characters |
| **Legal Entity Name** | Appears on your invoices | Required, up to 100 characters |

<Warning>
Update the **Legal Entity Name** before you purchase credits. Invoices that have already been issued cannot be edited in the product; see [Change Billing Information](/change-billing-information) if you need one re-issued.
</Warning>

## Timezone and Defaults

The Organization tab also holds these team-wide defaults:

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Timezone** | The timezone used for email invitations |
| **Size** | Your organization's headcount range |
| **Industry** | The default industry for new job openings |
| **Default Dashboard** | The dashboard you land on after signing in: Job Openings, Assessments, or AI Interviews |
| **Allow Auto-join** | Whether colleagues with your email domain can join without an invite; see [Joining via Email](/manage-team-members#joining-via-email) |

## Organization Logo

Your **Organization Logo** is shown to candidates who apply.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team page">
    Go to [**Account > Team**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/) and open the [**Organization** tab](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/team/organization/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    Under **Organization Logo**, choose an image file. It must be **500 KB or smaller**, in JPEG, PNG, WebP, or SVG format.
  </Step>
</Steps>
