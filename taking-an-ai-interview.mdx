Equip offers two kinds of AI interview. A **One-Way Interview** shows you a fixed set of questions that you answer by video, audio, or text. A **Conversational Interview** is a live, spoken conversation with the **Equip AI Interviewer**, which asks follow-up questions based on your answers.

<Note>
You reach the interview through a link your recruiter shares, either in an invitation email or as a public URL. Sign in with the 6-digit code sent to your email, which expires after 10 minutes. See [Candidate Login Methods](/candidate-login-methods).
</Note>

## Equipment Checks

Before the interview begins, Equip runs a short **three-step wizard** to make sure your camera and microphone work. You cannot start until both are verified.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the format">
    The first screen explains the format and lists the active proctoring measures. You consent to proctoring before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test your devices">
    Grant camera and microphone access when your browser asks. Equip records a **5-second test clip** and plays it back. Conversational interviews add a **read-aloud microphone calibration**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the interview">
    If your recruiter set an availability window, you can only start within that window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use **Google Chrome** on a laptop or desktop for the smoothest experience. On an iPhone or iPad, use **Safari**. See [Device Compatibility](/device-compatibility).
</Tip>

## During the Interview

| | One-Way Interview | Conversational Interview |
|---|---|---|
| **Format** | Fixed questions, no follow-ups | Live conversation with adaptive follow-ups |
| **How you answer** | Video, audio, or text, as set by the recruiter | Speaking, plus optional written or coded answers |
| **Question order** | All questions visible at once; answer in any order | The AI Interviewer leads the conversation |
| **Sessions** | Resumable if interrupted | One live session |

### One-Way Interviews

For recorded answers, the screen shows how many **retakes** you have left and enforces any minimum or maximum answer duration.

Recorded answers upload in the background. After you submit, keep the tab open until the upload progress screen completes.

### Conversational Interviews

The interview is a **live video call** with a real-time transcript, conducted in the language your recruiter chose.

Some questions may open a **Workspace** panel beside the call, where you write code or type a plain-text answer.

<Note>
AI interviews use the same proctoring options as Equip assessments, and the session is recorded on Equip's servers with the transcript retained. See [AI Proctoring](/ai-proctoring) for what gets tracked.
</Note>

## Reconnection and Grace Windows

Brief network drops do not end your interview: Equip **reconnects automatically**. A **grace window** covers short disconnects, so if a status banner appears, stay on the page and let the connection recover.

If you leave before submitting, what happens next depends on a recruiter setting. When the recruiter allows **resuming unfinished attempts**, you can restart as many times as needed until you submit, and only the submitted attempt is graded.

<Warning>
A conversational interview **cannot be resumed after it is submitted**. If restarting is not enabled and you cannot get back in, contact your recruiter rather than trying repeatedly.
</Warning>

## Submitting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your answers">
    In a one-way interview, check that each answer shows as uploaded. In a conversational interview, the AI wraps up the conversation when the topics are covered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the interview">
    Submission is final for conversational interviews, so only submit when you are done. Once the completion screen confirms your interview was received, you can close the tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Results are shared by the recruiter, not by Equip. You will not see a score or report on screen.
</Note>
