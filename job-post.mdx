When you create a job opening on Equip, a three-step wizard walks you through **Job Post** (details candidates see), **Application Form** (fields candidates fill out), and **Candidate Pipeline** (stages and automations). This article covers the first step: the job post itself.

<Tip>
The fastest way to fill in a job post is to upload an existing job description PDF or paste its text. Equip's AI parses it and pre-fills every field, usually in 30-45 seconds. See [AI Job-Description Import](#ai-job-description-import) below.
</Tip>

## Job Details

The job details section captures the structured facts about the role. Candidates see these on the public job post, and Equip uses them to power filters on your [careers page](/careers-page) and the [Job Fit Score](/job-fit-score).

{/* screenshot: job creation wizard, step 1 (Job Post) with the job details form */}

You can specify:

- **Job title / role** and minimum and maximum **years of experience**
- **Employment type**, **seniority level**, and **work arrangement**
- **Locations** (cities), location restrictions, and an optional timezone restriction (a zone plus an overlap window in hours)
- A **hybrid policy** description for hybrid roles
- **Mandatory and preferred skills**
- **Salary range** with currency and frequency, and a **minimum education qualification**
- **Employment start date** and **application deadline**
- A **key callout**, a short highlight line that stands out on the post

The dropdown fields offer these options:

| Field | Options |
|---|---|
| **Employment type** | Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Internship, Other |
| **Seniority level** | Intern, Entry-level, Junior, Mid-level, Senior, Lead, Manager, Director |
| **Work arrangement** | On-site, Remote, Hybrid |
| **Salary frequency** | Yearly, Monthly, Weekly, Hourly, Fixed, Other |
| **Minimum education** | High School, Diploma, Bachelor's, Master's, PhD |

<Note>
You can edit any of these details after the job opening is created. The opening's Settings page has the same three tabs as the creation wizard: Job Post, Application Form, and Candidate Pipeline.
</Note>

## AI Job-Description Import

If you already have a job description, you do not need to retype it. Upload it and let Equip's AI fill in the form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload or paste your JD">
    On the Job Post step, use **Upload Job Description PDF** to drag and drop a PDF or click **Choose PDF File** to browse. PDF is the only supported file format, with a 10 MB size limit. You can also paste the job description as plain text instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for parsing">
    Equip shows "Uploading file..." and then "Processing your PDF...". Parsing usually takes 30-45 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the pre-filled fields">
    The AI pre-fills all job fields, including the rich-text sections. Review each field, correct anything the parser missed, and continue to the next step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If parsing does not complete within about a minute, Equip shows "Unable to Parse PDF" and switches to manual entry. Nothing is lost; you simply fill in the fields yourself.
</Note>

<Tip>
Starting from scratch? Equip provides a **sample job description** for a Data Analyst role that you can load as a starting point and adapt.
</Tip>

## Rich-Text Sections

Beyond the structured fields, the job post has four **rich-text sections** where you describe the role in your own words:

| Section | What to put in it |
|---|---|
| **Job Summary** | A concise overview of the role and why it matters |
| **Responsibilities** | What the person will actually do day to day |
| **Perks** | Benefits, culture, and anything that makes the role attractive |
| **Additional Requirements** | Anything not covered by the structured fields, such as certifications or travel expectations |

Keep these sections candidate-focused and scannable. The structured job details already cover experience, skills, salary, and location, so use the rich-text sections for context rather than repeating those facts.

## Field Limits

Equip validates the job post before it can be published. These are the limits to keep in mind:

| Field | Limit |
|---|---|
| **Job Summary** | 50 to 2,000 characters |
| **Years of experience** | Maximum 50 years; the gap between minimum and maximum can be at most 20 years |
| **Salary range** | Maximum salary can be at most 3x the minimum salary |
| **Skills** | Up to 10 mandatory skills, 15 skills in total |
| **Hybrid policy** | 10 to 300 characters |
| **Location restriction** | Minimum 3 characters |
| **Timezone restriction** | 1 to 12 hours of overlap |
| **Application deadline** | Up to 6 months in the future |
| **Key callout** | Maximum 200 characters and 30 words |

<Warning>
The application deadline cannot be set more than 6 months out. If your role stays open longer, extend the deadline from the opening's Settings page before it passes.
</Warning>

## Job Post Status

Every job post has a **status** that controls whether candidates can see it and apply. You change it from a dropdown on the Job Openings dashboard.

{/* screenshot: Job Openings dashboard row with the status dropdown open */}

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Published** | Visible to candidates and accepting applications |
| **Hidden** | Not visible to candidates; the public link returns a 404 page |
| **Archived** | No longer accepting applications; the post may still be visible for reference |

<Note>
Only **Published** job posts appear on your [careers page](/careers-page). Hiding or archiving a post removes it from the public listing without deleting any of its applications.
</Note>

Once the job post is complete, continue to the [Application Form](/application-form) step to choose which fields candidates fill out.
