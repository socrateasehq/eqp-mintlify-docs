When you create a job opening on Equip, a three-step wizard walks you through **Job Post** (details candidates see), **Application Form** (fields candidates fill out), and **Candidate Pipeline** (stages and automations).

<Tip>
Have a job description PDF? Upload it and Equip's AI pre-fills every field. See [AI Job-Description Import](#ai-job-description-import) below.
</Tip>

## Job Post Details

The structured fields power the filters on your [careers page](/careers-page) and the [Job Fit Score](/job-fit-score). The free-text boxes describe the role in your own words.

{/* screenshot: job creation wizard, step 1 (Job Post) with the job details form */}

### Important Fields

- **Job title** and an optional **role** (what the industry calls it)
- **Seniority level** and **employment type**
- Minimum and maximum **years of experience**

### Job Details

- **Salary range** with currency and frequency
- **Work arrangement**, with **locations**, location restrictions, and an optional timezone restriction
- A **hybrid policy** description for hybrid roles
- **Job Summary**, **Responsibilities**, and **Perks & Benefits**, see [Rich-Text Sections](#rich-text-sections) below

### Candidate Requirements

- **Mandatory and preferred skills**
- **Minimum education qualification** and **course or specialization**
- **Additional Requirements**, see [Rich-Text Sections](#rich-text-sections) below

### Additional Info

- **Status**, **employment start date**, and **application deadline**
- A **key callout**, a short highlight line that stands out on the post
- An optional **Google Maps URL** for the office location

<Note>
You can edit any of these details later from the opening's Settings page.
</Note>

## AI Job-Description Import

Upload an existing job description and let Equip's AI fill in the form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your JD">
    From the **Job Opening Dashboard**, click **+ Job Opening** and upload your PDF. PDF is the only supported file format, with a 10 MB size limit. Parsing usually takes 30-45 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the pre-filled fields">
    Correct anything the parser missed, then continue to the next step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If parsing does not complete within about a minute, Equip shows "Unable to Parse PDF" and switches to manual entry.
</Note>

<Tip>
Starting from scratch? Load the **sample job description** for a Data Analyst role and adapt it.
</Tip>

## Rich-Text Sections

The job post has four **rich-text sections**. Use them for context rather than repeating the structured fields.

| Section | What to put in it |
|---|---|
| **Job Summary** | A concise overview of the role and why it matters |
| **Responsibilities** | What the person will actually do day to day |
| **Perks** | Benefits, culture, and anything that makes the role attractive |
| **Additional Requirements** | Anything not covered by the structured fields, such as certifications or travel expectations |

{/* screenshot: rich-text editor showing the Job Summary section */}

## Field Limits

Equip validates the job post against these limits before it can be published.

| Field | Limit |
|---|---|
| **Job Summary** | 50 to 2,000 characters |
| **Years of experience** | Maximum 50 years; a gap wider than 20 years between minimum and maximum shows a warning, but is still allowed |
| **Salary range** | A maximum more than 3x the minimum shows a warning, but is still allowed |
| **Skills** | Up to 10 mandatory skills, 15 skills in total |
| **Hybrid policy** | 10 to 300 characters |
| **Location restriction** | Minimum 3 characters |
| **Timezone restriction** | 1 to 12 hours of overlap |
| **Application deadline** | A date more than 6 months out shows a warning, but is still allowed |
| **Key callout** | Maximum 200 characters |

## Job Post Status

Every job post has a **status** (Published, Hidden, or Archived) that controls whether candidates can see it and apply. Change it from the dropdown on the [Job Openings dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/job-openings/dashboard/). See [Sharing Job Posts](/sharing-job-posts) for what candidates see in each status.

{/* screenshot: Job Openings dashboard row with the status dropdown open */}

<Note>
Only **Published** job posts appear on your [careers page](/careers-page). Hiding or archiving a post removes it from the public listing without deleting any of its applications.
</Note>

Next, continue to the [Application Form](/application-form) step.
