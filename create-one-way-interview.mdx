A **One-Way AI Interview** asks every candidate the same fixed set of questions. Candidates answer with video, audio, or text at a time that suits them, and Equip's AI grades each response. Each candidate submission costs **1 credit** for up to 20 questions, or **2 credits** for 21 to 40 questions (\$1 to \$2).

## Building from Templates

One-way interviews are built from **reusable templates**. A template holds your questions, answer formats, and grading rules, so you can attach the same question set to multiple job openings.

<Note>
You need at least one one-way template before you can create an interview. If your team has none, Equip sends you to the templates page to create one first.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the AI Interviews dashboard">
    Go to [**AI Interviews**](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/), click **Add AI Interview**, and pick **One-Way Interview**.
    {/* screenshot: AI Interviews dashboard with the Add AI Interview button and the two interview type choices */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a template and a job opening">
    Choose a one-way template, pick an existing job opening or create a new one, and click **Create Interview**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Templates can be **cloned**, so the fastest way to build a variant for a new role is to clone a template and edit only the questions that differ.
</Tip>

## Question Setup

Questions live in the **template editor**. Click **Add Question** to add one, or [import questions from Excel](/import-questions-from-excel) if you already have them written elsewhere.

A one-way interview supports two question types:

1. **AI-graded**: the candidate answers in text, audio, or video, and Equip's AI evaluates the answer.
2. **MCQ**: the candidate selects the correct answer from your options.

{/* screenshot: one-way template editor showing a question with answer format, points, and evaluation prompt fields */}

For AI-graded questions, the **Answer Format** determines which response controls apply:

| Setting | Applies to | Range | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Points** | All formats | Per question | 10 |
| **Min / Max Words** | Text | 0 to 1,000 words | 50 / 250 |
| **Min / Max Seconds** | Audio, Video | 5 to 180 seconds | 30 / 180 |
| **Max Retakes** | Audio, Video | Up to 5 | 5 |

Each question also offers an optional **Evaluation Prompt** (up to 100 words) that tells the AI what to weigh when grading, and a choice of **response languages** candidates may answer in.

<Warning>
You cannot add more than 40 questions to an AI Interview, and the last remaining question cannot be deleted.
</Warning>

## Configure Interview Settings

After creation, Equip opens the interview's **Settings** page. Add candidate instructions, set a start window, and configure proctoring there before you invite candidates.

<Note>
The **Linked Template** is required. The interview cannot run without one.
</Note>

{/* screenshot: one-way interview settings page with job title, linked template, instructions, timer, and proctoring sections */}

See [Interview Settings](/interview-settings) for every option, including all proctoring toggles.

## Candidate Responses

Candidates see **all questions at once** and can answer them in any order, but the interview happens in a **single session**.

Once a candidate submits, the AI grades every answer, and the results appear in the candidate's [report](/interview-reports).

<Note>
Credits are deducted per candidate. If an invited candidate never starts the interview, you can [reclaim the credits](/reclaim-credits).
</Note>
