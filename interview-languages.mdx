Equip's AI interviews are not limited to English. A **conversational interview** can run entirely in the language you choose: the AI interviewer speaks, listens, and asks follow-ups in that language. **One-way interviews** work differently: you control which languages candidates may use in their responses, question by question.

## Supported Languages

For conversational interviews, the **Interview Language** dropdown offers 15 options:

| Group | Options |
|---|---|
| **English** | English (US), English (India), English (UK) |
| **Indian languages** | Hindi, Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali |
| **Other languages** | Spanish (Spain), Spanish (US), French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian |

<Tip>
Where a language has regional variants, pick the one closest to your candidate pool.
</Tip>

<Note>
Candidates do not pick a language themselves; the AI conducts the session in the language you chose.
</Note>

## Choosing a Language

<Warning>
Set the language before inviting candidates, and keep your topics and questions written in that same language. A mismatch between the interview language and the language of your questions can confuse both candidates and the AI.
</Warning>

### Conversational interviews

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the interview's settings">
    Go to the [**AI Interviews** dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/) and click **Settings** on the conversational interview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an Interview Language">
    Pick a language from the **Interview Language** dropdown. If no language is selected, the interview runs in **English (US)**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### One-way interviews

One-way interviews have two language settings: the language your **questions** are written in, and the language candidates may **respond** in.

#### Question Language (Multilingual Questions)

To offer the same question in multiple languages, enable **Multilingual Questions** on the template.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the template's settings">
    Go to [**AI Interviews**](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/), open [**One-Way Templates**](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/one-way-templates/), and click **Settings** on the template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Enable Multilingual Questions">
    In the **Alternative Languages** section, turn on **Enable Multilingual Questions**, then choose a default language and one or more additional languages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Enabling an additional language does not translate your questions automatically. You must write the question text in every language you enable, or candidates who pick that language see a blank or incomplete question.
</Warning>

#### Response Language

Each question also has its own **Response Language** selector, offering around 100 languages, which controls the language candidates may answer in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the template itself">
    Go to [**AI Interviews**](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/), open [**One-Way Templates**](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/one-way-templates/), and click the template, not its Settings, to open the **template editor**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Response Language per question">
    On each question, add up to 3 languages in the **Response Language** field. The default is **English**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The candidate's transcription is always in whichever language they spoke, but the AI evaluates their answer based on the response language you selected for that question.
</Note>

### How the settings compare

| | Conversational | One-way: Question Language | One-way: Response Language |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What it controls** | The language the AI interviewer speaks and understands | The language(s) your questions are written in | The language(s) candidates may use in their answers |
| **Where you set it** | Interview Language dropdown on the interview's settings page | Alternative Languages section in the template's settings | Response Language selector on each question in the template editor |
| **Scope** | One language for the entire interview | Per template, multiple languages allowed | Per question, up to 3 languages allowed |
| **Default** | English (US) | English | English |
