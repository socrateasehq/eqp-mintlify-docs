Equip's AI interviews are not limited to English. A **conversational interview** can run entirely in the language you choose: the AI interviewer speaks, listens, and asks follow-ups in that language. **One-way interviews** work differently: you control which languages candidates may use in their responses, question by question.

## Supported Languages

For conversational interviews, the **Interview Language** setting controls the entire session. The dropdown offers 15 options:

| Group | Options |
|---|---|
| **English** | English (US), English (India), English (UK) |
| **Indian languages** | Hindi, Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali |
| **Other languages** | Spanish (Spain), Spanish (US), French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian |

<Tip>
Where a language has regional variants, pick the one closest to your candidate pool. The AI's speech recognition and voice adapt to the variant you choose, so it matches how your candidates actually speak.
</Tip>

<Note>
The interview language is a recruiter setting. Candidates do not pick a language themselves in an interview; the AI conducts the session in the language you chose.
</Note>

## Choosing a Language

### Conversational interviews

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the interview's settings">
    Go to [**AI Interviews** dashboard](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/) from the sidebar and click **Settings** on the conversational interview you want to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Interview Language dropdown">
    On the settings page, locate the **Interview Language** dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a language">
    Choose from the 15 options listed above. The AI interviewer will speak, understand, and transcribe in that language for every candidate who takes this interview.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: Interview Language dropdown on the conversational interview settings page */}

<Note>
If no language is selected, the interview runs in **English (US)** by default.
</Note>

### One-way interviews

One-way interviews use pre-set questions with recorded answers, so there is no live AI voice to configure. Instead, language works at two separate levels: the language your **questions** are written in, and the language candidates may **respond** in.

#### Question Language (Multilingual Questions)

By default, a one-way template's questions are written in a single language. To offer the same question in multiple languages, enable **Multilingual Questions** on the template.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the template's settings">
    Go to [**AI Interviews**](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/) in the sidebar, open [**One-Way Templates**](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/one-way-templates/), and click **Settings** on the template you want to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to Alternative Languages">
    Scroll down to the **Alternative Languages** section and turn on **Enable Multilingual Questions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the default and additional languages">
    Choose a **Default Language**, then add one or more **Additional Languages**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: Alternative Languages section with Enable Multilingual Questions toggle, Default Language, and Additional Languages fields */}

<Warning>
Enabling an additional language does not translate your questions automatically. You must write the question text in every language you enable, or candidates who pick that language see a blank or incomplete question.
</Warning>

#### Response Language

Separate from the question's language, each question also has its own **Response Language** selector, offering around 100 languages, which controls the language candidates may answer in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the AI Interviews section">
    Go to [**AI Interviews**](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/dashboard/) in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open One-Way Templates">
    Open [**One-Way Templates**](https://equip.co/recruiter/ai-interviews/one-way-templates/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the template itself">
    Click the one-way interview template, not its Settings, to open the **template editor**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Response Language per question">
    On each question, use the **Response Language** field (Max 3) to add one or more languages candidates may answer in. The default is **English**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: per-question Response Language selector in the one-way template editor */}

<Note>
The candidate's transcription is always in whichever language they spoke, but the AI evaluates their answer based on the response language you selected for that question.
</Note>

### How the settings compare

| | Conversational | One-way: Question Language | One-way: Response Language |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What it controls** | The language the AI interviewer speaks and understands | The language(s) your questions are written in | The language(s) candidates may use in their answers |
| **Where you set it** | Interview Language dropdown on the interview's settings page | Alternative Languages section in the template's settings | Response Language selector on each question in the template editor |
| **Scope** | One language for the entire interview | Per template, multiple languages allowed | Per question, up to 3 languages allowed |
| **Default** | English (US) | English | English |

<Warning>
Set the language before inviting candidates, and keep your topics and questions written in that same language. A mismatch between the interview language and the language of your questions can confuse both candidates and the AI.
</Warning>
