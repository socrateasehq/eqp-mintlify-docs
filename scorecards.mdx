**Scorecards** are structured questionnaires your team fills in after evaluating a candidate. Instead of free-form impressions, every interviewer rates the same criteria, so candidates are compared fairly and hiring decisions are backed by data.

## Creating Scorecards

Scorecards are managed as reusable **templates**. Create a template once, then attach it to as many pipeline stages as you like.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Scorecards templates page">
    Go to **Account → Templates → Scorecards**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new template">
    Give the template a name, for example "Technical Interview Scorecard". Names can be up to 100 characters. Pick a layout and the stage types the template applies to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the criteria">
    New templates start with four default criteria. Add your own, remove ones you do not need, and use the up/down arrows to reorder them. At least one criterion must remain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the template">
    The template is now available in the scorecard dropdown when you configure supported pipeline stages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: scorecard template editor with criteria list */}

The default criteria gives you a sensible starting point:

| Criterion | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Communication** | Clarity and articulation |
| **Technical Skills** | Domain expertise and knowledge |
| **Problem Solving** | Analytical and critical thinking |
| **Cultural Fit** | Alignment with team values |

A template's **layout** is chosen once, when you create it:

| | Compact | Detailed |
|---|---|---|
| **Structure** | One list of criteria | Criteria grouped into weighted sections |
| **Limits** | Up to 10 criteria | Up to 5 sections, with up to 10 criteria each |
| **Feedback blocks** | No | Yes, such as an overall recommendation, strengths and concerns, and a level read |
| **Best for** | A phone screen or a single scorer | An interview panel |

In a Detailed scorecard, each section carries a weight, and the weights add up to 100. You can also choose whether the note under each criterion is required, optional, or hidden.

<Tip>
Create separate templates for different rounds, such as a phone-screen template with broad criteria and a technical-round template with deeper ones. Interviewers then rate only what their round is designed to assess.
</Tip>

## Attaching Scorecards to Stages

Scorecards attach to the pipeline stages where a human evaluates the candidate. Assessments and AI interviews are scored automatically, so they do not use scorecards.

| Stage type | Scorecard support |
|---|---|
| **Phone Screening** | Yes |
| **Live Interview** | Yes |
| **Custom** | Yes |
| Assessment, AI Interviews | No, these stages are auto-scored |
| Applied, Offered, terminal stages | No |

To attach a template, open the job's **Candidate Pipeline** settings, select a supported stage, and pick the template from the scorecard dropdown. See [Pipeline Stages](/pipeline-stages) for the full stage configuration options.

{/* screenshot: pipeline stage settings with scorecard dropdown */}

<Note>
The same template can be attached to stages across multiple job openings. Alongside the scorecard, each stage can also have its own email templates and an assignee.
</Note>

## Filling In a Scorecard

When a candidate reaches a stage with a scorecard attached, evaluators fill it in from the candidate's profile.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate">
    From the applications dashboard, open the candidate details panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rate each criterion">
    Give a star rating for every criterion on the scorecard. Equip computes an overall score from your ratings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    The submission is logged in the candidate's **Candidate Journey** timeline as a "submitted scorecard" event, attributed to the team member who filled it in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: scorecard star-rating form on a candidate profile */}

<Note>
Live Interview stages work differently. Each panellist fills in the scorecard on their own scoring page, and the scores combine into a single result. See [Interview Feedback and Results](/interview-feedback-and-results).
</Note>

<Warning>
Opening an existing scorecard and submitting it again always creates a new scorecard event. It does not overwrite the earlier submission, so treat each submission as a fresh evaluation.
</Warning>

## Scores in Analytics

Scorecard results do not stay siloed on individual profiles. They roll up in two places:

- **Candidate profile**: submitted scorecards appear on the **Candidate Details** tab alongside test scores, fit score, and other evaluation data, and every submission is logged in the Candidate Journey.
- **ATS Insights**: The **Avg scorecard by recruiter** chart in the Team section averages scorecard results per recruiter, so you can spot evaluators who rate consistently harsher or more leniently than the rest of the team.

<Tip>
Reviewing average scores by recruiter is a quick calibration check. If one interviewer's averages drift far from the team's, align on what each star rating should mean before your next round of interviews.
</Tip>
