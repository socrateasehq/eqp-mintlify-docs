**Scorecards** are structured questionnaires your team fills in after evaluating a candidate. Every interviewer rates the same criteria, so candidates are compared fairly.

## Creating Scorecards

Scorecards are managed as reusable **templates**. Create a template once, then attach it to as many pipeline stages as you like, across job openings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Scorecards templates page">
    Go to [**Account → Templates → Scorecards**](https://equip.co/recruiter/account/templates/scorecards/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new template">
    Give the template a name, then pick a layout and the stage types it applies to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the criteria and save">
    New templates start with four default criteria: Communication, Technical Skills, Problem Solving, and Cultural Fit. Add, remove, or reorder them, then save the template.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: scorecard template editor with criteria list */}

A template's **layout** is chosen once, when you create it:

| | Compact | Detailed |
|---|---|---|
| **Structure** | One list of criteria | Criteria grouped into weighted sections |
| **Limits** | Up to 10 criteria | Up to 5 sections, with up to 10 criteria each |
| **Feedback blocks** | No | Yes, such as an overall recommendation, strengths and concerns, and a level read |
| **Best for** | A phone screen or a single scorer | An interview panel |

In a Detailed scorecard, the section weights add up to 100. You can also choose whether the note under each criterion is required, optional, or hidden.

Create separate templates for different rounds, so interviewers rate only what their round is designed to assess.

## Attaching Scorecards to Stages

Scorecards attach to the pipeline stages where a human evaluates the candidate.

| Stage type | Scorecard support |
|---|---|
| **Phone Screening** | Yes |
| **Live Interview** | Yes |
| **Custom** | Yes |
| Assessment, AI Interviews | No, these stages are auto-scored |
| Applied, Offered, terminal stages | No |

To attach a template, open the job's **Candidate Pipeline** settings, select a supported stage, and pick the template from the scorecard dropdown. See [Pipeline Stages](/pipeline-stages) for the full stage configuration options.

## Filling In a Scorecard

When a candidate reaches a stage with a scorecard attached, evaluators fill it in from the candidate's profile.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate">
    From the applications dashboard, open the candidate details panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rate each criterion and submit">
    Give a star rating for every criterion. Equip computes an overall score from your ratings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* screenshot: scorecard star-rating form on a candidate profile */}

<Note>
Live Interview stages work differently. Each panellist fills in the scorecard on their own scoring page, and the scores combine into a single result. See [Interview Feedback and Results](/interview-feedback-and-results).
</Note>

<Warning>
Opening an existing scorecard and submitting it again always creates a new scorecard event. It does not overwrite the earlier submission, so treat each submission as a fresh evaluation.
</Warning>

## Scores in Analytics

Scorecard results appear in two places:

- **Candidate profile**: submitted scorecards appear on the **Candidate Details** tab, and every submission is logged in the Candidate Journey.
- **ATS Insights**: The **Avg scorecard by recruiter** chart in the Team section averages scorecard results per recruiter.

<Tip>
If one interviewer's averages drift far from the team's, align on what each star rating should mean before your next round of interviews.
</Tip>
